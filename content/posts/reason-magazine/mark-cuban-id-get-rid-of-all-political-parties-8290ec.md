Today's guest is [Mark Cuban](https://x.com/mcuban), the billionaire entrepreneur and former *[Shark Tank](https://www.sonypictures.com/tv/sharktank)* star who is now on a mission to reform the American healthcare system. Through his companies, *[Cost Plus Drugs](https://www.costplusdrugs.com/)* and *[Cost Plus Wellness](https://costpluswellness.com/)*, Cuban is trying to make healthcare more affordable by cutting out middlemen and making pricing more transparent. But he argues that fixing America's healthcare system will require breaking up massive conglomerates that wield enormous power over pricing and competition, often at the expense of patients.

Nick Gillespie talks with Cuban about why he supports [bipartisan legislation](https://www.warren.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/warren-hawley-introduce-bipartisan-bill-to-break-up-big-medicine/) from Elizabeth Warren and Josh Hawley to break up Big Medicine. Cuban explains how artificial intelligence can expose hidden costs in health insurance contracts and transform the way doctors diagnose and treat patients. They also discuss Cuban's support for [Texas Democrat James Talarico](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/texas-senate-james-talarico-mark-cuban-healthcare.html) and his belief that political parties have outlived their usefulness. The billionaire entrepreneur makes the case for restricting social media algorithms for children under 16 and discusses why he isn't particularly worried about socialism's growing popularity among young people. Finally, Cuban reflects on how Ayn Rand's *[The Fountainhead](https://aynrand.org/novels/the-fountainhead/)* shaped his understanding of capitalism and why he believes helping others is part of being a successful entrepreneur.
