O\_O is back! Every October, the Obsidian community turns “I should make that” into “I made that!” Whether you’ve been tinkering with a plugin, theme, vault, guide, or video, this is your month to finish it and share it.

This year the Obsidian team will also be working on our own project, a new survey we’re calling [State of Obsidian](https://2026.obsidian.md/).

### Take the State of Obsidian survey

In May, we [launched](https://obsidian.md/blog/future-of-plugins/) the new [Obsidian Community](https://community.obsidian.md/), and since then the number of plugins and themes has more than doubled to over 8,000 projects!

We thought this would be a great opportunity to reimagine our annual *Gems of the Year* awards and report on how you are using Obsidian in 2026. This first survey will also set a baseline for how the community continues to evolve going forward.

As you know, we take [privacy](https://obsidian.md/privacy) seriously. The Obsidian app doesn’t collect personal data, and our developer guidelines prohibit plugins from including telemetry. So the [State of Obsidian](https://2026.obsidian.md/) survey is how you can tell us about your favorite plugins, themes, tools, and ways to use Obsidian. We’ll compile this data into a report that we will share publicly by the end of the year.

[Take the State of Obsidian survey](https://2026.obsidian.md/)

### Give your projects some love

Do you have an existing plugin? Give it a tune-up with the APIs we’ve added since last October:

- The new [Settings API](https://docs.obsidian.md/plugins/guides/migrate-declarative-settings) in Obsidian 1.13 makes your settings searchable.
- If your plugin stores API keys, passwords, or other secrets, explore [SecretStorage and SecretComponent](https://docs.obsidian.md/plugins/guides/secret-storage) to let people use secrets they manage in the Obsidian Keychain.

Your project doesn’t have to be a plugin. Refresh a theme, share a vault, write a guide, or make a video that helps someone discover a new way to use Obsidian. If you’ve been waiting for a nudge to release it, this is it!

[Join us on Discord](https://discord.gg/obsidianmd) in the Obsidian October channel to share what you’re making as it takes shape, ask for help when you get stuck, and lend a hand on someone else’s project. Rock on! 🎸🪨
