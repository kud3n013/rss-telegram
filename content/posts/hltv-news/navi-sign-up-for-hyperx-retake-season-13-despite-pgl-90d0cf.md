[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) are among the teams signed up to compete at HyperX Retake Season 13, a 16-team LAN in Lisbon, Portugal, scheduled to run from October 29 - November 1 with $16,996 on offer.

The organization had until October 19 to pull out of the event, which overlaps with PGL Masters Bucharest (October 24-31) and ends less than a day before the start of IEM Beijing (November 2-8), but remained signed up after the deadline passed.

The scheduling conflict comes after [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) was put at risk of missing a Major for the first time in the organization's history after a dismal Fall season that saw the team crater below the top 20 on the VRS in September.

Two valuable LAN wins at ESL Pro League Season 24 provided a brief boost, but the team has since slipped to 30th on the VRS and NAVI's spot at the Major is [still not guaranteed](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/major) as teams below them fight to catch up.

They currently sit at a 96.6% chance of qualifying on HLTV's simulation page, but that number can be misleading: it takes into account that [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) will play two events before the invite deadline and assumes the team will win at least some of their matches, particularly those against lower-ranked teams at HyperX Retake.

However, without enough LAN wins, [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) would remain firmly out of the running for a Major spot, and a further spanner is thrown in the works as the team list for the LAN in Portugal remains incomplete.

That could mean [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) ends up in a GSL group with only three teams, leaving them devoid of a chance to grab one of the crucial LAN wins they need to lock in their invite.
