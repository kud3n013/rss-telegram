BLAST has revealed the groups and opening matchups for BLAST Rivals 2026 Season 2, set to be held in Chek Lap Kok, Hong Kong from November 11-15.

The eight attendees have been split into two groups of four, set to feed into a six-team playoff bracket.

Group A is headlined by an opening clash between [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) and [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) in the teams' fifth clash this year, with a Brazilian derby between [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) the second opener of the group.

![Brazil](https://www.hltv.org/img/static/flags/30x20/BR.gif) [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) vs. ![Brazil](https://www.hltv.org/img/static/flags/30x20/BR.gif) [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)

In Group B, [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) are set to face off against [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) in what will be the international squad's second event with [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus). The Spaniard's former team [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) have also been seeded into the second group, and will meet [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) in the first match.
