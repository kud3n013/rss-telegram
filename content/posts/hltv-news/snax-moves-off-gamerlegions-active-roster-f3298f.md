[Janusz "Snax" Pogorzelski](https://www.hltv.org/player/2553/snax) is no longer on the team's active roster, [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) have announced.

The benching comes with [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) all but [out of the race](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302#team-9928) to qualify for PGL Major Singapore.

The Pole was in his second stint at the organization, after captaining the side from September 2023 to July 2024 and then rejoining in December 2025.

Since [Snax](https://www.hltv.org/player/2553/snax) rejoined, the team made the semi-finals of BLAST Rivals and second place at IEM Atlanta. But results took a turn after an 0-3 exit from Stage 2 in Cologne and [Oldřich "PR" Nový](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr)'s sale to [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), and the team tumbled from ninth on the VRS in June to 42nd now.

Part of that was outside of their control: [Sebastian "Tauson" Tauson Lindelof](https://www.hltv.org/player/20301/tauson) missed the IEM Beijing qualifier due to health issues, [Snax](https://www.hltv.org/player/2553/snax) missed FPG 3 due to travel issues and [Fredrik "REZ" Sterner](https://www.hltv.org/player/9278/rez) was absent from Logitech G Play Connect due to food poisoning.

But either way, missing the Major has led the organization to act. [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) are next in action at the RELOG Tesla Cup, and must now put a new in-game leader in place for the event.
