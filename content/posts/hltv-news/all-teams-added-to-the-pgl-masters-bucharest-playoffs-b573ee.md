### Things to note

- The game was created with the first five playoff teams, meaning that the players might not be properly priced relative to each other. The prices didn't account for the bracket either.

### Player prices & pick stats

- [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) is the most-picked player in the game with over half the teams after his 1.35-rated group stage performance. With a $188,000 price tag and underdog opponent in the quarter-finals, the Spanish AWP makes a no-brainer pick.
- [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) is not far behind despite his usually heavy price tag of $240,000. He's likely to pay off if he's to reach the semis and automatically play out the entire three rounds with either grand final or decider, but whether it's a safe bet or not with Vitality facing NAVI in the quarter-final is up to you.
