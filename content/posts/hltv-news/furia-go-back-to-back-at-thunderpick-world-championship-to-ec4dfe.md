[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) have defeated [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) in a nail-biting 3-2 series, denying [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) a massive Mirage comeback before closing it out on the deciding Cache to secure the Thunderpick World Championship title in Malta.

With the win, the Brazilian-majority side has broken a nearly year-long title duck dating back to the BLAST Rivals Season 2 last November, doing so at the same tournament where they were once victorious already just a month before that.

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) got off to a flying start in the series on Dust2. It didn't matter much that [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) was getting the better of [Danil "molodoy" Golubenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/24144/molodoy) all throughout the game when the two faced off in AWP duels down Middle because [Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) continued his hot streak.

With a 21-8 scoreline, he powered his team to a 13-6 victory as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) won forcebuy wars to run away with the economy game early into both halves.

The Kazakh AWPer was back in his element early on Ancient and it looked as if [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) would run away with that map, too, but it all went wrong when, at 7-1, two teamkills within seconds from one another gave [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) a way back in.

The Russian side took the opportunity and ran away with it, leaving [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) scrambling as they lost the next eight rounds in a row. The game ultimately went to overtime after a late back-and-forth, and [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) completed the comeback in two sets of extra rounds.

[Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4)'s charges then took a convincing lead in the series on Nuke off the back of a superb display from [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez), who ensured their 7-2 start on the defense with two massive multi-kills under pressure on the A bombsite.

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) looked to reply in kind on Mirage and did just that to start off on the CT side, securing a 10-2 CT side after repeatedly taking the fight to [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) in Middle and pulling off crucial flanks when the rounds got tough.

But despite winning the second pistol round, too, closing the map proved difficult against a [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) side that was making the double AWP in [zorte](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) and [Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez)'s hands work wonders.

It came down to a nail-biting finish at 11-11, when [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) broke the nine-round run with a successful 5v5 post-plant on A, before [Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) ensured a 13-11 closing with a crucial triple in a 2v4 on the same bombsite.

It was all decided on Cache. [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)'s gameplan was clear: abuse the B bombsite with speed as much as possible. They did just that with success in the pistol and in a couple of crucial gunrounds to get ahead on the T side, with [FalleN](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen)'s 1v2 towards the end of the half then allowing the favorites to win out the half.

Chaos ensued at the beginning of the second half with forcebuy wars going back and forth with the title on the line, and it was [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) who ultimately nipped it in the bud with a perfectly-timed flank on a [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) team executing into the B bombsite with just moments to spare.
