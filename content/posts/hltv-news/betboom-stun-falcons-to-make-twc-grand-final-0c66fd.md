[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) reverse-swept [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) in the semi-finals of the Thunderpick World Championship (5-13 Anubis, 16-14 Dust2, 13-7 Mirage) to complete the upset of the tournament, bringing an end to [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons)' hopes of getting a trophy to lift morale after a rough Fall season.

It marks the first grand final appearance for [Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) at an MVP-eligible event since PGL Major Antwerp 2022, and the Russian in-game leader was all smiles after his team put away the tournament favorite in dominant fashion.

[Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons)' struggles since winning the Cologne Major looked to be turning around in Malta, particularly with [Finn "karrigan" Andersen](https://www.hltv.org/player/429/karrigan) showing better form, but errors plagued the team in the semi-final. [Maksim "kyousuke" Lukin](https://www.hltv.org/player/24177/kyousuke)'s level was also a glaring sore spot, with the Russian rifler ending the series at the bottom of the board with a 0.84 rating to cap off a miserable tournament, and [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) will now have to fight for bronze against [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora).

[Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) never looked troubled on Anubis, making up for a lost pistol and conversion with a methodical approach to their offense. An early clutch by [René "TeSeS" Madsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/12018/teses) gave them momentum to begin running away with the lead, and another 1v2 by [Nikola "NiKo" Kovač](https://www.hltv.org/player/3741/niko) kept them in firm control en route to an 8-4 half.

[karrigan](https://www.hltv.org/player/429/karrigan)'s uptick in form in Malta also continued as he found several impactful kills down the stretch, including a triple to crack open Middle, and [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) were never given the chance to recover as the Danish captain combined with [NiKo](https://www.hltv.org/player/3741/niko) and [Ilya "m0NESY" Osipov](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) to romp to a 13-5 victory.

[Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) carried that momentum onto Dust2 with a 5-1 start, but struggled to make a dent in [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom)'s defenses for a string of rounds as the gap closed to just two rounds by the break.

Individual errors plagued [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) after the swap, with overextensions and dry swings getting punished time and time again by [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) and [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte). [kyousuke](https://www.hltv.org/player/24177/kyousuke) gave away a few key early fights that allowed [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) to storm ahead, 12-9, but they couldn't get the job done in regulation as [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) recovered from a 1v3 clutch from [Daniil "d1Ledez" Kustov](https://www.hltv.org/player/20357/d1ledez) to force overtime.

That looked to be where [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) would come alive after a masterclass AWP ace from [m0NESY](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) was followed by a vintage [NiKo](https://www.hltv.org/player/3741/niko) triple to lead 14-13, but two triple kill rounds from [Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) helped [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) sweep the defensive half to clinch victory, 16-14.

[Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) continued to dominate on the Mirage decider, where [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) squandered a few prime chances to stabilize. Both pistol wins, and a 4-1 start to the map, served only to make the scoreline look respectable in what was otherwise a masterclass map from [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) and [Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez), who romped to a 13-7 victory off the back of a seven-round spree after winning the second-round force-buy in the second half.
