[Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) has gone back-to-back to earn his second HLTV x 1XBET MVP medal at Thunderpick World Championship 2026.

The Malta event was the venue for his maiden MVP in October 2025, and continues to be a happy hunting ground for [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) in his second visit.

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)'s star man had a huge lead in the MVP race before the final after averaging a 1.52 rating across the team's first three series and picking up six player of the map (POTM) awards in his first seven maps.

[KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) then opened the final with a 1.83-rated Dust2 to extend that lead even further and pick up a seventh POTM. He was also solid on Mirage, with a 1.85-rated first half as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) secured a 10-2 lead that they eventually converted 13-11.

So while he did have competition from an impressive [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez), [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) was ahead before the final and remained the clear candidate for the medal at its close.

The MVP award strengthens an already brilliant year for the Brazilian. [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) has picked up four EVPs so far in 2026, and has finished eight LAN events with a 1.20 rating or higher. But this one, a 1.34-rated Big Event MVP, is the best of the bunch.
