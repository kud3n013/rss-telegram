World No.7 [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) got their PGL Masters Bucharest campaign off to a disappointing start following a 0-2 defeat to [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga), who travelled to Romania on a massive wave of momentum after lifting the WINLINE MPKBK CIS LAN Season 7 trophy and placing second at Stake Ranked Episode 4.

The victory all but seals [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga)'s place at PGL Major Singapore as they move up three places to 13th in the Europe VRS. According to HLTV’s latest projections, their chances of qualifying for the Major now stand at 97%.

[Daniel "syph0" Ageyev](https://www.hltv.org/player/23319/syph0) was the hero in a chaotic Ancient game that went to quadruple overtime. The [Spirit Academy](https://www.hltv.org/team/7900/spirit-academy) graduate put in a 1.55 rating as [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) ran out 25-23 winners after wasting three map points in regulation.

Dust2 proved far less entertaining as [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) took control from the opening rounds. [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) still put up a fight while facing series point, but their comeback hopes ended in the 21st round, when [Nikita "robo" Dushin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22864/robo) — who, like [syph0](https://www.hltv.org/player/23319/syph0), is a [Spirit Academy](https://www.hltv.org/team/7900/spirit-academy) graduate — came close to a triple kill in a mid-B split.
