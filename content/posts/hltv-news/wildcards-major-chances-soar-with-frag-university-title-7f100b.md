[Wildcard](https://www.hltv.org/team/12257/wildcard) have won FRAG University 2026, taking down [NRG](https://www.hltv.org/team/6673/nrg) once and [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) twice on their way to the trophy and $13,750 in prize money.

The international squad showed stiff resolve on their run to the final, battling through overtime maps against [NRG](https://www.hltv.org/team/6673/nrg) and [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) en route to a BO5 bout with [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele), who made the final via the lower bracket.

[Wildcard](https://www.hltv.org/team/12257/wildcard) benefitted from a 1-0 advantage in the grand final as they came from the upper bracket, and that advantage proved vital as they contested a hard-fought game with [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele). The first three maps were one-sided for either team, [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) taking Mirage and Inferno whilst [Wildcard](https://www.hltv.org/team/12257/wildcard) bagged Nuke, sending the series to a decider on Anubis.

On Anubis strong T-side starts gave way to CT comebacks for both teams, [Nemanja "nEMANHA" Đukić](https://www.hltv.org/player/8311/nemanha) and [João "koala" Pfeffer](https://www.hltv.org/player/21170/koala) each standing head and shoulders above their teammates in terms of impact as the map went to OT. There [Wildcard](https://www.hltv.org/team/12257/wildcard) had the last laugh, posting a flawless three-round CT half to secure the title.

By winning the trophy and securing valuable wins over fellow Major chasers [NRG](https://www.hltv.org/team/6673/nrg) and [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele), [Wildcard](https://www.hltv.org/team/12257/wildcard) have seen their chances at securing a PGL Major Singapore rocket upwards, going from 33% from before the final day of play to 70% according to HLTV's projections. [NRG](https://www.hltv.org/team/6673/nrg) meanwhile exited the tournament in fourth place and saw their Major hopes take a dent, going from comfortable odds to a 61% chance.

[Wildcard](https://www.hltv.org/team/12257/wildcard)'s next confirmed event is [FiRe Last Chance](https://www.hltv.org/events/9421/fire-last-chance-2026), an event that will prove impactful in the race for Singapore qualification for both Europe and the Americas regions. [Wildcard](https://www.hltv.org/team/12257/wildcard), [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas), [100 Thieves](https://www.hltv.org/team/8474/100-thieves) and [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) are all slated to attend, and top-four finishes for any of them will prove a significant boon with the Major invite cutoff looming.
