## Thunderpick World Championship 2026

### Format

- You can create your teams on the Fantasy page [here](https://www.hltv.org/fantasy/664/gameredirect).
- Two four-team double-elimination groups.
- Four-team single-elimination playoffs with a third-place decider.
- All matches are BO3 except for BO5 grand final.

### Things to note

- Due to a presence of the third-place decider, teams making semi-finals will guarantee two rounds of play for themselves.
- Teams making playoffs via upper final will get one round of padding for skipping group stage decider matches.

### Player prices & pick stats

- [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) are the most popular team with [Finn "karrigan" Andersen](https://www.hltv.org/player/429/karrigan) being the most popular player in the game with 34% of all teams featuring the Dane. The Danish IGL is also the cheapest player in the game at $173k, but as usual, it’s worth noting that he averages a 0.77 rating in the past three months, and he was in the red in nine out of ten past maps.
- Four [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) players made the top-10 picks in this game, with [Özgür "woxic" Eker](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic) being the most popular one with 28% teams featuring the Turkish AWPer. The team’s popularity is explained by modest prices, but it’s worth noting that they are set to face [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) in the winners’ match, a team they lost to a few days ago at EPL.
