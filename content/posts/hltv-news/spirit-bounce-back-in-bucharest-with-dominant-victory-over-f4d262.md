[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) made quick work of [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) with a 2-0 victory on Mirage (13-5) and Anubis (13-3) to bounce back after their opening day loss to [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) at PGL Masters Bucharest, and are now 1-1 in the Swiss group stage.

The series kicked off on [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo)'s Mirage pick, and although they won the first pistol round on the attack, they were quickly subdued as [Dmitry "sh1ro" Sokolov](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) and [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) took down all five Ts with their SMGs in the second round.

From there, [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) never lost control of the series, going on to take an 8-4 lead on the CT side and giving up just a single round in the second half on the way to a dominant 13-5 before going to their pick.

Anubis started the same way as Mirage had, with [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) winning the first pistol round on the attack, and once again failing to convert the second round against the forcebuy.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) found even less resistance on Anubis than they had on Mirage, giving up just three rounds at the half before cleanly mopping up the map on the attack, 13-3, to put an end to the series.

By the end of the match, [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) had failed to win consecutive rounds even once, which is not a promising prospect for the Chinese team as they enter the 0-2 pool of the Swiss group stage without reaching double digits in any of the four maps played against [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) and [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit).
