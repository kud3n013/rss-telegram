[FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) are the first team into PGL Masters Bucharest playoffs after taking down [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) 2-1.

It's a brilliant result for the team on debut with [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus), who ended the series on a 1.33 rating in a throwback to some of his [past matador-like performances](https://www.hltv.org/stats/players/matches/19164/sunpayus?startDate=2021-01-01&endDate=2023-12-31&opponentTeam=9565) against [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) and [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality).

Everyone in this team treats me as a star player

[Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) to PGL

The Spanish sniper, benched by [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) in June, has a 1.35 rating for the event after 7 maps and has clearly made the most of his time away from the professional scene.

"Everything is clicking", [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) told the desk post-game. "I told the coach, I don't want to change what you guys do, I just want to adapt. Everyone in this team treats me as a star player, they're dropping me a Deagle on ecos, they're saving money for my AWP.

"It feels so good. I feel so confident. I have so much fun with them, they listen in reviews, and are learning super fast."

Mirage began the series, and despite [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) stand-in [Justinas "jL" Lekavicius](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) [grenading himself](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/SavorySuspiciousKeyboardDuDudu-nsJUME-So6xdYnYA), the world number one showed their class down the stretch and took the map.

On [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s pick of Dust2, though, the game started to flip. The crucial round was a [2v4 won](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/BillowingObeseCattleNomNom-OuA3BLTvJNtVBOya) by [Aulon "Krabeni" Fazlija](https://www.hltv.org/player/22203/krabeni) and [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus), but the Spaniard was excellent throughout and has now won six clutches in this group stage.

On Anubis, [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) looked like they were in the driving seat after an 8-4 CT side, but that was when [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) came alive.

[Yasin "xfl0ud" Koç](https://www.hltv.org/player/19187/xfl0ud) was particularly stellar, posting two four-kill holds on the B bombsite, as [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) felt the absence of [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex) in their inability to break through.

[Krabeni](https://www.hltv.org/player/22203/krabeni)'s mid-round calling was praised by [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) post-game, and it's clear why; [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) were simply a step ahead, all the way through, and now have playoffs to look forward to.

## Astralis' nervous wait goes on

With this result, [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) and [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) remain in a situation where both teams might have [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) on their invited roster to PGL Major Singapore.

Under Major rules, the higher-ranked team would be the only one allowed to attend Singapore, which is likely to be [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) after they went 3-0 in this group stage.

To avoid this, [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) must play five games with [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus), something that can only happen now if they win their quarter-final.

[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) would then need [Rasmus "HooXi" Nielsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/10096/hooxi) or [Jakob "jabbi" Nygaard](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi) to play at Stake Ranked instead of [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) to prevent the Ukrainian entering their lineup.

The five-game rule is also in play for [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality); [jL](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) will now be the player on their invited roster, since [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) must play at least five games at this event, which should mean stickers for the Lithuanian and none for [apEX](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex).
