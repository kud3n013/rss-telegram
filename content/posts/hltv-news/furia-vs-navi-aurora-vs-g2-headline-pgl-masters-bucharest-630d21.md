The match-ups for the second day of PGL Masters Bucharest are set after an opening day of upsets, with [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), and [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) all stumbling.

The 1-0 pool will see the apEX-less [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) lock horns with [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz), while [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) will be looking to keep their win streak going against [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere).

[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid), who pulled off one of Saturday's biggest upsets by defeating [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), will face [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut), while [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) will square off against [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga).

In the 0-1 pool, the headline fixture is [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) vs [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2), who have not met since BLAST Open Porto. [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) will aim to recover from their opening-day setback against [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo), while [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) will try to claim their first win at the event against [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z).

Rounding out the 0-1 pool will be a Brazilian derby featuring [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) and [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele).
