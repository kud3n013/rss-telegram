[FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) won the Roman Imperium Cup IX on Saturday after a 2-0 thrashing of [NAVI Junior](https://www.hltv.org/team/10371/navi-junior), moving up 14 places in the global VRS to 16th.

[Russel "Twistzz" Van Dulken](https://www.hltv.org/player/10394/twistzz)'s charges now have a 71.8% chance of qualifying for PGL Major Singapore, [according to HLTV's latest projections](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302). They will attend two more LAN events before the November 2 invite cutoff: DraculaN Season 7 (October 19-23) in Bucharest, and RLG Tesla Cup (October 27-29) in Belgrade.

It was [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze)’s first LAN title since IEM Chengdu 2024, with [David "frozen" Čerňanský](https://www.hltv.org/player/9960/frozen) the sole member left from that squad. Their previous LAN final, at HLC Belgrade PRO 2026, ended in a loss to [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big) that cost them a place at the IEM Cologne Major.

[FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) capped their Roman Imperium Cup IX run with a one-sided series in which they conceded only four rounds. The campaign was far from straightforward, though, as they had to recover from a map down against [Sangal](https://www.hltv.org/team/10333/sangal) in the opening round and again against [NAVI Junior](https://www.hltv.org/team/10371/navi-junior) in the upper bracket final, where the decider went to overtime.
