[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) have [announced](https://t.me/betboomteamcs2/2891) that [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) has extended his contract with the organization during the summer off-season.

"For us as an organization, retaining a player of such caliber and continuing to build a team around him was one of our top priorities," [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) CEO Artem Saveliev wrote in [the Telegram statement](https://t.me/betboomteamcs2/2891).

The 22-year-old joined the team in March 2024 and averaged a 1.17 rating across his tenure, rising to a team-high 1.20 during 2026 in a breakout period for the squad.

He made his Major debut with [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) at the IEM Cologne Major earlier in the year, with the team recently placing second at the Thunderpick World Championship and clinching Singapore Major qualification in the process.

*HLTV has reached out to BETBOOM for comment regarding the contract length and has not yet received a response. The article will be updated accordingly should a statement be issued.*
