FISSURE has announced details of its fourth Counter-Strike Playground event, which will run from August 18-30, 2027, and feature 15 teams invited directly through the VRS alongside one closed qualifier winner.

Invitations will be based on the first monthly VRS update published in July 2027, with main-event invites issued from the top of the global ranking. Eight teams will receive invitations to an online closed qualifier through the European VRS, competing for the final LAN spot.

It is worth noting that Georgia is listed as the tournament's visa region, but the announcement does not specify a venue.

The advertised $1.25 million pool consists of $450,000 in prize money, $550,000 in club rewards and $250,000 for teams ranked in the VRS top 8.

The champions will earn $165,000 in prize money and $135,000 in club rewards, for a combined $300,000 before any applicable participation bonus. It will be distributed as follows:

1. $165,000 + Club share: $135,000\
2. $75,000 + Club share: $85,000\
3. $50,000 + Club share: $60,000\
4. $30,000 + Club share: $46,000\
5-8. $20,000 + Club share: $33,500\
9-11. $8,000 + Club share: $14,000\
12-14. $6,000 + Club share: $10,000\
15-16. $4,000 + Club share: $9,000

The separate VRS participation payments will range from $60,000 for the world No. 1 to $10,000 each for the seventh and eighth-ranked teams, provided they accept an invitation. The full breakdown is:

**Participation bonus**\

FISSURE also announced August 17-28, 2028, as the dates for another Counter-Strike tournament, without providing further details.
