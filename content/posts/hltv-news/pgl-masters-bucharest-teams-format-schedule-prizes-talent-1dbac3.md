## Teams and format

The $1.25 million event will feature a stacked field as 12 of the 13 highest-ranked teams per the [July 6 VRS](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/2026/july/6) accepted invites. This includes teams like [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality), Thunderpick World Championship winners [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) and the reigning PGL Masters Bucharest champions [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora).

The only exception was [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons), who turned down a place at the event, leaving them with no further opportunities to earn VRS points before the November 2 cut-off for Singapore Major invites.

The 12 invited teams will be joined by the winners of regional qualifiers held in Europe ([Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga)), North America ([Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid)), South America ([DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele)) and Asia ([TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo)).

The tournament will utilize a best-of-three Swiss stage to send eight teams to a single-elimination playoff bracket, which will culminate in a best-of-five grand final.

PGL Masters Bucharest will mark [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus)'s debut for [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) after he was brought in to replace [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) on the AWP. The event will also see [Justinas "jL" Lekavicius](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) again play for [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) as a stand-in, this time as a replacement for [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex).
