[FlyQuest](https://www.hltv.org/team/12774/flyquest) made quick work of the field at the ANZC Locals Brisbane Season 2 LAN and have added 149 points to their name in the VRS ranking, putting them in fifth place above [Lynn Vision](https://www.hltv.org/team/8840/lynn-vision) and [5star](https://www.hltv.org/team/13724/5star) as the race for Major qualification starts to heat up.

The [Alistair "aliStair" Johnston](https://www.hltv.org/player/11139/alistair)-led squad beat [madlikewizards](https://www.hltv.org/team/9275/madlikewizards) and [Abyssal](https://www.hltv.org/team/13686/abyssal) in the group stage, before taking out [KZG](https://www.hltv.org/team/12152/kzg) and [Ground Zero](https://www.hltv.org/team/8536/ground-zero) on the way to the final, where they once again beat [Abyssal](https://www.hltv.org/team/13686/abyssal) 2-0 to secure the tournament win in dominant fashion with no maps lost.

[FlyQuest](https://www.hltv.org/team/12774/flyquest) will attend two more regional events coming up before the Major's November 2 cutoff date, DFRAG Logitech G Pro Series and DFRAG Invitational, as they continue to contest for one of five Major invites in their region among a tightly packed group of seven teams comprising [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo), [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame), [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz), [Chinggis Warriors](https://www.hltv.org/team/12646/chinggis-warriors), [FlyQuest](https://www.hltv.org/team/12774/flyquest), [5star](https://www.hltv.org/team/13724/5star), and [Lynn Vision](https://www.hltv.org/team/8840/lynn-vision), all within less than 130 VRS points of each other.

Over at the MESA Pro Series Fall tournament in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, [Chinggis Warriors](https://www.hltv.org/team/12646/chinggis-warriors) beat [5star](https://www.hltv.org/team/13724/5star) in a crucial grand final that allowed them to surpass their compatriots in the VRS ranking and become the second-highest team in Mongolia behind [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz).

[5star](https://www.hltv.org/team/13724/5star) had beaten [Chinggis Warriors](https://www.hltv.org/team/12646/chinggis-warriors) in the opening round of the tournament, but the [Sodbileg "cool4st" Batbaatar](https://www.hltv.org/player/19537/cool4st)-led squad ran the gauntlet through the lower bracket to take revenge in the final and secure the $1,650 cash prize and the important VRS points.

[Chinggis Warriors](https://www.hltv.org/team/12646/chinggis-warriors) added 171 points to their VRS ranking to [5star](https://www.hltv.org/team/13724/5star)'s 94 after the tournament, and provisionally put themselves in fourth place while their grand final rivals dropped down just below the invite threshold in sixth place.
