[Virtus.pro](https://www.hltv.org/team/5378/virtuspro) have taken home the WINLINE MPKBK CIS LAN S9 title with a 3-2 win over [Nemesis](https://www.hltv.org/team/13355/nemesis) in the grand final, with the latter missing out on a win that would massively boost their chances of making the Singapore Major.

[Ivan "SELLTER" Miroshnikov](https://www.hltv.org/player/18670/sellter)'s squad came into the event as the highest-ranked VRS squad in attendance and cashed in on their favorite status with a flawless group-stage campaign, before wins against [WW](https://www.hltv.org/team/12967/ww) and [Black Phoenix](https://www.hltv.org/team/13924/black-phoenix) secured them a grand final spot.

There they met [Virtus.pro](https://www.hltv.org/team/5378/virtuspro), who were playing the tournament with [Robert "Patsi" Isyanov](https://www.hltv.org/player/19673/patsi) as a stand-in, and it was the Russian rifler who topped the series with a 1.14 rating to help his team secure the trophy.

The event was especially important in terms of Singapore Major implications for [Nemesis](https://www.hltv.org/team/13355/nemesis), as the Russian squad would have kept realistic chances of qualifying alive with a win at the event.

A loss means their chances drop to an incredibly slim 8.9%, however, with their grand final opponents [Virtus.pro](https://www.hltv.org/team/5378/virtuspro) sitting at an even smaller 1.8%.

The other squad in attendance with a chance at making the Major, [K27](https://www.hltv.org/team/12895/k27), also failed to capitalize on the opportunity with a semi-final exit.
