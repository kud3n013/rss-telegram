[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) may have struggled at international events over the course of the season, but despite losing [Dongkai "Jee" Ji](https://www.hltv.org/player/20702/jee) to illness, they have taken a massive stride towards PGL Major Singapore qualification with a victory over [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) at PGL Masters Bucharest.

"It didn't really affect our mentality too much," super stand-in [WeiJie "zhokiNg" Zhong](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8601/zhoking) said of [Jee](https://www.hltv.org/player/20702/jee)'s absence in the wake of his team's victory. "The players probably felt like they could just play freely, without too many concerns, and they played really well."

[zhokiNg](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8601/zhoking) also spoke about what he brings to the team when he plays, the importance of this win over [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z), and their struggles across the season thus far.

> Note: This interview was conducted with the help of an interpreter, and the answers translated by HLTV.

---

**How important was that series for you and TYLOO? It looked after the game like you were very happy to get that victory.**

This match actually, to a large extent, determines whether we can qualify for the Singapore Major. So it's very important, this is key, this victory was crucial.

**How did the situation with Jee, his illness, affect the team's mentality going into the match?**

Actually for us, it didn't really affect our mentality too much, but on the server our firepower was definitely a lot weaker. But the players probably felt like they could just play freely, without too many concerns, and they played really well.

I can offer my own opinions and give calls, and in the mid-round I'll also coordinate everyone

[WeiJie "zhokiNg" Zhong](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8601/zhoking) on serving as a stand-in

**TYLOO have had a lot of success with you as a stand-in. Why do you think that is? Do you help with the calling in game, or what is your contribution to the team as a stand-in?**

Actually our team's biggest problem has always been the lack of a secondary IGL on the server, lack of someone who can help share the pressure with our in-game leader. So when I'm on the server, I can actually fill that gap pretty well. I can offer my own opinions and give calls, and in the mid-round I'll also coordinate everyone to execute things more smoothly.

**You've had a difficult season in international tournaments. The Big Event you've not had a lot of success, particularly compared to previous seasons. Why do you think that is? Why do you think you've struggled at these big international tournaments?**

You mean recently, right?

**Yeah, recently.**

The main reason is also what I just mentioned, that we're now stuck in a bottleneck. There's no secondary IGL on the server to share some of the pressure on offense, and also on defense, having a second person's ideas as well. That's mainly the reason. And that's also what we need to change and improve going forward.
