Long-time MVP critic [Alex "Mauisnake" Ellenberg](https://www.hltv.org/player/14497/mauisnake) and HLTV's Editor-in-Chief [Milan "Striker" Švejda](https://www.hltv.org/player/8678/striker) joined the regular Come Here panel of [Danish "Nohte" Allana](https://www.hltv.org/player/20824/nohte) and [Harry "NER0" Richards](https://www.hltv.org/player/25798/ner0) to shed light on the process and decision-making that goes into awarding the medals, how it affects the Top 20 list, and more.

The podcast broke down the criteria used for MVPs and EVPs, how the process has (or hasn't) changed over the years, and reviewed recent decisions event-by-event to answer questions from [Mauisnake](https://www.hltv.org/player/14497/mauisnake) and the community surrounding awards like [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) getting the BLAST Open Porto medal over [Dmitry "sh1ro" Sokolov](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro), [Nikola "NiKo" Kovač](https://www.hltv.org/player/3741/niko) beating [Emil "nota" Moskvitin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22929/nota) at BLAST Bounty Season 1 Finals, and [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) clinching the MVP instead of [Robin "ropz" Kool](https://www.hltv.org/player/11816/ropz) at the StarLadder Budapest Major.

[Striker](https://www.hltv.org/player/8678/striker) and [NER0](https://www.hltv.org/player/25798/ner0) also explained the tiebreaker process and cleared up widespread confusion and misunderstanding around why different metrics are referenced in MVP decision articles.

Does [NER0](https://www.hltv.org/player/25798/ner0) rig MVPs for [Ilya "m0NESY" Osipov](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy)? Does [Striker](https://www.hltv.org/player/8678/striker) award them for the sake of making the Top 20 race more interesting? All that and more is answered in the podcast, which you can watch on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3byCyeBwwe1kz5dkD9YAXq) or [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9SE4hvbC_u8).
