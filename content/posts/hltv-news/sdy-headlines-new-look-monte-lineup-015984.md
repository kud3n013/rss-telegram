**Update 13:26** : This article has been updated after Monte announced the rest of their lineup.

---

[Monte](https://www.hltv.org/team/11811/monte) have announced the arrival of [Viktor "sdy" Orudzhev](https://www.hltv.org/player/12731/sdy) as the first part of a rebuild after the sale of their core to [Luminosity](https://www.hltv.org/team/6290/luminosity).

[sdy](https://www.hltv.org/player/12731/sdy) was previously a [Monte](https://www.hltv.org/team/11811/monte) player from January 2023 to February 2024, making it to the BLAST.tv Paris Major playoffs.

He was last in action for [Passion UA](https://www.hltv.org/team/12426/passion-ua) on loan from [ENCE](https://www.hltv.org/team/4869/ence), where he had been in-game leader before the organization returned to their Finnish roots.

[sdy](https://www.hltv.org/player/12731/sdy) will be under the tutelage of [Tiaan "T.c" Coertzen](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8447/tc), with [Mateusz "mantuu" Wilczewski](https://www.hltv.org/player/10981/mantuu) on the AWP.

The three riflers will be [Cai "CYPHER" Watson](https://www.hltv.org/player/18571/cypher) — another hero of the Paris Major —, [Alexander "br0" Bro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16717/br0), and former [top 50 prospect](https://www.hltv.org/news/45439/hltv-prospects-ranking-september-2026) [Vladyslav "St0m4k" Lykhoshva](https://www.hltv.org/player/24281/st0m4k).

[Monte](https://www.hltv.org/team/11811/monte)'s new lineup has already been spotted in the Stake Ranked [open qualifier](https://www.faceit.com/en/championship/b2fa284a-d330-4063-99f7-91ab07d50069/Stake%20Ranked%20Episode%206%20Open%20Qualifier/teams?group=1), where they were knocked out by [Aurora Young Blud](https://www.hltv.org/team/12366/aurora-young-blud).

The Ukrainian organization are in need of a boost after engaging in a public relations battle following accusations of missed payments from [Aurimas "Bymas" Pipiras](https://www.hltv.org/player/19015/bymas).

[Bymas](https://www.hltv.org/player/19015/bymas) said payment issues were a "recurring issue" in his post, and that he was owed tens of thousands of dollars. [Monte](https://www.hltv.org/team/11811/monte) later denied that sum in a statement to [HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/45398/bymas-monte-owes-me-tens-of-thousands-of-dollars-monte-responds).
