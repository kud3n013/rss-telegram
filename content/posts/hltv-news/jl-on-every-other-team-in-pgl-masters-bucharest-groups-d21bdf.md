### Things to note

- This game will only include the group stage. The playoff game will start once round 5 is concluded and five teams are known.
- Players whose teams finish 3-0 get two padding rounds; teams that go through 3-1 get one round of padding.

### Player prices & pick stats

- jL stands in for apEX at this event and was assigned the price of $190,000. This offering was fairly popular among players, making the Lithuanian the most-picked player in the game with nearly half the teams featuring the stand-in.
- donk is the most popular “golden card” in this game despite the barely affordable price tag of $250,000. Serious compromises are required to afford the Russian star, but it might be worth it in case of a confident group stage run with up to two rounds of padding available.
