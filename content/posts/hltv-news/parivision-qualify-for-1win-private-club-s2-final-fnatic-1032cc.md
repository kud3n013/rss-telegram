The penultimate day of 1win Private Club Season 2 has finished with just three teams remaining in the running for the trophy.

The day began with [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) and [magic](https://www.hltv.org/team/13592/magic) being eliminated from the event and suffering costly losses in both of the teams' Major bids.

[fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) were bested by [PRIVATE](https://www.hltv.org/team/14030/private), which has decreased their chances of qualifying to 17%, while [magic](https://www.hltv.org/team/13592/magic) are down to 13% after their loss to [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz).

Elsewhere in the bracket, [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) qualified for the final by beating [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big) in what could have been a huge VRS-point haul for [Benjamin "blameF" Bremer](https://www.hltv.org/player/15165/blamef) and company, and [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) stayed alive after a dominant 2-0 over [PRIVATE](https://www.hltv.org/team/14030/private).

Wednesday's results mean that [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big) will face [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) in the lower bracket final, where the international squad could raise their Major chances to 40%, with the winner then set to face [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) in the best-of-five final.
