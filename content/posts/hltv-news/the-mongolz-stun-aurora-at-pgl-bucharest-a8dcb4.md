[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) began their PGL Masters Bucharest campaign with an upset 2-1 victory over [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) (Inferno 12-16, Nuke 13-2, Dust2 13-9).

The Mongolian team are making their tier-one debut with [Yesuntumur "nin9" Gantulga](https://www.hltv.org/player/15072/nin9) at the Romanian tournament, and while their newest recruit averaged a steady 1.04 rating across the series, the team looked much more cohesive overall.

The victory comes just days after [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) secured the title at 1win Private Club Season 2, likely earning a Singapore Major Stage 2 invite in the process.

The win against [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) gave the Mongolian squad a further 84 VRS points, which now puts their [Stage 2 qualification chances at 98.4%.](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302)

Their PGL Masters Bucharest opener was a hard-fought affair, with [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) finding themselves a map down after dropping their Inferno map pick in overtime due to a fantastic [Ali "Wicadia" Haydar Yalçın](https://www.hltv.org/player/21243/wicadia) performance.

Nuke was a completely different story, however, as the Mongolian squad romped to a 13-2 victory and kept up their momentum on the Dust2 decider.

[Usukhbayar "910" Banzragch](https://www.hltv.org/player/21809/910) (1.55 map rating) and [Sodbayar "Techno" Munkhbold](https://www.hltv.org/player/20275/techno) (1.51) took over proceedings and helped [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) take a 13-9 victory, aided by two won pistols, and secure a spot in the 1-0 pool.
