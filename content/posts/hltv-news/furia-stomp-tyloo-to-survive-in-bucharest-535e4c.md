[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) stayed in cruise control to eliminate [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) from PGL Masters Bucharest.

After beating [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) in spite of [Dongkai "Jee" Ji](https://www.hltv.org/player/20702/jee)'s absence, [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) put themselves in a strong position for the Major, but any further wins would have made a huge difference for the Chinese side.

Now, they travel to HyperX Retake needing a top-three finish in Lisbon to guarantee an invite to Singapore.

Defeating [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in this kind of form, however, would have proved a bridge too far for many a team, let alone one ranked nearly 30 places below them that had to use their coach as a stand-in.

Two of [WeiJie "zhokiNg" Zhong](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8601/zhoking)'s lowest-rated maps had already come against [Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) before this series, which has now made it [four out of six](https://www.hltv.org/stats/players/matches/8601/zhoking?sortColumn=Rating&sortDirection=Ascending&matchType=MvpEvents) as the firepower differential was laid bare.

Even [FalleN](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) was able to frag out, topping the scoreboard on Inferno and finishing the series with a 1.48 rating as [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) grabbed only seven rounds.

[Qianhao "Moseyuh" Chen](https://www.hltv.org/player/21621/moseyuh) seemed the likeliest to provide significant resistance, but even in this round where he strung together a brilliant quad-kill, [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) still left the round with the win.

[Yi "JamYoung" Yang](https://www.hltv.org/player/19645/jamyoung) managed just 46.8 ADR, and with that any win condition [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) hoped for quickly vanished.

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) now head into the 2-2 pool needing just one more win to make the playoffs.
