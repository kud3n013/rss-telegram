[MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) have announced the departure of [Breno "brnz4n" Poletto](https://www.hltv.org/player/20987/brnz4n), who was benched from the starting lineup in August to make way for [Tomas "tomaszin" Corna](https://www.hltv.org/player/22570/tomaszin).

The news brings to an end a long association between [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) and [brnz4n](https://www.hltv.org/player/20987/brnz4n), the 23-year-old having joined the organization in 2021 as part of the academy setup.

After impressing on the academy squad [brnz4n](https://www.hltv.org/player/20987/brnz4n) was promoted to the main team to replace outgoing AWPer [Adriano "WOOD7" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/14390/wood7) mid-way through 2022, and became a versatile member of the squad through many iterations.

[brnz4n](https://www.hltv.org/player/20987/brnz4n) was briefly benched at the end of 2024 in favor of [Lucas "Lucaozy" Neves](https://www.hltv.org/player/18717/lucaozy), but returned to the lineup early the next year and continued his time with the squad until his recent replacement by [tomaszin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22570/tomaszin).

[brnz4n](https://www.hltv.org/player/20987/brnz4n) won numerous regional titles during his time with [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) and even enjoyed modest international success, winning ESL Challenger Melbourne 2024. He also attended three Majors with [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr), his best finish coming at BLAST.tv Austin Major, when [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) knocked out [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) and [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) before narrowly missing out on a Stage 3 berth.
