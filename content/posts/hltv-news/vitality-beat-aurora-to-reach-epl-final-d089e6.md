[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) are through to the grand final of ESL Pro League 24 following a come-from-behind victory against [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), who burned bright on the opening map of Anubis thanks to a great performance from [Özgür "woxic" Eker](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic) but couldn't play with the same intensity in the rest of the series.

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s victory was anchored by a standout performance from star player [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo), who bounced back from a quiet first map to post a series-high 1.44 rating, driven largely by a 2.36 rating on Cache, his highest ever on the map.

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) will contest the final against the winner of [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) vs. [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz). It will be the chance for the three-time Major champions to win their second trophy of the season before [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex) temporarily steps down from the roster for PGL Masters Bucharest.

Neither team could pull away in the first half of Anubis, trading three-round streaks. [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) twice appeared on course for a sizeable lead, only for [Ismailcan "XANTARES" Dörtkardeş](https://www.hltv.org/player/7938/xantares) and [woxic](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic) to halt the momentum with big individual plays.

Heading into the second half, [Ashley "ash" Battye](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8975/ash) hailed his players' reaction to [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s early aggression. "We played really, really, really well. I'm very happy with how we played. Very, very impressed," the British coach said, asking them to keep it up in the second half.

[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora)'s players followed the coach's instructions and took control of the game from the initial exchanges. [A lapse in communication](https://www.twitch.tv/eslcs/clip/PlainSlipperyDragonOSsloth-IIoDH0oFR07wUFm8) cost them an important round against a shoddy buy, but they remained composed and put the game to rest, with [woxic](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic) posting a jaw-dropping 3.13 CT rating.

The Turkish AWPer lacked the same bite on Cache as [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) could only muster four rounds on defense despite a stellar showing from [XANTARES](https://www.hltv.org/player/7938/xantares) (15-6 K/D, 130 ADR and 2.31 rating).

[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) kept their comeback hopes alive by turning around a 2v4 in the pistol and adding two more rounds to cut the deficit to one. However, [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) surged once their economy recovered and went on a 5-0 run to close out the map and force a decider.

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) carried that momentum into Mirage, strolling to a 7-1 lead before [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) hit back with three in a row, making great use of a double AWP setup. [Damjan "kyxsan" Stoilkovski](https://www.hltv.org/player/19677/kyxsan)'s troops put together a streak of rounds while up against series point, but their comeback attempt was thwarted by a triple kill from [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) in the 21st round.
