At PGL Masters Bucharest [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) have seemed like a team revitalized, taking down [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in 2-0 fashion to take a massive stride towards Major qualification, despite enduring a difficult season. In their first crack at making the playoffs in Bucharest, it took an in-form [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) to slow [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere)'s roll.

"I do think that there are things that we are doing, like always trying to do better," [Aleksi "Aleksib" Virolainen](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib) told HLTV in the wake of his team's defeat by [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom). "All the small details, like remembering everything that we have on the playbook. We've been doing that pretty well thus far this tournament."

[Aleksib](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib) says his team are not getting ahead of themselves despite the positive results thus far and are focused on making the playoffs. "Overall, I think everything is going well. We just need to do our best not to lose to ourselves and not to be frustrated and just keep our heads up high and play the best CS we can ever play."

**Yeah, so first commiserations. Talk me through that series through your eyes.**

Not the best day from us. Unfortunately, there have been probably quite a few of these not best days in recent history. But I do think that today really felt completely different when it came down to the atmosphere and everything. I do think that they played better than our last two opponents, and yeah, they really deserved the 2-0 today.

**In general though, this tournament has been a lot better from NAVI, right? We've seen you pick up some good wins, and I think also we've seen the CS on the server look better. How has the tournament been overall so far for you?**

Yeah, I do think we agree. 2-0 wins, and some maps convincing, some maps obviously tough even against G2. I think that game was tough, but in the end we were the winner, so that's all that matters pretty much. It's always good to start the tournament like this, but now we just need to close it. We need to get the third win and get into the playoffs, and go from there.

It's always good to start the tournament like this, but now we just need to close it

[Aleksi "Aleksib" Virolainen](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib)

**Do you feel like you're on the right trajectory with NAVI at the moment?**

Yeah, for sure. What else is there to ask for? Start with two wins, especially grabbing the win against FURIA. Pretty important for us overall as a team, and they were having kind of a streak and also just came off of winning Malta.

Sucks to lose this game, but there's nothing to do about it. We have a new game tomorrow, and whatever happens tomorrow, one more match to be played. So just overall we are on a good trajectory, and we just need to do our best.

**Was there a sense of relief, particularly after yesterday? It put you in a really good position to qualify for the Major, which has been up in the air recently. Was there any sense of relief or any kind of positive feeling in the team?**

No. I don't think so at all. Maybe... I can't speak on what everyone is thinking to themselves, but I do think that as a group, we were just happy beating FURIA. It was not like celebrating anything that revolves around the Major. Overall, every one of us also expects to play the Major at least, you know? So that was not a topic at all. I think that we just need to focus on getting into the playoffs in this tournament.

Overall we are on a good trajectory, and we just need to do our best.

[Aleksi "Aleksib" Virolainen](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib)

**Okay. Considering that NAVI have played better at this tournament, are looking on an upward trend, why do you think that is? Is there anything you're doing differently? Is there anything you've seen improve within the team?**

It's tough to say, really. Maybe it's all of the discussions we've been having. It's tough for me to pick up on all the positive stuff since we just lost the game five minutes ago. But I do think that there are things that we are doing, like always trying to do better, all the small details, like remembering everything that we have on the playbook. We've been doing that pretty well thus far this tournament. Back to the drawing board after this game, and hopefully we're going to be twice as strong tomorrow.

Overall, I think everything is going well. We just need to do our best not to lose to ourselves and not to be frustrated and just keep our heads up high and play the best CS we can ever play.
