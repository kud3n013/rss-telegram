[G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) beat [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) twice in August but their fortunes have reversed since, and the difference between the two sides was made stark in the 0-1 match at PGL Masters.

[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) had to earn their 2-0 win, losing both pistols before winning Cache 13-11, and needing an [Özgür "woxic" Eker](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic) ace to fire their 16-14 comeback win on Inferno, but were the better side in the gun rounds and deserved their 2-0 win.

[Jimi "Jimpphat" Salo](https://www.hltv.org/player/18850/jimpphat) was the star on Cache, with [two brilliant B holds](https://www.hltv.org/news/45786/the-best-highlights-from-pgl-masters-bucharest-groups#f54), and was joined by [Ismailcan "XANTARES" Dörtkardeş](https://www.hltv.org/player/7938/xantares) winning eight opening duels.

Inferno, long a [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) favorite, was next and we seemed set for a decider after [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) went 12-9 behind.

But that was when [woxic](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic) came alive with all five frags in a 2v5, as [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2)'s stunning lack of coordination was laid bare.

That laid the path to overtime, and it took another [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) ace, this time from [Damjan "kyxsan" Stoilkovski](https://www.hltv.org/player/19677/kyxsan), who was blind for half of the kills, to take the scoreline up to 16.

After a day one loss to [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz), it's an important win for [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) who are set for a Stage 3 invite to the Major as things stand.

[G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2), meanwhile, now sit 0-2 and face a second group stage exit in a row. Their only win since losing the FPG 3 final in September came against [ShindeN](https://www.hltv.org/team/12959/shinden), a run of six losses in seven matches.

It was yet another series to forget for new AWPer [Artem "r1nkle" Moroz](https://www.hltv.org/player/20425/r1nkle), who ended the match with just 53 ADR and a 0.86 rating.
