[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) were the next underdogs to secure a victory as they defeated [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) in a three-map series (Anubis 10-13, Ancient 13-3, Mirage 16-14).

The result continues a trend of upsets at PGL Masters Bucharest, as [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) were the only high seeds to win their opening match so far.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) have [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) to thank for their victory, as the Russian sniper topped the scoreboard on all three maps and won the series for his team with a 1v3 clutch featuring a noscope and a Glock kill.

[Clip link](https://www.hltv.org/news/45791/[stream,https:/www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/ReliableThankfulOxPartyTime-E8nw6fWQs2fAX7hT])

On the other side of the server, [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) was a non-factor for most of the match, finishing the series with a 0.84 average rating and only one map above 1.00.

Worrying signs began early for [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) as they struggled to get their Anubis offense going, but a recovery to 5-7 proved enough for the [Boris "magixx" Vorobiev](https://www.hltv.org/player/18317/magixx)-led squad to secure their map pick.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) then completely took over in a one-sided Ancient victory before the match was decided on Mirage.

[zorte](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) continued his impact and had several individual pop-offs throughout the series, with [Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) eventually securing two match points with a defuse in a Molotov.

Despite that, [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) brought the game back to overtime, where [zorte](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) put the final nail in the [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) coffin with a 1v3 to send the Russian squad to the 0-1 pool.
