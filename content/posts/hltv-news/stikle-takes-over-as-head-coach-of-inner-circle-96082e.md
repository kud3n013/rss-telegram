[Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle) have announced that [Dmytro "jR" Chervak](https://www.hltv.org/player/8786/jr) is moving on from his position as head coach into a management role within the organization, and that [Klesti "stikle-" Kola](https://www.hltv.org/coach/20642/stikle) is stepping in as head coach for their upcoming events.

The news brings to an end [jR](https://www.hltv.org/player/8786/jr)'s year-long stint at the reins of [Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle), during which time he helped the team establish itself inside the top 20 of the global VRS and make deep runs in a number of events, notably winning the first Super DraculaN, which helped rocket them into Singapore Major contention.

[Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle)'s recent group-stage exit at Stake Pulse Beat III seriously hurt their Major chances, however, and now the Ukrainian side need at minimum a top-two finish at Stake Ranked Episode 5 to have a realistic shot at earning a berth for the Singapore Major.

[stikle-](https://www.hltv.org/coach/20642/stikle) has been serving as an assistant coach for [Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle) in recent months, departing his last head coach role with [Fingers Crossed](https://www.hltv.org/team/13466/fingers-crossed) at the start of the year. He is best known for his time with [Bad News Eagles](https://www.hltv.org/team/11518/bad-news-eagles) in 2022, during which time he helped the team make underdog runs to the Legends Stage of both PGL Major Antwerp and IEM Rio Major.

[Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle) did not state whether [stikle-](https://www.hltv.org/coach/20642/stikle) would remain at the helm of [Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle) long term, merely saying he would be coaching the team at Stake Ranked Episode 5 and Episode 6.
