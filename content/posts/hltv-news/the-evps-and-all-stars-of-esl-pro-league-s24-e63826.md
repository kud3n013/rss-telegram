[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) are back. If you weren't sure after StarSeries, you can be sure now.

That statement is tempered by [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex)'s upcoming absence for PGL Masters Bucharest, but ESL Pro League Katowice offered us a chance to see [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s main five players on fire before the final Major of the year.

[Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo)'s 34th MVP was not a procession, either, given that four of [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) ended the group stage above a 1.30 rating.

But after five player of the map performances in playoffs, en route to a 1.58 arena rating, [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) simply refused to let a teammate snatch this medal away.

---

This EVP article features new metrics on the player radars, so we would encourage you to read the new glossary.

The new metrics are designed to align more with how EVPs are actually decided, rather than a collection of aggregated stats over the whole event.

## Radar Chart Stat Glossary

- **Rating 3.0**: HLTV's composite rating of kills, damage, survival, KAST, multis and Swing
- **KPRW**: Kills per round win
- **Eco Swing**: Duels won above expected value, based on equipment of a player and their opponent
- **Impact Rounds**: Rounds with positive round swing
- **Round Swing**: The win probability sum of a player's actions
- **A Game**: A player's 75th percentile map
- **B Game**: A player's 50th percentile map
- **C Game**: A player's 25th percentile map
- **Vs Top 10**: Rating vs top 10 teams (HLTV Ranking)
- **Vs Top Half**: Rating vs teams who placed in the top half of the event
- **Rank in Team**: Avg. rank within your team by rating
- **Map Wins**: Rating in map wins in series wins

### Israel [Shahar "flameZ" Shushan](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez)

[flameZ](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez)'s fifth EVP of 2026 is a strong one, and he was the closest player to denying [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) the MVP after posting nine standout maps in Katowice.

The highlights were 2.00+ rated maps against [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) and [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision), but he had a map above 1.55 in every single [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) series, which shows the volume of his peaks.

So while it's another second place in the MVP race, Pro League is sure to be one of [flameZ](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez)'s best events of the year, and give him plenty of credit as he looks to improve on his two-time number seven placement in the Top 20 Players of the Year.

---

### Estonia [Robin "ropz" Kool](https://www.hltv.org/player/11816/ropz)

[ropz](https://www.hltv.org/player/11816/ropz) was a pillar of consistency in Poland, with only one map below the 1.00 mark and a 1.30 rating against top 10 teams. Like [flameZ](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez), he fell behind [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) in map and round wins, but it remains a very strong EVP indeed.

His 1.40 rating in groups came from peaks against [1win](https://www.hltv.org/team/10621/1win), [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) and [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons), no slouches, and he was solid in playoffs too with a 1.24 rating.

So even with a bit of a summer slump, [ropz](https://www.hltv.org/player/11816/ropz) now has back-to-back EVPs, and six EVP+ events for 2026.

---

### Israel [Lotan "Spinx" Giladi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18221/spinx)

[Spinx](https://www.hltv.org/player/18221/spinx) was [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz)'s team leader en route to the final in Spodek, with a 1.34 rating in map wins and 1.22 overall.

The team had a very tough run at the event, with an average opponent rank of 5.2, which makes [Spinx](https://www.hltv.org/player/18221/spinx)'s performance even more impressive.

He finished 79% of maps above the 1.00 mark, was brilliant against [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) on Dust2 twice, and did just enough to stand out in what was a true team effort.

---

### Türkiye [Ali "Wicadia" Haydar Yalçın](https://www.hltv.org/player/21243/wicadia)

It's three closers in a row, as [Wicadia](https://www.hltv.org/player/21243/wicadia) joins the EVP list.

After early concerns about his adaptation to the international [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), [Wicadia](https://www.hltv.org/player/21243/wicadia) has been restored to his prior status as the team's most impactful player.

Even before the low-stakes third-place decider, [Wicadia](https://www.hltv.org/player/21243/wicadia) was a strong EVP after some great map wins and putting up a fight against [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) in semi-finals.

He averaged a 1.19 rating against top 5 and top 10 teams, a 0.16 gap to his next teammate, and was just as dominant in round wins (1.10 KPRW, 0.94 next best).

---

### Russia [Ilya "m0NESY" Osipov](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy)

Even amid struggles for [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) as a team, [m0NESY](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) has been in excellent form since the summer break with a [1.33 rating](https://www.hltv.org/stats/players/19230/m0nesy?startDate=2026-07-01&endDate=2026-10-19) and three EVPs.

He and [Nikola "NiKo" Kovač](https://www.hltv.org/player/3741/niko) led the team in groups, but only [m0NESY](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) and [Maksim "kyousuke" Lukin](https://www.hltv.org/player/24177/kyousuke) put up any semblance of resistance against [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) in the quarter-finals as [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) floundered once again.

And it was that consistency that helped him reach EVP status, with him placing in the top three on the scoreboard (by rating) in 67% of his maps played, the second most of any player behind [flameZ](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez) (69%).

---

### Russia [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk)

[donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) completes the EVP list after what can still be considered an off-event by his lofty standards.

His floor often disappeared beneath him in Katowice, with five maps at or below a 0.81 rating, and he had three bad maps in the arena compared to two good ones.

But when we strip the nameplate off, there is no way that a player with a 1.48 rating in map wins, 1.24 KPRW and a 1.19 in playoffs would be denied an EVP nod.

So while his poor floor and [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s fairly easy run (their average opponent rank was just 25.1) held him back, [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) still did just enough for his eighth award of 2026.

## Honorable mentions

[Ádám "torzsi" Torzsás](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi) and [Oldřich "PR" Nový](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr) were the closest players to the EVP list, both doing their part in [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz)'s run to the final.

[torzsi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi), especially, was in prime position for an EVP after a 1.32 rating in groups, but he finished playoffs with a 0.93 rating after a disastrous final.

[PR](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr) was much better in playoffs, with four strong maps in the arena, but didn't get much credit in groups, which meant both players fell just below the threshold.

[NiKo](https://www.hltv.org/player/3741/niko), as mentioned, was great in groups but like [Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato), [William "mezii" Merriman](https://www.hltv.org/player/18462/mezii) and [Vladislav "xiELO" Lysov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22471/xielo), did not do well enough in playoffs to cross the EVP boundary.

In groups, [Ihor "w0nderful" Zhdanov](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) and [Franco "dgt" Garcia](https://www.hltv.org/player/14736/dgt) stood out most, but neither did enough for a nod.

## All-Star

[ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) and [flameZ](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez) are the easy picks for our All-Star best five, as the two best players of the event.

Then [PR](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr) slots in as anchor, as the best in his role despite missing out on an EVP.

The tricky bit is the IGL and Closer, due to our rule around having a maximum of three players per team.

When choosing between [apEX](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex) and [ropz](https://www.hltv.org/player/11816/ropz), the key factor is the distance to the second-best option in their category, which led us to choose [apEX](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex) for his 10% lead in T-side round win rate over [Dorian "xertioN" Berman](https://www.hltv.org/player/20312/xertion).

That means [Spinx](https://www.hltv.org/player/18221/spinx) leapfrogs [ropz](https://www.hltv.org/player/11816/ropz) to be our Closer, reuniting him with the [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) core he left behind.
