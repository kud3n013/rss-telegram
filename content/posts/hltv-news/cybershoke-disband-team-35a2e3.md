[CYBERSHOKE](https://www.hltv.org/team/12380/cybershoke) [announced](https://t.me/team_cybershoke/6382) they disbanded their current roster and are set to return with a new lineup next season.

The announcement states that [Roman "Mokuj1n" Petrushenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/24176/mokuj1n) and [Daniil "alpha" Demin](https://www.hltv.org/player/20430/alpha) are moved to the transfer list, while [Vladimir "yiksrezo" Mitrofanov](https://www.hltv.org/player/24485/yiksrezo) and [Ilya "FenomeN" Kolodko](https://www.hltv.org/player/22879/fenomen) depart as free agents.

The organization also shared that both [Emil "nota" Moskvitin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22929/nota) (on loan from [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision)) and [Alimzhan "Alkaren" Bitimbay](https://www.hltv.org/player/23600/alkaren) (on loan from [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic)) will return to their parent organizations with immediate effect.

[CYBERSHOKE](https://www.hltv.org/team/12380/cybershoke) were ranked No. 73 in the VRS ahead of their disbandment after a turbulent period, during which they were represented by 11 different players throughout 2026.
