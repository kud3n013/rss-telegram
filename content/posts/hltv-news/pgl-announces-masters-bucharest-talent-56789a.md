PGL has [announced](https://x.com/pglesports/status/2112511601739751745) the talent lineup for Masters Bucharest 2026.

The $1,250,000 studio event features a stacked field of teams, including [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality), [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), and will be played in PGL's usual format of a Swiss group feeding into an eight-team playoff.

[Sudhen "Bleh" Wahengbam](https://www.hltv.org/player/18474/bleh), [Jacob "Pimp" Winneche](https://www.hltv.org/player/7154/pimp), [Zvonimir "Professeur" Burazin](https://www.hltv.org/player/21814/professeur) and [Halvor "vENdetta" Gulestøl](https://www.hltv.org/player/7261/vendetta) will join the desk as analysts, alongside desk hosts [Richard "Richard Lewis" Lewis](https://www.hltv.org/player/24072/richard-lewis) and [Pala "Pala" Gilroy Sen](https://www.hltv.org/player/10046/pala).

[Conner "Scrawny" Girvan](https://www.hltv.org/player/19511/scrawny), [Mohan "launders" Govindasamy](https://www.hltv.org/player/10472/launders), Harvey ["Skriv"](https://x.com/skrivcasts) Rodgers and [Neo "Ne0kai" Caine](https://www.hltv.org/player/24181/ne0kai) will be the on-site commentary team with [James "BanKs" Banks](https://www.hltv.org/player/18473/banks) on interview duty.

[Anders "Anders" Blume](https://www.hltv.org/player/8527/anders), [Vince "Vince" Hill](https://www.hltv.org/player/16027/vince), [Harry "JustHarry" Russell](https://www.hltv.org/player/21859/justharry) and [Hugo "Hugo" Byron](https://www.hltv.org/player/21858/hugo) will fulfill remote casting duties.
