[Italo "ksloks" Meinberg](https://www.hltv.org/player/22182/ksloks) will replace [Vinicius "n1ssim" Pereira](https://www.hltv.org/player/19686/n1ssim) on [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy)'s starting lineup until the end of the year.

Earlier in 2026, [n1ssim](https://www.hltv.org/player/19686/n1ssim) was briefly benched on [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) as the team sought a deal for [Felipe "insani" Yuji](https://www.hltv.org/player/21037/insani), but the anchor-lurker soon returned to the lineup and helped them win FISSURE Playground 3.

The team's results have taken a nosedive since that trophy lift, however, after [Andrei "arT" Piovezan](https://www.hltv.org/player/12521/art) missed Pro League due to a bout of meningitis.

The reason for this second benching remains unclear at this time.

[ksloks](https://www.hltv.org/player/22182/ksloks), a 21-year-old who frequently appeared on the HLTV Prospects list before graduating, has been a free agent since leaving [ODDIK](https://www.hltv.org/team/11768/oddik) and was already registered by [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) as a substitute in case [arT](https://www.hltv.org/player/12521/art) had to miss any more matches.

He will now get his chance, as a short-term addition for the final three events of the year in what is still a top-ten team.
