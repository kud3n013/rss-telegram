PGL has confirmed its calendar for 2028, featuring a total of six events over the year with a combined investment of $11 million.

The tournament organizer previously announced eight events across 2028, but has now reduced that number.

The events that were previously announced were an event during August 16-28, which coincides with regular dates for CS Asia Championships, and a tournament from November 1-13.

PGL's 2028 calendar now looks as follows:

- PGL #1 – January 14-24
- PGL #2 – February 9-21
- PGL #3 – March 23-April 3
- PGL #4 – April 19-May 1
- PGL #5 – August 31-September 11
- PGL #6 – October 4-16
