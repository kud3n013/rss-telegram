[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) AWPer [Dongkai "Jee" Ji](https://www.hltv.org/player/20702/jee) has been ruled out for the remainder of PGL Masters Bucharest with a bout of chickenpox, the Chinese organization has announced.

The 21-year-old is currently undergoing treatment and resting in isolation. Head coach [WeiJie "zhokiNg" Zhong](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8601/zhoking) will step in for the rest of the tournament, starting with Monday's elimination series against [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z).

The 32-year-old coach last played for the team at FISSURE Playground 1, where he replaced [Jee](https://www.hltv.org/player/20702/jee) for three matches due to visa issues. The AWPer linked up with the rest of the squad ahead of the semi-finals and helped the team win the $1 million tournament.

The news comes as a heavy blow for [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo), who find themselves in the 0-2 pool after defeats to [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) and [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit). They are currently ranked fifth in the Asia VRS with a 59 percent chance of reaching the Singapore Major.

It remains unclear if [Jee](https://www.hltv.org/player/20702/jee) will be available for HyperX Retake Season 13, a tier-2 LAN that will kick off Thursday in Portugal.

"We will continue to monitor Jee's condition and make further arrangements based on his recovery and tournament regulations," [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) wrote.

[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) will take on [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) with the following lineup:
