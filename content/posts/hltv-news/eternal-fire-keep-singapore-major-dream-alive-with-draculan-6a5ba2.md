[Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire) won Digital Crusade DraculaN Season 7 after getting the better of [SINNERS](https://www.hltv.org/team/10577/sinners) in the grand final with a 2-1 scoreline (Mirage 6-13, Cache 16-12, Ancient 16-14).

The win completes a playoff run that included a loss to [SINNERS](https://www.hltv.org/team/10577/sinners) in the upper bracket final, but [Samet "jottAAA" Köklü](https://www.hltv.org/player/22361/jottaaa) and company got their revenge in the grand final to take home the trophy.

The victory keeps [Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire)'s dream of making Singapore alive ([5.4% as per HLTV's prediction](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302)), with the international squad currently set to compete at\
Stake Ranked Episode 5 before the cutoff.

The event had a massive effect on the Major race, with several teams transforming their campaigns for better or worse.

Outside of winners [Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire), [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) increased their chance of making it to Singapore with a 5th-6th placing, while [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) and [Luminosity](https://www.hltv.org/team/6290/luminosity) suffered tough defeats in their Major bid.
