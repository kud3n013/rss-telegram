[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) have locked in a playoff berth at PGL Masters Bucharest after outlasting [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) in a three-map series, making up for a blowout loss on their own Nuke pick (2-13) with wins on Dust2 (13-8) and Mirage (16-14).

A Herculean effort from [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) on the decider, where he posted 12 multi-kills, a 35-20 K-D, 123.6 ADR, and 1.93 rating, wasn't enough for [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) to get the job done despite leading 12-10, with [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz)'s excellent teamplay and mental resilience making all the difference in clinching the victory.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) steamrolled on Nuke, never looking troubled in a near-lockout defense powered by [Boris "magixx" Vorobiev](https://www.hltv.org/player/18317/magixx), [Dmitry "sh1ro" Sokolov](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro), and [Myroslav "zont1x" Plakhotia](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x). [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) won just two rounds, one by way of a 1v2 by [Oldřich "PR" Nový](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr), in an otherwise desolate showing on their own pick, leaving [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) to run away with a 13-2 victory to open the series.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s good form carried into the start of Dust2, where a pistol win and a few B attacks put them up 5-2 on the T side. Their hard work came undone after a triple and 1v1 clutch by [Dorian "xertioN" Berman](https://www.hltv.org/player/20312/xertion) in round nine spurred a recovery for [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), with [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) failing to get onto a bombsite for the rest of the half as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) won out.

[xertioN](https://www.hltv.org/player/20312/xertion) continued to lead by example on the T side, with [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) securing their first pistol win of the series to extend their lead to 11-5. Multis from [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) and [zont1x](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x) helped [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) close the gap to three rounds, but a double Deagle entry up Catwalk from [xertioN](https://www.hltv.org/player/20312/xertion), combined with a AWP triple from [Ádám "torzsi" Torzsás](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi), set [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) on map point and allowed them to convert the map to force a decider.

The script was flipped on Mirage, where a 1v2 by [PR](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr) in the pistol and a 1v3 clutch by [torzsi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi) with the AWP saw [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) run out to an early 6-1 lead on the offense. [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk), already having an excellent map, then turned it up a notch further as he ran rampant over [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), finding a masterful ace to break their money. Another triple in the final round of the half went to waste as [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) overextended in a 3v2, leaving [xertioN](https://www.hltv.org/player/20312/xertion) to clutch a 1v2 to keep [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) in the lead at halftime.

[donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) couldn't be stopped, though, and more multi-kills from the Russian phenom put [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) back in control as they went up 8-7 off the back of a pistol win. But [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) had a read on [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s early game plan, and a lean towards B in the first gun rounds paid dividends with [torzsi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi) grabbing triple kills on the AWP to put [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) back in the lead.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) then strung four together off the back of more impactful kills by [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk), but they squandered their 12-10 lead as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) stepped up with some excellent teamplay to secure overtime and convert a 15-12 lead for the series win.
