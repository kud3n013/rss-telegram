[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) survived some tilting rounds to overcome [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) and take their second win at PGL Masters.

Up 8-6 on Nuke, [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) lost an anti-force, a 5v3 after [Ádám "torzsi" Torzsás](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi)'s game crashed, and a 3v1 to [Yesuntumur "nin9" Gantulga](https://www.hltv.org/player/15072/nin9). That was also during a run where [Anarbileg "cobrazera" Uuganbayar](https://www.hltv.org/player/23402/cobrazera) got a kill eight rounds in a row.

But the favorites' mentality did not waver, and [Oldřich "PR" Nový](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr)'s Ramp hold at 9-10 was the turning point as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) recovered to win 13-10.

Mirage was also close in scoreline, but [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) avoided the types of round losses that affected Nuke and were dominant in the gun rounds.

[PR](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr) carried over his CT form, starting map two with a 14-1 K-D as the B anchor on Mirage. He ended the series with 30 frags on CT side, a 1.74 rating over both maps.

That was in stark contrast to his counterpart support player [Sodbayar "Techno" Munkhbold](https://www.hltv.org/player/20275/techno), who has now had [eleven straight Mirage maps under a 1.01 rating](https://www.hltv.org/stats/players/matches/20275/techno?startDate=2026-04-26&endDate=2026-10-26&maps=de_mirage). The absences of [Garidmagnai "bLitz" Byambasuren](https://www.hltv.org/player/20194/blitz) and [Usukhbayar "910" Banzragch](https://www.hltv.org/player/21809/910) were even more damning for [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz)'s chances.

[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) would have climbed to 12th on the VRS had they beaten [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), strengthening their case for some invites at the start of next year, but their status in Stage 2 of PGL Major Singapore is already fairly safe.

Instead, their focus will be on picking up two more wins and making their first notable event playoffs since Bounty Season 2 in July to cement the clear improvement shown by their new lineup.

[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), meanwhile, are one game away from playoffs and are already putting their day-one defeat to [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) in the rearview mirror.
