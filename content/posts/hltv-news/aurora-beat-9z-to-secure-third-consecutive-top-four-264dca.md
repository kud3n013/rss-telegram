[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) have defeated [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) in a straightforward 2-0 rematch to secure a playoffs spot at the Thunderpick World Championship in Malta.

They have earned themselves a matchup against [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in the semi-finals, a rematch from their encounter in the group stage of ESL Pro League Season 24 just 10 days ago, in which [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) came out victorious 2-1.

With the victory over [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z), [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) continue their successful streak, now securing their third consecutive finish after a runners-up placing at StarSeries and third place at ESL Pro League.

It came as a recovery from their tough start to the tournament, which saw [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) deal [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) a heavy blow with a 13-11 win on Inferno and a 13-1 on Nuke.

[Ashley "ash" Battye](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8975/ash) and company understandably decided against the Nuke pick the second time around and opted for Cache instead.

They got off to a winning start in the series thanks to some clutch play from [Damjan "kyxsan" Stoilkovski](https://www.hltv.org/player/19677/kyxsan), which helped [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) start strong on both sides, before they narrowly escaped a comeback attempt from [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) in the second half with a low buy win in round 24.

A replay of Inferno followed, which was made considerably easier by a dominant CT side spearheaded by [Ali "Wicadia" Haydar Yalçın](https://www.hltv.org/player/21243/wicadia) on the B bombsite.
