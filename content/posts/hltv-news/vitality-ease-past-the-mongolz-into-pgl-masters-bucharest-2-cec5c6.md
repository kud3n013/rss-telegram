[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) made quick work of [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) on the second day of play at PGL Masters Bucharest, beating the Asian team on Inferno (13-6) and Cache (13-3), to advance to the 2-0 pool in the Swiss group stage.

[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) struck first on their Inferno map pick, and went on to reach 5-0 off the back of a [Usukhbayar "910" Banzragch](https://www.hltv.org/player/21809/910) 1v2 in the fifth round, his third clutch of the tournament, but back-to-back quad-kills from [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) got [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) going.

The French star had multi-kills in four consecutive rounds, including a 1v2 clutch, to keep his team rolling as they put ten consecutive rounds on the board to turn the series on its head, 10-5.

The writing was on the wall, and [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) only tallied one CT round, giving way to a crushing [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) victory, 13-6.

[ZywOo quad from mini pit gets Vitality going](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/NimbleCourteousClipsmom4Head-auPD-o5I70kuDcnr)

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) went into their Cache map pick on a streak, as they had won the map seven times in a row, with [Shahar "flameZ" Shushan](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez) putting up 1.20+ ratings in six of them, while [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) had four 2.00+ ratings in his last nine times playing it.

[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) went up 2-0, but their struggles soon started and were nowhere more apparent than they were in newcomer [Yesuntumur "nin9" Gantulga](https://www.hltv.org/player/15072/nin9), who failed to deal damage in six rounds in a row as [flameZ](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez) ran rampant on the attack.

From 0-2 to 9-3, [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) made their victory painless on the CT side, winning the pistol round off the back of a [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) USP quad-kill — he ended up the map with a 2.10 rating, making it 50% of his Caches in CS2 with 2+ ratings — and the three rounds that followed to make it 13-3.
