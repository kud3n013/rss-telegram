ESL has announced the groups and opening matchups for IEM Beijing, set to be played from November 2-8 in China's capital.

The 16 participating teams have been split into two double-elimination groups of eight, where each group winner will earn a direct ticket to the semis, while second- and third-placed teams will qualify for the quarter-finals.

In Group A, [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) vs. [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) headlines the opening matchups in what will be a repeat of their earlier clash at EPL S24. [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) will face [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) in another exciting match, with [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic) vs. [B8](https://www.hltv.org/team/11241/b8) and [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) vs. [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) rounding out the openers.

Group B will see [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) clash with [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) and recent Thunderpick World Championship runners-up [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) with [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z). [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) have also been seeded in the second group and are set to meet [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) in their opener, with [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) vs. [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) rounding out the first round of the tournament.
