The semi-finals have been set at Stake Pulse Beat III after [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas), [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic), [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) and [100 Thieves](https://www.hltv.org/team/8474/100-thieves) won their respective quarter-finals in Stockholm, Sweden.

Having just traveled back from Malta the night before the playoffs, [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) narrowly avoided punishment for their scheduling headache with a hard-earned victory over [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid).

The North American side rued the missed opportunity, as they gave up a lead in the series and an 8-0 advantage on the second map, Nuke, as well as a 12-10 on the deciding Cache.

[HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic) then made short work of [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) before [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) and [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) clashed in another three-map affair. There, the Brazilian-majority team had [Felipe "insani" Yuji](https://www.hltv.org/player/21037/insani) to thank for a hard-carry performance and recovery from a 0-1 deficit in the series.

The 22-year-old ended the series on a 1.78 rating with his second-best teammate on 1.00, putting on a 36-20 masterclass in an overtime win on Cache before leading the way with another dominant showcase on Mirage.

The quarter-final day came to a close with [100 Thieves](https://www.hltv.org/team/8474/100-thieves) securing a swift 2-0 victory over [Sangal](https://www.hltv.org/team/10333/sangal), continuing their return to form and uptick in the Major race, in which they now sit at a healthy 68.1% chance to qualify.
