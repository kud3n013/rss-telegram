BLAST has unveiled the team list for BLAST Rival Season 2, which is set to take place November 11-15 in Chek Lap Kok, Hong Kong. A strong set of eight teams is set to contest the $1,000,000 tournament, with all of the current top five slated to attend.

Considering the stacked roster of teams this iteration of BLAST Rivals will feature the strongest competition of any Rivals event, as [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality), [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) and [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) all attend having won previous events this season.

Also of note is the presence of [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) amongst the attendees, as the majority-Brazilian side are already facing an incredibly busy calendar and have spoken at length about the difficulties they are enduring as a result of a packed schedule.

The tournament will see the eight teams split into two double-elimination group stage brackets, before a single-elimination BO3 bracket takes over for the playoffs. The final will be BO5.
