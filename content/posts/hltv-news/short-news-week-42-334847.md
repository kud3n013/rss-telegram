[Short: Alter Ego unveil Bloody](https://www.hltv.org/news/45811/short-news-week-42#f1)

[Alter Ego](https://www.hltv.org/team/9958/alter-ego) announced the addition of [Zain "Bloody" Talha](https://www.hltv.org/player/23956/bloody) to their team ahead of DFRAG Invitational in Brisbane, Australia.

The Pakistani player averaged a 0.98 rating in his debut as the team beat [Off Chops](https://www.hltv.org/team/14095/off-chops) 2-0 in Group A's winners' match after a default win over [NEXVOID](https://www.hltv.org/team/13667/nexvoid) in the first round.

[Bloody](https://www.hltv.org/player/23956/bloody) is replacing [Hansel "BnTeT" Ferdinand](https://www.hltv.org/player/7131/bntet) in Australia, but it is unclear whether the Indonesian star is out of the team permanently.
