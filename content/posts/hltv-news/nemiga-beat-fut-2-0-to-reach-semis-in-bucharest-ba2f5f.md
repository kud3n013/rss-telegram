[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) are through to the PGL Masters Bucharest semi-finals after taking out [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) 2-0 on Nuke (13-11) and Dust2 (13-9). The [Aulon "Krabeni" Fazlija](https://www.hltv.org/player/22203/krabeni)-led squad had cleared the group stage cleanly, but failed to bring their A game against the [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) underdogs, who came through the group stage after winning in the final day of deciders in the 2-2 pool.

The series wasn't an easy one for [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) early on, as a pistol round win was nullified by [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) in the second-round forcebuy, and a disjointed Nuke T-side put them in a 3-9 hole at the half. [Nikita "robo" Dushin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22864/robo) had just one kill in the first half, and [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga)'s map win probability was down to 4%, but they slowly built their confidence back up after winning the second pistol round.

[Kirill "Xant3r" Kononov](https://www.hltv.org/player/20387/xant3r) came into the map with six straight Nuke maps rated 1.10 or higher, and he didn't disappoint, making it seven in a row with a 1.28, which included a big 1v2 round. But it was [Daniel "syph0" Ageyev](https://www.hltv.org/player/23319/syph0), with [a 1v2 clutch of his own](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/VainGorgeousNigiriRitzMitz-Pezb5NbIaSBON7MS) and a 2.38 CT-side rating, as well as +14.76% CT-side swing, who led the way for [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) to win it in the final round of regulation to secure their pick.

[Xant3r 1v2 down in forcebuy brings Nemiga closer](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/ColdSwissPicklesBatChest-c0m0FxDQu1g8WGw2)

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) then leapt ahead on the CT side of Dust2 by winning their third pistol round in comic fashion — although it could have turned tragic had [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) managed to find the final kill in the 1v3 — and took an early 4-1 lead after five rounds. From there, [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) took over to secure six at the half, with [Xant3r](https://www.hltv.org/player/20387/xant3r) not dealing any damage in four consecutive rounds.

[robo dance gives SunPayus free kill in 1v3 clutch](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/InquisitiveEphemeralCroquetteAllenHuhu-A2YBuz7Foe2TiIOH)

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) made it four for four in pistol rounds after the switch, a big leg up in an otherwise close series, and regained the lead 9-6 before [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) made a push of their own to tie it back up, 9-9. That would be all [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) could muster, however. [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) settled on the attack and won out, 13-9, against a CT side scrambling for solutions that were just out of reach to secure their passage to the next round of the playoffs.
