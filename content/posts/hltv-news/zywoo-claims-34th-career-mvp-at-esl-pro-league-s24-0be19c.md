[Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) has claimed the HLTV x 1xBET Most Valuable Player award at ESL Pro League Season 24 after leading [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s charge to their second consecutive trophy.

The French sniper averaged a tournament-high 1.43 rating across the event and topped the board in all three of [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s playoff games against [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) (1.66), [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) (1.44), and [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) (1.65).

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s star also placed first on the leaderboards in terms of Kills per round (0.86) and Round swing (+4.33%), and placed second in Damage per round (85.0) and KAST (79.2%).

The award is [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo)'s sixth of the year and extends his all-time overall record to 34 medals.
