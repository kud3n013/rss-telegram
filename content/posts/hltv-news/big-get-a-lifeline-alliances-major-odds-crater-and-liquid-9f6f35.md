The second day of 1win Private Club Season 2 came to an end after [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) outlasted [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) in a marathon three-map series in the upper bracket, completing a reverse-sweep in overtime on Inferno (16-14) after the teams traded wins on Cache (10-13) and Ancient (13-10).

[fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) will lament letting a big chance to gain ground in the Major race slip as a win would have nearly doubled their odds of qualifying (48.3%) on [HLTV's invite simulation](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302). Instead, the Ukrainian team now sits at a 21.7% chance, but still has chances to claw back in the lower bracket in Belgrade and at Stake Ranked Episode 5.

The other three series all ended 2-0, with [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big) emerging from the day as the biggest winner after an important win over [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz). [Benjamin "blameF" Bremer](https://www.hltv.org/player/15165/blamef)'s troops had slid down the VRS after early exits at several LANs and sat firmly outside the invite bubble on our prediction page, but went from an 18% chance to qualify to 37% after the win and will have a chance to increase that further against [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) on day three.

[Alliance](https://www.hltv.org/team/12474/alliance)'s Major dreams are all but over after the team bowed out of the event in last place following losses to [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) and [PRIVATE](https://www.hltv.org/team/14030/private), dropping their already miserable odds from 10% to below 1% as they recorded their fourth series loss in a row. A trophy lift at RLG Tesla Cup is [Alliance](https://www.hltv.org/team/12474/alliance)'s only chance to salvage their hopes, but even that would leave them outside the invite bubble unless several other teams stumble.

[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) are in no danger of losing their Major invite, but will have a lot to think about after a miserable outing in Belgrade that saw them fail to win a map against [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) and [magic](https://www.hltv.org/team/13592/magic), including letting a 10-3 lead slip on Dust2 in the latter series to be eliminated. [Johnny "JT" Theodosiou](https://www.hltv.org/player/11630/jt)'s side have precious little time to fix their mistakes with an opening match against [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) at PGL Masters Bucharest looming on October 24.
