Reports are surfacing showing [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s V.Hive headquarters in Paris, France being vandalized, as disorder broke out amid demonstrations against education standards and the cost of living.

Posts to social media show [gaming chairs being thrown](https://x.com/Azgarsg/status/2111499926408470658) atop a fire and smashed windows at the team's headquarters, and suggest the team's [StarLadder StarSeries trophy was stolen](https://x.com/NartOutHere/status/2111531267422314656). [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s CEO took to X to address the situation, saying the vandalism has left him with "a lot of sorrow" whilst also defending the rights of protesters.

"The right to demonstrate is a fundamental freedom that I will defend all my life," he began. "Thank you to those who mobilize for their convictions. But looting and destruction should never have a place in these fights."

The manager of the office also [posted to social media](https://x.com/Aerzia_/status/2111521123183550828), saying "there are no injuries" as the location was closed to both the public and staff at the time of the incident.

The vandalism occurred against a backdrop of wider civil unrest in France, which has seen students protesting in recent weeks over staff shortages and dilapidated facilities in the country's lycées, which are schools attended by 15 to 18-year-olds.
