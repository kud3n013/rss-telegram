The first day of the playoffs of DraculaN Season 7 is done and dusted, with 16 teams finding themselves whittled down to 12 as several important results were recorded in the race for a spot at PGL Major Singapore.

[FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) found themselves on the right side of the equation with a victory, [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) took a hit by dropping to the lower bracket but stayed alive, whilst [Luminosity](https://www.hltv.org/team/6290/luminosity) suffered elimination and face a tall task to make it to Singapore.

The highest-ranked team in attendance, [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze), kicked off their playoff journey with a hard-fought win over [ASTRAL](https://www.hltv.org/team/13333/astral), needing a big performance from [Jason "JBOEN" Boe Nielsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/22866/jboen) in an overtime Cache decider to get the job done.

If [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) can keep their run going until at least a top-four finish, and they make the final three at RLG Tesla Cup, the European combine will be all but guaranteed a Major berth.

[Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) meanwhile suffered an upset loss at the hands of [BASEMENT BOYS](https://www.hltv.org/team/13181/basement-boys) to kick off their playoff campaign, but bounced back in their next game against [INFINITE](https://www.hltv.org/team/10823/infinite) to continue their journey in the lower bracket.

For [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy), a deep run in both DraculaN and RLG Tesla Cup is likely necessary to bag a Major spot, so there is still a lot of work left for the Frenchmen to do if they want to book a ticket for Singapore.

[Luminosity](https://www.hltv.org/team/6290/luminosity) were the notable casualty of the first day of DraculaN's playoffs, as they found themselves shocked by [Nexus](https://www.hltv.org/team/7187/nexus) only to be punished by a tough lower bracket draw against [BBL](https://www.hltv.org/team/13996/bbl).

Whilst [Luminosity](https://www.hltv.org/team/6290/luminosity) are not dead and buried just yet, they are now in need of a trophy-winning campaign at RLG Tesla Cup to have any realistic chance, where they face stiff competition from the likes of [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze), [B8](https://www.hltv.org/team/11241/b8), [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) and [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy).

The final notable result of the day saw [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame) taking strides towards a spot in Stage 2 of the Singapore Major, grabbing a clean 2-0 victory over a solid [BBL](https://www.hltv.org/team/13996/bbl) side. [Oleksandr "s1mple" Kostyliev](https://www.hltv.org/player/7998/s1mple) and company will need to hope that the likes of [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) and [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) slip up, and keep on winning themselves, but they have taken another step in the right direction.
