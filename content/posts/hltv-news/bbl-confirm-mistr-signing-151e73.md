[BBL](https://www.hltv.org/team/13996/bbl) have announced that they have completed the signing of former [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) member [Mikkel "MistR" Thomsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/21199/mistr), replacing [Jonas "Boye" Boye Østergaard](https://www.hltv.org/player/21574/boye), who has been moved to the bench.

[MistR](https://www.hltv.org/player/21199/mistr)'s future had been up in the air since he and [Casper "Cabbi" Jensen](https://www.hltv.org/player/16723/cabbi) were benched by [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) in favor of Australian duo [Tynan "TjP" Purtell](https://www.hltv.org/player/20527/tjp) and [Jordan "Hatz" Bajic](https://www.hltv.org/player/11162/hatz) as the organization opted to switch to the Asian VRS region.

The 22-year-old then announced that he had mutually parted ways with [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi), thanking the organization for allowing him "to step away from this crazy situation."

His signing marks the first change to [BBL](https://www.hltv.org/team/13996/bbl)'s roster since the Turkish organization entered Counter-Strike with the acquisition of the [BBL](https://www.hltv.org/team/13996/bbl) roster.

"His impact was noticeable from the very first practice sessions, bringing a new level of energy and intensity to the team," team manager Derya Üçpınar told HLTV.

"We believe that overcoming the challenges he has faced will only make him stronger, and we’re excited to support him as he takes this next step with BBL.

"We’re building this project with long-term ambitions, and we’re confident that MistR will be an important part of what we aim to achieve."

[BBL](https://www.hltv.org/team/13996/bbl) are currently ranked 38th in the global VRS following a semi-final run at ROG JOURNEY Autumn, where they notably beat [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame) in the playoffs. Stake Pulse Beat III and DraculaN Season 7 will offer them two more chances to pick up Valve points before the November 2 invite cut-off for the Singapore Major.
