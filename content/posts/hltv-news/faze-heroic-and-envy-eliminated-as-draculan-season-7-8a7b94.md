[SINNERS](https://www.hltv.org/team/10577/sinners), [Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire), [JiJieHao](https://www.hltv.org/team/10245/jijiehao), and [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame) are the only teams left standing at DraculaN Season 7 after a few grueling days of best-of-threes, with Wednesday's final games ending close to 02:00 local time.

[FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze), [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic), [BASEMENT BOYS](https://www.hltv.org/team/13181/basement-boys) and [Nexus](https://www.hltv.org/team/7187/nexus) played three back-to-back series as a result of getting dropped to the lower bracket, with [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) left walking a tightrope for their Singapore Major qualification chances with matchups against [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) and [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic).

[Jason "JBOEN" Boe Nielsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/22866/jboen) and [David "frozen" Čerňanský](https://www.hltv.org/player/9960/frozen) rose to the occasion to net [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) wins in both series, requiring overtime to clinch the decider against [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) and all three maps to close out against [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic). Losses in either of those games would have left [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) on the bubble for a Major invite, but instead the team secured valuable points that have given them some wiggle room.

[Russel "Twistzz" Van Dulken](https://www.hltv.org/player/10394/twistzz)'s men missed a chance to further cement their place on Thursday after suffering elimination at the hands of [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame), who have clinched their own Major invite through Asia. [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) will now turn their attention to RLG Tesla Cup, where a top-three finish would likely be enough to secure their Major berth.

The upper bracket saw [SINNERS](https://www.hltv.org/team/10577/sinners) follow up their victory over [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic) by beating [JiJieHao](https://www.hltv.org/team/10245/jijiehao) to reach the upper final, while [Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire) beat [BASEMENT BOYS](https://www.hltv.org/team/13181/basement-boys) and [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame) to cling on to an extremely slim chance of qualifying for the Major.

[Nexus](https://www.hltv.org/team/7187/nexus) eliminated both [SAW](https://www.hltv.org/team/10567/saw) and [BASEMENT BOYS](https://www.hltv.org/team/13181/basement-boys) on Wednesday, but had their own run put to an end by [JiJieHao](https://www.hltv.org/team/10245/jijiehao), who is now taking on [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame) in the lower bracket final.
