[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) booked the last spot in the Thunderpick World Championship's four-team playoff bracket with a hard-fought 2-1 victory over [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy).

The Russian side overturned a 12-7 deficit to win their pick of Nuke in overtime, almost pulled off a similar comeback on Dust2, then ran out 13-11 winners on Mirage in a lively decider map.

"It was a crazy game," [Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) told HLTV after the match. "I think the one bad thing in this series was our T side on Dust2. It was stressful. We were doing something wrong.

"But it's new errors to fix for the next matches. But it was still a crazy comeback on Nuke and an overtime, so crazy game.

"I think everyone is happy right now because we made the playoffs."

The win puts [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) on a collision course with [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) in the semi-finals. The last time the two teams met was in Stage 3 of the IEM Cologne Major — where [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) had a 1.86-rated performance as the Russians ran out 2-0 winners with [Timur "FL4MUS" Marev](https://www.hltv.org/player/20994/fl4mus) as a stand-in.

"Last season we had a lot of stand-ins, and we went to the playoffs in Atlanta and the Major as well," [Boombl4](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) said. "It was a [good] experience for some players like Magnojez, d1Ledez and S1ren.

"Right now we have a full lineup. It [the match against Falcons] should be a great experience, and maybe we can make it to the final."

The back-to-back group victories in Malta, after an opening-day defeat to [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), have propelled [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) to the top 10 in the global VRS — a massive boost before the final leg of the season, with stops in Bucharest and Beijing [before the Singapore Major](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302).

"This tournament we're playing better, and I feel the progress," [Boombl4](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) said. "The first three tournaments of the season were not the best for us. We were feeling a lot of stress.

"Right now, I can say my guys are enjoying the game. We're showing something, you know?"

He added: "At the start of the season, we were discussing with the team, and the main goal of our coach this season was to stay in the top-15, top-16, to have LAN invites and get some experience on LAN.

"Right now we are top-10, and we have Beijing and Bucharest as well, and, I hope, Singapore Major. I hope we can make two playoff runs minimum. Who knows what will happen in this tournament as well?"
