## Teams and format

[Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons), [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) and [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) headline the eight-team field at Thunderpick World Championship 2026, joined by [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom), [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z), [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) and [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas).

[Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) were brought in to replace [Virtus.pro](https://www.hltv.org/team/5378/virtuspro), whose withdrawal followed visa issues affecting [Aleksandr "AquaRS" Kovalev](https://www.hltv.org/player/24950/aquars) and health problems that forced [Dias "F0R3VER" Kutubay](https://www.hltv.org/player/24292/f0r3ver) to temporarily step away from the lineup.

Meanwhile, [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) will welcome [Andrei "arT" Piovezan](https://www.hltv.org/player/12521/art) back after the in-game leader missed ESL Pro League Season 24 while recovering from meningitis. The Brazilian side played with [Pedro "bobz" Rocha](https://www.hltv.org/player/17215/bobz) as a stand-in in Poland, where they exited in the group stage.

## Schedule and opening matchups

All eight teams will begin their campaigns on Wednesday, October 14. The opening day will start with [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) facing [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), followed by [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) taking on [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas). For group B, [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) vs. [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) vs. [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) will complete the day's action.
