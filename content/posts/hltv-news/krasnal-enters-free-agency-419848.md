[ENCE](https://www.hltv.org/team/4869/ence) have announced a mutual agreement with [Szymon "kRaSnaL" Mrozek](https://www.hltv.org/player/20282/krasnal) to terminate the Pole's contract with the organization.

[kRaSnaL](https://www.hltv.org/player/20282/krasnal) was benched by [ENCE](https://www.hltv.org/team/4869/ence) in March when the Finnish organization announced its pivot to the domestic scene, sidelining its entire international squad in the process.

Since his benching, [kRaSnaL](https://www.hltv.org/player/20282/krasnal) had a stand-in stint with [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) at Logitech G Play Connect but otherwise remained mostly inactive.

"The past year was tough for me as a player due to lack of team stability, but I think I have matured even more, and I’m ready to see what the future holds," the Pole wrote on X, adding that [he is on the lookout for a new project.](https://x.com/kRaSNaLkox/status/2112213901429420037)
