[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) are through to the semi-final stage at PGL Masters Bucharest following a 2-1 victory over [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere), recovering from a one-map deficit to beat [Aleksi "Aleksib" Virolainen](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib)'s troops a second time in this tournament.

NAVI came out with fire in their bellies and picked up a dominant win on Cache, snapping [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s eight-game win streak on the map. They were left to rue some costly mistakes on Mirage before turning in a flat showing in the Dust2 decider.

[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) went for a Cache pick, a surprise decision after first-banning the map in the previous series against [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality), just two days earlier. After a slow start to their T side, NAVI dictated the tempo with a prolific offense and went on a dominant 7-1 run, with [Mihai "iM" Ivan](https://www.hltv.org/player/14759/im) leading the charge with a 1.89 rating and +7.67% round swing.

[Aleksib](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib)'s troops carried that momentum into the second half, winning six rounds without response to close out the map and hand [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) his second-lowest Cache rating in CS2.

NAVI picked up where they left off, taking an early lead on Mirage behind a triple kill from [Valeriy "b1t" Vakhovskiy](https://www.hltv.org/player/18987/b1t) in the pistol round. [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)’s offense looked far livelier this time, winning six rounds in the half, though the scoreline would have been quite different without key clutches from [Justinas "jL" Lekavicius](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) and [William "mezii" Merriman](https://www.hltv.org/player/18462/mezii).

And then [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) took over the game. A trio of USP frags marked the start of a dominant half from the French star, who posted a 2.61 rating and a +15.9% round swing on the CT side to lead [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) to victory. For a short spell, the game appeared to be tilting in NAVI’s favor, but a missed smoke cost them an anti-eco round and killed off any momentum they had gained.

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) refused to let up and cruised to an 8-4 lead on the CT side of Dust2 after a one-sided half. [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) responded with a flawless pistol, but their comeback hopes were immediately crushed as [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) hit back thanks to a [ZywOo](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) Scout triple kill, and then ran away with the game.
