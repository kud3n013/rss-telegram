[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) have secured the third playoffs spot at PGL Masters Bucharest after eking out a narrow series against [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) for their third win in the Swiss group stage.

[Jimi "Jimpphat" Salo](https://www.hltv.org/player/18850/jimpphat) was crucial to success, coming up clutch in several key moments and ending the series on a server-high 1.28 rating and +4.96% round swing.

His patience allowed him to catch [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) off-guard in a 1v2 clutch on the opening Anubis, and he went on to rack up 14 kills and +20% swing over the second half to spearhead [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora)'s 13-11 win.

[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) then pulled off a superb comeback from down 4-10 on Inferno with an indomitable defense, but could not complete the job despite finding themselves up 10-8 on the Cache decider.

[Jimpphat](https://www.hltv.org/player/18850/jimpphat) saved [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) in two consecutive rounds with more patient play in a 1v2 situation and great AWPing when down in numbers in defense of B, before adding another big double-kill anchoring the same bombsite on the way to a 13-10 win.

Off the back of the victory, [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) continue their recent uptick in form with their fourth consecutive playoffs qualification, after the team finished runers-up at StarLadder StarSeries Fall and in third place at ESL Pro League S24 and Thunderpick World Championship.
