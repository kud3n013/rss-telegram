[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) began life with [Kamil "siuhy" Szkaradek](https://www.hltv.org/player/16820/siuhy) and [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) with victories over [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) and [HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu) to progress to the Stake Pulse Beat III playoffs.

The international squad recently went through a shock rebuild just one month before the Major, benching IGL [Rasmus "HooXi" Nielsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/10096/hooxi) and [Jakob "jabbi" Nygaard](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi) due to the duo entering the final months of their contracts in order to play the Major with their new recruits.

In Stockholm, [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) began their campaign by defeating [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi), who have also gone through a controversial change in recent times, which meant they faced [HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu) in the winners' game.

[Gytis "ryu" Glušauskas](https://www.hltv.org/player/21217/ryu), who also topped the board in the game against [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) (1.41 rating), was once again the star of the show and was his team's highest-rated player (1.55), putting him second overall in the tournament charts.

[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) will now have a day off before the playoffs get underway on Friday, while a potentially Major-ending clash between [HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu) and [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) is played next in Group C.
