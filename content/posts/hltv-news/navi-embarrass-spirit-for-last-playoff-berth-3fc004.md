[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) have clinched the final spot in the playoffs of PGL Masters Bucharest after beating [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) in a three-map series, picking up landslide wins on Cache (13-2) and Anubis (13-3) on either side of a narrow loss on Dust2 (10-13).

It was only the third win over [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) in fifteen matches for [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere), whose last win over [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk)' side dates back to the group stage of IEM Katowice 2025, and came emphatically as [Drin "makazze" Shaqiri](https://www.hltv.org/player/22673/makazze) and [Ihor "w0nderful" Zhdanov](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) came alive to power their team to victory.

The win also puts [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) through to the playoffs for the first time since their title-winning campaign at IEM Atlanta, and comes as a big confidence boost after the team endured a miserable start to the Fall season.

[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere)'s mind games began in the veto, surprising [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) with a Cache pick that paid dividends. [makazze](https://www.hltv.org/player/22673/makazze) was in terrifying form as he led a stalwart defense, while [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) looked lost on the T side with mistake after mistake leaving them destitute.

One of those errors came early in the half as [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) squandered a 5v3 by walking into a stacked B site despite knowing A was clear, and that became the tone-setter for a miserable, two-round offensive half. Three lost clutches only added further pain to that scoreline, including a final 1v2 by [makazze](https://www.hltv.org/player/22673/makazze) in round 12 that gave [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) all the momentum they needed to close out after the swap.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s fortunes didn't improve at the start of Dust2, a pistol win going to waste with [Valeriy "b1t" Vakhovskiy](https://www.hltv.org/player/18987/b1t)'s Five-SeveN striking back to win [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) the force. The Ukrainian rifler added further multi-kills alongside [w0nderful](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) and [makazze](https://www.hltv.org/player/22673/makazze) as [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) started running away with the lead again, and [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) had to fight tooth and nail to just scrape four rounds by the break.

A lost pistol in the second half could have spelled [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s doom, but they answered back with their own force-buy in what became a crucial turning point in the map. A double AWP setup between [Dmitry "sh1ro" Sokolov](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) and [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) helped close the gap early on before the latter gave it up for an AK, and [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) could find no avenue in with [Boris "magixx" Vorobiev](https://www.hltv.org/player/18317/magixx) shutting down Middle and [Andrey "tN1R" Tatarinovich](https://www.hltv.org/player/19808/tn1r) and [sh1ro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) finding impact everywhere else.

Anubis marked a return to form for [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) and a mirror of [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s woeful offense on Cache, with [Mihai "iM" Ivan](https://www.hltv.org/player/14759/im) and [makazze](https://www.hltv.org/player/22673/makazze) disrupting [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s defaults in the early gun rounds and [w0nderful](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) running rampant with his AWP.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) eventually broke through by exploiting a gap in Middle repeatedly to win three in a row, but even those came by way of two clutches from [sh1ro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) and [Myroslav "zont1x" Plakhotia](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x) — the latter of which put [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s lack of communication on display as [zont1x](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x) left [sh1ro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) out to dry in a 2v2.

[donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) was nowhere to be seen, struggling to find any impact alongside the rest of his team and finishing with a 0.41 rating — the fifth-lowest-rated LAN map of his career. Any hopes of a comeback were crushed near the end of the half as [w0nderful](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) channeled his inner [Oleksandr "s1mple" Kostyliev](https://www.hltv.org/player/7998/s1mple) with an absurd and flashy AWP 4k on 1HP to send [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) into a spiral, and [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) wasted no time in closing out their lead after the swap.
