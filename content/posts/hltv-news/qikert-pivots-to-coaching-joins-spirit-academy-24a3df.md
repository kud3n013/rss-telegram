[Aleksei "Qikert" Golubev](https://www.hltv.org/coach/13239/qikert) has joined [Spirit Academy](https://www.hltv.org/team/7900/spirit-academy) as the team's new coach, marking the end of a playing career that spanned nearly a decade and saw the Kazakh rifler become a Major champion with [Outsiders](https://www.hltv.org/team/11595/outsiders) at IEM Rio Major 2022.

The 27-year-old also reached the final of StarLadder Major Berlin with [AVANGAR](https://www.hltv.org/team/8120/avangar) and attended seven Majors during his career, with his latest appearance coming at StarLadder Budapest Major with [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr).

[Qikert](https://www.hltv.org/coach/13239/qikert) left [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) at the start of the year and later joined [1win](https://www.hltv.org/team/10621/1win), with whom he won MPKBK CIS LAN Season 4 to add a 12th piece of silverware to his cabinet alongside more notable trophies like BLAST Pro Series Moscow 2019, ESL Challenger Rotterdam and ESL Challenger Katowice 2023.

The Kazakh brings a wealth of experience to [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s academy roster, which has proven to be a consistent pipeline of talent over the years with rosters that saw to the development of players like [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk), [Myroslav "zont1x" Plakhotia](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x), [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez), [Timur "FL4MUS" Marev](https://www.hltv.org/player/20994/fl4mus) and [Pavel "S1ren" Ogloblin](https://www.hltv.org/player/18506/s1ren).

[Spirit Academy](https://www.hltv.org/team/7900/spirit-academy)'s previous skipper, [Vladislav "Flash\_1" Bykov](https://www.hltv.org/player/12241/flash-1), remains with the organization and will coach Spirit Academy White.
