[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) have secured a spot in the 2-1 pool at PGL Masters Bucharest after edging past [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in a three-map series (13-7 Nuke, 8-13 Ancient, 13-11 Mirage).

[Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) will lament having a miserable series, ending at the bottom of the board with a 0.76 rating as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) failed to close out an 11-9 lead on the Mirage decider to end up staring down an elimination match heading into day four.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) continued their recovery run after losing their opener to [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom), and now have two chances to make the playoffs with [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) looking back in form. The Russian phenom ended the series atop the board with a 1.46 rating, and though he posted 11 kills more than his closest teammate on the Mirage decider, [Myroslav "zont1x" Plakhotia](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x) and [Dmitry "sh1ro" Sokolov](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) also deserve credit for helping complete the comeback when the map reached its dying moments.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) never looked troubled on Nuke between [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) getting a few openers, [sh1ro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) thwarting [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) Outside, and [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) having a noticeably quiet map. A Dualies quad-kill by [sh1ro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) set the tone for the map as [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) romped ahead, 7-3, with a 4k from [Danil "molodoy" Golubenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/24144/molodoy) to recover a 3v5 offering [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) a small glimmer of hope near the end of the half.

A pistol and conversion then let [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) tie the game at 7-7, but [sh1ro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) and [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) continued to steamroll and ran over the defenses for a six-round run to win the map.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) couldn't keep up the same form on Ancient despite [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk) finding openers, with [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) taking control on the defense. One of [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s few rounds came by way of a triple spray through a smoke by [zont1x](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x), but [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)'s prowess in the mid-round reigned supreme for a 9-3 lead at the break.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) started to claw back from a pistol win, losing only the first gun round in a 5-1 run to bring the deficit to just two rounds. But entries from [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) on B stemmed the bleed and ensured [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) locked in the victory, forcing a Mirage decider.

[KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) had another map to forget on the decider, with [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) again assuming control with a 5-0 start. A pivot to attacking B paid dividends for [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) with [Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) finding a few impactful multi-kills, which was enough to close the gap to 7-5 at the break.

A pistol win and excellent teamplay on the defense saw [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) take control after the swap. [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) struggled in the mid-rounds as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) took the lead, 11-9, but [zont1x](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x) and [sh1ro](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) executed a game plan to exploit B to perfection to lead [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) to a four-round spree to close out the series.
