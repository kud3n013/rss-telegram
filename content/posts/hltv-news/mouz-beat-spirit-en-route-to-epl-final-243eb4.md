[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) became the second grand finalists of ESL Pro League Season 24 after defeating [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) in a close-fought three-mapper (Dust2 13-7, Mirage 9-13, Nuke 13-11).

The result marks [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz)'s third grand final of the season, with [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) standing between them and the trophy, while [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) are set to meet [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) in the third-place decider.

"This win will give us a lot of confidence," [Ádám "torzsi" Torzsás](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi) said in a post-match interview. "We are a new team, so I don't think it's going to be the same [as in 2025] against Vitality."

The series began on [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s pick of Dust2, where the Russian squad was found asleep at the wheel in the opening rounds. While they won and converted the pistol, [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) found themselves 2-8 down before finally clicking into gear and won five in a row.

[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) then won a crucial halfbuy after [Danil "donk" Kryshkovets](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk)'s overpeek into Lower and took that momentum all the way to the bank, with [torzsi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi) crowning a fantastic performance with a post-plant 3k.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) showed a different face on Mirage, however, looking composed and, more importantly, buoyed by an online [donk](https://www.hltv.org/player/21167/donk). The Russian phenom topped the board for his team with 19 kills on the map, while also putting the final nail in the [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) coffin with a 1vs2.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) kept up their flawless record in pistol rounds at the start of Nuke, but couldn't convert their success into a big lead. [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), led by [Oldřich "PR" Nový](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr), quickly seized the lead and edged out a 7-5 half thanks to 17 frags by the Czech rifler.

[Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) came back into the game after a pair of clutches by [Andrey "tN1R" Tatarinovich](https://www.hltv.org/player/19808/tn1r) and [Myroslav "zont1x" Plakhotia](https://www.hltv.org/player/20423/zont1x), but [PR](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr)'s unrelenting fragging continued. The 19-year-old ended the map on 27 kills, with [Lotan "Spinx" Giladi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18221/spinx) recovering from his fumbled 1vs1 with a crucial double late on, which helped [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) edge out a 13-11 victory.
