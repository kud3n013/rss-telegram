The playoffs bracket has been set at PGL Masters Bucharest after [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) secured the final spot from the Swiss group stage with a dominant win over [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit).

The single elimination bracket is headlined by a first-time meeting between [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) and [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora)'s new squads, which have established themselves in the world's top five following a string of deep runs with the freshened lineups this season.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) find themselves on that side of the bracket as well, set for a rematch after their five-map grand final affair at Thunderpick World Championship.

On the other side is the surprise of the tournament, [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga), taking on [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut), whom [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) will be cheering on with hopes of freeing up [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) for the PGL SIngapore Major.

Another rematch awaits between [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) and [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere), who will be looking for revenge after coming on the wrong side of a lopsided affair in the group stage of the tournament.
