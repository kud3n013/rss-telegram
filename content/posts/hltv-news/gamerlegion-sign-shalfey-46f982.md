[GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) have announced the signing of [Aleksandr "shalfey" Marenov](https://www.hltv.org/player/17011/shalfey), who is set to take over in-game leadership of the team from [Janusz "Snax" Pogorzelski](https://www.hltv.org/player/2553/snax) after the Pole got benched earlier on Wednesday.

[shalfey](https://www.hltv.org/player/17011/shalfey) joins the team after spending the last two months playing for [ex-RUSTEC](https://www.hltv.org/team/13901/ex-rustec) in several online competitions. The 24-year-old averaged a 1.06 rating across multiple stints in different domestic rosters during 2026, but is now tasked with leading his first international project.

The news comes shortly after [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) bombed out of the PGL Bucharest 2027 Closed Qualifier in last place, putting them [almost out of the Singapore Major race](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302#team-9928).

[shalfey](https://www.hltv.org/player/17011/shalfey) will make his [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) debut at the RLG Tesla Cup 2026, where the international squad are set to face [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) in the first round.
