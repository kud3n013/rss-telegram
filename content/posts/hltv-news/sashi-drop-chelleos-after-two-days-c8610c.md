[Michael "chelleos" Hawkins](https://www.hltv.org/player/12877/chelleos) has announced that he is no longer playing for [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) after just two days on the Danish organization's roster.

The 26-year-old, formerly of [Rooster](https://www.hltv.org/team/9881/rooster), joined the team on Thursday, bringing the Oceanic contingent to three players following the signing of Australian pair [Tynan "TjP" Purtell](https://www.hltv.org/player/20527/tjp) and [Jordan "Hatz" Bajic](https://www.hltv.org/player/11162/hatz).

"Sashi didn't work out for me," [chelleos](https://www.hltv.org/player/12877/chelleos) wrote on X, adding that he didn't even sign a contract with the Danish organization.

[Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) have been practicing with [Frederik "acoR" Gyldstrand](https://www.hltv.org/player/10330/acor), who was benched to make way for [chelleos](https://www.hltv.org/player/12877/chelleos)' arrival, a source told HLTV.

[Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) still have an Asian core as [Anton "Anlelele" Huynh](https://www.hltv.org/player/20417/anlelele) is now playing under a Vietnamese flag, but they will need three months for the region change to come into effect, unless they are granted an exception by Valve.

[TjP](https://www.hltv.org/player/20527/tjp)'s charges are slated to compete at Stake Pulse Beat III in Stockholm next week. They will also attend DraculaN Season 7 and have signed up for two overlapping LANs: HyperX Retake Season 13 and NSTLGA Texas Open.
