[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga)’s remarkable Bucharest run continues with a 2-0 upset over [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) in the PGL Masters semi-finals with a 13-8 on Cache and a 13-11 on Dust2. [Nikita "robo" Dushin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22864/robo) led the series with 46 kills, 101 ADR, and a 1.52 rating.

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga)'s tournament run is also visible in the VRS ranking, as the live preview shows them being awarded 56 points for beating [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality), going from 1,589 points and 12th globally to 1,645 and 10th. That represents a gain of 153 points and 13 places since their opening match.

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) wasted little time on Cache, racing into a 7-1 lead. [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) won the remaining four rounds of the half to give themselves a way back, but their recovery stalled after the switch. [robo](https://www.hltv.org/player/22864/robo) led the way with 24 kills as his team closed out their pick 13-8, while [William "mezii" Merriman](https://www.hltv.org/player/18462/mezii), who is acting IGL in [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex)'s absence, struggled with just three frags and 49.6 ADR.

[Clip link](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/IgnorantResourcefulPhoneCmonBruh-TPSz5uEVm_yg4ANn)

The Englishman found his footing again on Dust2, producing a quad kill in the final round of the half to help his team take an 8-4 lead, a response which added to [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s hope of forcing a decider.

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) gradually erased the deficit after the break, however, drawing level at 10-10. [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) edged ahead once more, but that was their final round as [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) won three straight to complete the sweep, 13-11. [mezii](https://www.hltv.org/player/18462/mezii) finished with 22 kills and a map-leading 1.60 rating, but could not force Mirage, while the IGL on the other side of the server, [robo](https://www.hltv.org/player/22864/robo), added another 22 frags to secure [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga)’s place in the final.

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga)'s dark horse run continues as they now contend to win it all in Bucharest, and they will face the winner of [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) vs. [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in the grand final.
