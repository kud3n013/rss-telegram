[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) continued their encouraging PGL Masters Bucharest campaign with a 2-0 win over [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) (Ancient 13-10, Inferno 13-8) to qualify for the 2-1 pool.

[Johnny "JT" Theodosiou](https://www.hltv.org/player/11630/jt)'s troops defeated [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) in their tournament opener, and while they lost the 1-0 game against [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut), a confident victory against [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) will serve as a nice boost for a team that had a rough time in their recent open-LAN marathon.

Meanwhile, [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) are now one loss away from elimination and will need two straight victories to make the playoffs.

The match began on Ancient, where the Brazilian squad got off to a very slow start and found themselves 1-8 down. That deficit proved too much to overcome for the Brazilians despite [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) having some issues closing out the game, with [Georgi "Jorko" Mitev](https://www.hltv.org/player/24203/jorko) leading his squad to a 13-10 victory with a 1.57 rating.

Next was [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid)'s pick of Inferno, where a cagy first half ended 6-6 as neither team was able to exert control. [JT](https://www.hltv.org/player/11630/jt) finished the half on zero frags, but the South African found his fragging boots on the CT side of Banana and led his team's charge towards a comfortable 13-8 victory with 4 multi-kills and a 2.06 rating on the defense.
