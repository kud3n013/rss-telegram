[FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) have defeated [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) 2-0 at PGL Bucharest, securing Cache (13-5) and Dust2 (13-9) to maintain their unbeaten start to the tournament.

The victory puts [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) in the 2-0 pool, giving them three opportunities to secure the win required for a playoff berth. At same time, [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) drop to 1-1 after their opening victory over [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), with both teams awaiting their third-round opponents.

Notably, [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) also need to play five matches with [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) before the November 2 VRS cut-off for him to replace [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) on their Major invitation roster. With the Ukrainian now at [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis), falling short would leave him listed on both teams' invitation rosters, only one of which could accept the invite.

[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) won both pistol rounds on their Cache pick, but struggled to turn those openings into sustained success. [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) responded to the first with six consecutive rounds and built a 9-3 halftime lead, powered by dem0n's 16-3 first-half scoreline.

[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) briefly reduced the deficit to 9-5 after the switch, only for their opponent to take the next four rounds and close out the map. dem0n finished with 20 kills, six deaths, and a 2.15 rating, while [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) won the opening-kill battle 14-4.

Dust2 initially threatened to follow the same pattern as [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) raced into a 6-0 lead. [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) then mounted their strongest resistance of the series, winning 4 rounds to narrow the gap to 10-8. [Georgi "Jorko" Mitev](https://www.hltv.org/player/24203/jorko) contributed a 1v3 clutch on the map, but [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) prevented the comeback from reaching overtime, taking the final two rounds from 11-9 to complete the sweep. [Dmytro "dem0n" Myroshnychenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/20584/dem0n) again led the way with 23 kills and a 1.84 rating.

[dem0n](https://www.hltv.org/player/20584/dem0n) led the series with 43 kills, 19 deaths, and 110.8 ADR, topping the scoreboard on both maps. [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) added a 1.28 rating as [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) recorded their second consecutive sweep following the Spaniard's winning debut against [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo).
