[NIP stay on track with win over Betclic](https://www.hltv.org/news/45705/live-updates-from-the-race-for-pgl-major-singapore#f4)

[Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) can virtually guarantee their spot at the Major with a win in this event, and they are on track after a strong [Martin "stavn" Lund](https://www.hltv.org/player/10994/stavn) performance guided them to an opening win over [Betclic](https://www.hltv.org/team/12916/betclic).

A loss would have dropped [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas)'s odds to 57%, but instead they enjoy a 73% chance of qualification as things stand.

[What's at stake at Pulse Beat III](https://www.hltv.org/news/45705/live-updates-from-the-race-for-pgl-major-singapore#f3)

The first tier two event of the Major run-in is [Stake Pulse Beat III](https://www.hltv.org/events/9223/stake-pulse-beat-iii), which features a hefty $80,000 prizepool and a raft of Major-chasing squads in attendance.

[Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy), [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas), [Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle), [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic), [JiJieHao](https://www.hltv.org/team/10245/jijiehao), [100 Thieves](https://www.hltv.org/team/8474/100-thieves), [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic), [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big), and [HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu) are the teams in attendance who could see their Major hopes significantly bolstered by deep runs and important victories.

[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis), [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr), [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) and [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame) are also in attendance, but all have their Major spots either clinched or virtually certain. Rather than fighting for qualification themselves, they could offer a boost to any team that managed to beat them.

Some of the scenarios facing teams are:

- ![Europe](https://www.hltv.org/img/static/flags/30x20/EU.gif) [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) - First place takes their qualification chances up to 97%, while last place leaves them at 48%.
- ![Ukraine](https://www.hltv.org/img/static/flags/30x20/UA.gif) [Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle) - First place takes their qualification chances up to 99%, while last place leaves them at 26%.
- ![Sweden](https://www.hltv.org/img/static/flags/30x20/SE.gif) [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic) - First place would virtually guarantee their qualification, while last place leaves them at 71%.
- ![France](https://www.hltv.org/img/static/flags/30x20/FR.gif) [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) - First place takes their qualification chances up to 77%, while last place leaves them at 16%.
