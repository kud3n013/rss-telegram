Matchups are set for day three at PGL Masters Bucharest, featuring the first round of playoff-qualifying matches and elimination games.

[G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) have their backs against the wall heading into the third Swiss round after suffering losses to [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) and [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), and will begin their recovery campaign against [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) in the 0-2 pool.

[9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) and [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) will also face off to avoid an early elimination, while [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) and [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) square off in the 2-0 pool for a spot in the playoffs. Extra attention will be on the latter team, who need to play five matches with [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) before the November 2 VRS cut-off for him to replace [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) on their roster.

Advancing undefeated and losing in the quarter-finals would spell doom for [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis), as it would leave the Ukrainian listed on both teams' invitation rosters, only one of which could accept the invite.

[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) arrived in Bucharest facing immense pressure to pick up some LAN wins to secure their Major invite, and look to have done enough after beating [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia). Any final doubts can be assuaged with a win over [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) in the 2-0 pool, but a win isn't guaranteed with [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) currently boasting the second-highest rating in Romania (1.53).

The 1-1 pool is headlined by a match between [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), the former of whom dropped their opener to [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) but bounced back with aplomb against [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo). 1win Private Club Season 2 champions [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) will also look to rebound from a loss to [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) against [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), while [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) will hope to carry their form against [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) into a matchup against [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga).
