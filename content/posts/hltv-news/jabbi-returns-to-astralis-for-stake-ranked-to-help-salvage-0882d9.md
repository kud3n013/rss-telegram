[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) has announced that the team will play out the remainder of its matches at Stake Ranked Episode 5 with [Jakob "jabbi" Nygaard](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi) as a stand-in.

Although the organization's announcement did not clarify who [jabbi](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi) would replace, HLTV confirmed with [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) that he will replace [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry), avoiding the possibility of the Ukrainian playing five matches for the team.

"jabbi is back in the lineup tomorrow for the rest of our Stake Ranked run in Barcelona, coming straight from the airport at very little notice," [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis)' statement reads.

"There is never a dull moment in esports, and jabbi stepping up to help at this pivotal moment shows exactly the kind of professional he is. Nothing but respect."

The swap comes as a solution to [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis)' Major plight after the team opted to replace [Rasmus "HooXi" Nielsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/10096/hooxi) and [jabbi](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi) with [Kamil "siuhy" Szkaradek](https://www.hltv.org/player/16820/siuhy) and [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) on October 13.

The latter player remains listed on [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut)'s VRS roster as the team only played three matches with [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) in a 3-0 run at PGL Masters Bucharest, which could have led to him being listed on both [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) and [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut)'s rosters if [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) go on to lose their quarter-final and [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) played one more match for [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis).

That situation would have put [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis)' Major invite in jeopardy, and possibly [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut)'s as well, as it remains unclear what team would receive an invite if a player is on two eligible rosters.

[jabbi](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi)'s last-minute return to the lineup gives [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) an insurance policy should [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) lose their quarter-final match by avoiding the double roster-listing of [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry).

[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) lost their opener at Stake Ranked to [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic), and will fight for their tournament lives in the lower bracket against [magic](https://www.hltv.org/team/13592/magic) on Wednesday.

[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) will field the following roster for the rest of their run in Barcelona:
