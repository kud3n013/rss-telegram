[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) have sent [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) home from PGL Masters Bucharest with a 2-0 victory (Mirage 13-8, Inferno 13-8).

[Garidmagnai "bLitz" Byambasuren](https://www.hltv.org/player/20194/blitz) was the star of the show for his team, topping the board on Mirage (1.74 map rating) and finishing second-highest on Inferno (1.47) to help the Mongolians stay alive in Bucharest.

[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) are now set for a playoff-qualifying clash in the 2-2 pool, where [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) and [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) are already present, while [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) suffer another early elimination after placing 5th-6th at Thunderpick World Championship.

The match itself was fairly comfortable for [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz), who began Mirage with a 7-5 CT half before dominating proceedings on their offense despite losing a second-round forcebuy to secure a 13-8 win.

Inferno finished with the same scoreline, with [bLitz](https://www.hltv.org/player/20194/blitz)'s men taking over in the final few rounds to turn a 7-8 deficit into a 13-8 win and keep themselves alive in the tournament.
