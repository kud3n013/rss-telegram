[Nikita "robo" Dushin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22864/robo) stopped for a quick chat with HLTV after his team's memorable upset victory over [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality):

**Were you expecting to be able to beat Vitality when you came into this game?**

Not really. Everyone on our team said it's a good result for us already, Vitality too good, so don't worry if you lose.

**Do you think that lack of pressure helped you play so well?**

Yeah, I think so.

**You've not been on Nemiga for that long. Why do you think you've been able to do so well so quickly, both you and as a team?**

I don't know. We just played good as a team. We practiced a lot and everyone started feeling each other. We have good synergy in team. But still, I don't know how do we do it.

**Last question. Grand final tomorrow, do you think you can win it and go all the way?**

Obviously, we still think the teams which are left are really good. So we don't have that much pressure that we must win this final. We just play and enjoy the game.

[We're down to the final four in Bucharest](https://www.hltv.org/news/45859/live-updates-from-pgl-masters-bucharest-semi-finals#f1)

Only four teams are left at PGL Masters in Bucharest: [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality), [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga), [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia). The second-to-last day of play will kick off with the [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex)-less [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) facing off against the final four's big underdogs, [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga).

The second series, featuring [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) and [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), will be the third time these teams are clashing for this month after the European squad cleared the Brazilian-majority side 2-0 twice at ESL Pro League.
