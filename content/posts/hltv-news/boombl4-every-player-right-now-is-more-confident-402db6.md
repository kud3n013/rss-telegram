[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) are a team on the rise, grabbing a second-place finish at Thunderpick World Championship before arriving at PGL Masters Bucharest and taking down [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), a regional rival and top-two squad. "It's crazy to win one of the best teams in the world," [Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) told HLTV. "We are from one region, and we know each other, a lot of friends, and a lot of good memories with some of them."

Despite their recent strides forward, [Boombl4](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) says his team are not putting pressure on themselves to produce results, as they should come if [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) keep putting in the work. "Sometimes you're thinking about winning the tournaments, but everyone is thinking about the focus on progress and how we're playing, because if we progress, we should win the tournaments."

Fresh from a victory over [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) that put [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) at a 2-0 record, [Boombl4](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4) spoke to HLTV about his team's recent results, the reasons for their improvement and his hopes for PGL Major Singapore.

**Congratulations on the win. I think in particular that second map was maybe a little bit closer than some people would have expected. How did you see it?**

Both maps were a little bit strange, because the first map, I think it was a lot of clutches and they could money fuck us in one round, but it didn't happen, and we won every clutch. So it was easy on score but not easy in the game. In the second map, it was a really close match and in some rounds we gave a lot of space for them. But still, a win is a win.

**Obviously you started off yesterday with a win against Spirit. It's a really, really big win for your team. Tell me how you felt about that match.**

It's crazy to win one of the best teams in the world. First we are from one region, and we know each other, a lot of friends, and a lot of good memories with some of them. After the Thunderpick tournament, when we took second place, I think we broke something mental for us, because we feel stress, but not a lot like in previous tournaments. So we have good game tournaments right now. And still, it's this tournament and IEM Beijing after, so it's a lot of matches, a lot of games, and I hope we will progress day by day.

After the Thunderpick tournament, when we took second place, I think we broke something mental for us

[Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4)

**Does it feel good to have so many tournaments and so many matches because you're playing so well? It feels like you can take advantage of how you're progressing right now.**

It's not a lot of time to chill. It's a lot of tournaments and a lot of flights and everything. It's really hard for everyone because we're seeing each other every day, a lot of time to spend together, and everyone is thinking about the families at home and something like this, but it's our job, so it's okay.

**Considering how well your team is playing at the moment, you took second to Thunderpick and you just beat Spirit, you must feel confident against any team in the world right now.**

It depends. Every player right now is more confident individually, so that's why you can close some matches right now. So this is perfect, because it's not only about the team progress, it's about individual experience progress.

So it should happen sometime, and right now it's happening, so it's cool because in the big stages and the playoffs, without the individual confidence, you can't win the matches and the tournaments. So I think everyone is happy, like they're doing some job right now on the server.

I think everyone is happy, like they're doing some job right now on the server

[Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4)

**Yeah. So does that mean looking forwards, you think you could maybe try and win a big event in the near future? Because you came close at Thunderpick and like you say, you're getting that individual confidence now from the team.**

Of course. Sometimes you're thinking about winning the tournaments, but everyone is thinking about the focus on progress and how we're playing, because if we progress, we should win the tournaments.

**Yeah, it will come.**

Yeah, it will come. So this is the main thing to do. Just play CS and try to be better, that's all.

**When you look at your own team at the moment, where do you put yourself, in the rankings as it were?**

I don't know. I don't know, actually. Don't-

Everyone is thinking about the focus on progress and how we're playing, because if we progress, we should win the tournaments

[Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4)

**Top five?**

Don't want to, don't want to. It's because sometimes we can play really bad, and I know because we have some shit practice days when everything's going wrong. So if we're focused and we have the good tournaments, we can be a good opponent for every team. That's all.

**Okay. And looking forwards to IEM Beijing and the Major, when you go into these tournaments, do you set goals for yourself as a team? Like, "We want to make playoffs. We want to get this position." Or do you just turn up and play and try your best?**

I think especially only maybe on the Major, because every player wants to try to go up in the Major, go playoffs and do some crazy things and feel this energy. But for Beijing, I think for this tournament and Thunderpick, we just want to feel the tournaments and try to progress. That's it.
