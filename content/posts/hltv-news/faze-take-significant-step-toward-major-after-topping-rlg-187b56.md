[FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) have topped Group A of RLG Tesla Cup to boost their Singapore Major chances to 98% as per [HLTV's invite prediction.](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/major/Europe)

[Russel "Twistzz" Van Dulken](https://www.hltv.org/player/10394/twistzz)'s troops came into the tournament with a solid but not guaranteed chance to make the Major, with wins against [Alliance](https://www.hltv.org/team/12474/alliance) and [K27](https://www.hltv.org/team/12895/k27) now boosting their chances of qualifying to a near certainty.

Both of their games in Belgrade were comfortable 2-0 victories, with [FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) allowing their opponents to reach double digits on only one map.

The international squad will now wait for their opponents in the semi-final, set to be played on Thursday.
