[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) were left ruing missed opportunities in Bucharest after a 2-0 defeat at the hands of [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia).

The new-look [Johnny "JT" Theodosiou](https://www.hltv.org/player/11630/jt) lineup has showed plenty of promise and beat both [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) and [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) at this tournament, but familiar frailties let them down against [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) and now [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia).

Old [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) boy [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) will be in [JT](https://www.hltv.org/player/11630/jt)'s nightmares tonight after winning three crucial rounds single-handedly with just a plucky Five-SeveN in his inventory.

Round 19 on Nuke was particularly damaging, at a point where [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) had looked great in their T-side gun rounds and were set for a big upset on [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)'s map pick.

[YEKINDAR](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) was also excellent with a rifle in his hand, posting more than 100 ADR on Inferno as he ensured [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) would secure playoffs.

The convincing nature of Inferno is a true achievement: [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) were 9-1 on the map before today and had beaten [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) and [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) on the map.

But the mental damage done by [YEKINDAR](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar)'s trusty pistol proved too tough to overcome, even with a strong [Keith "NAF" Markovic](https://www.hltv.org/player/8520/naf) performance, and it's [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) who stroll into playoffs.
