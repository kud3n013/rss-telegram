[FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) kicked off their PGL Masters Bucharest campaign with a 2-0 victory over [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo), with [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) leading the way in his first appearance for the team.

The Spaniard finished the series with 34 kills, 18 deaths and a 1.47 rating as [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) secured Nuke 13-9 and Ancient 13-6. His consistency complemented strong contributions from [Dmytro "dem0n" Myroshnychenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/20584/dem0n) on the entry and [Aulon "Krabeni" Fazlija](https://www.hltv.org/player/22203/krabeni) on the second map.

This result sends [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) into the 1-0 pool, while [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) will continue their campaign in the 0-1.

[SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) joined [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) from [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2)'s bench earlier this month, replacing [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) after spending the summer out of the starting lineup following [Artem "r1nkle" Moroz](https://www.hltv.org/player/20425/r1nkle)'s arrival. His signing brought an experienced AWPer into the young team.

For the first map, [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) initially found themselves in trouble on the opponent's Nuke pick, falling behind 2-7 before recovering to finish the half just two rounds down. They carried that momentum through the break, winning the second pistol round and eventually pulling clear with four consecutive rounds from 9-9.

Ancient followed a similar pattern. [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) recovered from 3-6 to level the score at the interval, but could not add another round after the side-switch. Just like on Nuke, [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) swept the second half to close out the series.
