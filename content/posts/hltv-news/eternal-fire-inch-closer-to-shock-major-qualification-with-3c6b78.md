[Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire) have secured the Stake Ranked Episode 5 title after a 3-2 win over [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) in the grand final, coming back from a 0-2 deficit after a fantastic show of resilience.

"I can’t really put my feelings into words. It truly feels like a dream," [Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire) CEO Furkan Güven [said on X.](https://x.com/1NCONcs2/status/2116270531326693686)

The grand final was a bout between two Major hopefuls, with the winner set to increase their chances to around 90%, while the loser will most likely be left without a Major spot.

In the end, it was [Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire) who battled to victory thanks to a great performance by [Samet "jottAAA" Köklü](https://www.hltv.org/player/22361/jottaaa) (1.21 series rating), finishing off a lower bracket run that included wins over [Nemesis](https://www.hltv.org/team/13355/nemesis), [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big), [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) and [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr).

The end of Stake Ranked Episode 5 means that only [five teams remain in the running](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302) for the three remaining Major spots in Europe.

[Eternal Fire](https://www.hltv.org/team/11251/eternal-fire) are the biggest winners of the event after rising to 87.9% thanks to winning their third tournament in a month and a half, while [fnatic](https://www.hltv.org/team/4991/fnatic) are down to 17.5%.
