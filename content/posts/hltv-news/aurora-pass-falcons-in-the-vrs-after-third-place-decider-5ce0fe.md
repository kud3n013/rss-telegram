[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) beat [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) in the Thunderpick World Championship third-place decider to secure their second podium in a row after ESL Pro League, and surpassed their direct rivals in the VRS ranking as a result, fortifying their standing for a Stage 3 invite to the PGL Major.

[Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) will not play until the Major invite cut-off, but they are still in a strong position for a Stage 3 invite with an 88.3% chance according to our invite predictor, although [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) — who are slated to play [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in the grand final — are chasing them in seventh place on the ranking, 120 points behind.

[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) and [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) clashed recently in the ESL Pro League Season 24 group stage, with [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) coming out on top, although the Turkish-majority squad went on to take third place at the Spodek while [Finn "karrigan" Andersen](https://www.hltv.org/player/429/karrigan) and his men failed to make it past the quarter-finals.

This time around [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) went for Anubis instead of Mirage, but the gambit didn't work, as [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) put up nine rounds on the attack off the back of a 2.04-rated half by [Ilya "m0NESY" Osipov](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy). On the CT side, it was his Russian teammate, [Maksim "kyousuke" Lukin](https://www.hltv.org/player/24177/kyousuke), who led the way with a +9.74% swing in the nine rounds it took to steal the map, 13-8, and secure the series lead.

[Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons)' Dust2 pick came next, and the series was theirs for the taking, as the last time the two teams met it was [Nikola "NiKo" Kovač](https://www.hltv.org/player/3741/niko) and company who took it, 13-10.

Starting on the CT side, [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) seized the chance to get ahead as [m0NESY](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) struggled to get going on the attack, securing eight at the half. After the switch, and with [m0NESY](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) setting the pace with the AWP on the defense, [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) came back from 7-11, and then up against three map points to force overtime.

[Danny "zonic" Sørensen](https://www.hltv.org/coach/21/zonic)'s men couldn't get it done in extra rounds, however, as the red-hot [Jimi "Jimpphat" Salo](https://www.hltv.org/player/18850/jimpphat) helped power [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) with a 1.65 rating and +6.48% to a 19-17 victory that tied the series up.

> [Aurora retake B to get last-second defuse](https://www.twitch.tv/thunderpicktv/clip/SpeedySpikyPigSoonerLater-MIUdk4FZ3uWKqXck)

The third-place decider went to Nuke and remained neck-and-neck throughout the early stage, with [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) taking a slim 7-5 lead at the switch and tying it up at 9-9 in the second half. From there, though, [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) settled in on the defense and won four in a row with [Özgür "woxic" Eker](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic)'s AWP ringing as he posted a 2.01 rating with 138.5 ADR on the CT side in the 13-9 victory.
