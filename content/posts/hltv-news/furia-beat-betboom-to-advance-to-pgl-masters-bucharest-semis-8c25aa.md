[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) have advanced to the semi-finals at PGL Masters Bucharest after clinching a 2-0 victory over [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom).

There, the Brazilian-majority squad will face off against [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) for the fourth time in just over a month, having won on the first occasion at StarSeries Fall before suffering two convincing 0-2 losses at ESL Pro League S24.

The rematch from the Thunderpick World Championship grand final, which [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) claimed 3-2 after a back-and-forth best-of-five series, saw the favorites repeat their T-side performances on Dust2 and Cache just two weeks later.

The series looked like a foregone conclusion when [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) went up 11-2 on the opener, toying with [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) all around the map with [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) and [Yuri "yuurih" Santos](https://www.hltv.org/player/12553/yuurih) pulling the opponents from side to side with flanks, while the pack weaved back and forth.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) made a game of it after all with a force-buy win and two crucial [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) rounds up against map point and forced overtime, but it all came apart there, as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) went flawless off the back of a clean [YEKINDAR](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) clutch.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) then took control of Cache early as their opponents' aggressive A pushes went hit or miss, but [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) once again put on a solid T side after the half switched thanks to a [Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) 1v2 clutch in the pistol and a gut-wrenching [YEKINDAR](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) quad-kill from Boost.
