[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) have secured their first trophy since winning Esports World Cup 2025 — 14 months ago — after a masterclass against [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) in the best-of-five grand final at 1win Private Club Season 2.

The tournament marked the debut of the new-look [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) roster, which saw [Anarbileg "cobrazera" Uuganbayar](https://www.hltv.org/player/23402/cobrazera) return to the active lineup and the addition of [Yesuntumur "nin9" Gantulga](https://www.hltv.org/player/15072/nin9) from [The Huns](https://www.hltv.org/team/12510/the-huns).

[Garidmagnai "bLitz" Byambasuren](https://www.hltv.org/player/20194/blitz)'s men have struggled to return to a top level since benching — and later losing — [Azbayar "Senzu" Munkhbold](https://www.hltv.org/player/20903/senzu), and their fortunes took a turn for the worse after [Ayush "mzinho" Batbold](https://www.hltv.org/player/21001/mzinho) also departed the lineup to link up with his former teammate on [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame).

A few playoff runs did little to ease the sting of several group stage exits, and the team opted for a bigger change after exiting FISSURE Playground 3 in last place.

Their Major invite never truly looked in danger, but their dismal run of form left the possibility of missing a direct invite to Stage 2 on the table. That possibility has likely been averted after a stellar run in their debut event with [nin9](https://www.hltv.org/player/15072/nin9) at 1win Private Club, where [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) tallied an opening win over [Alliance](https://www.hltv.org/team/12474/alliance) before dropping down to the lower bracket following a blowout 0-2 loss to [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big) in the upper semi-finals.

But [bLitz](https://www.hltv.org/player/20194/blitz)'s side recovered spectacularly with their backs against the wall, powering past [magic](https://www.hltv.org/team/13592/magic) and [PRIVATE](https://www.hltv.org/team/14030/private) to earn a rematch against [BIG](https://www.hltv.org/team/7532/big) in the consolidation final. This time, [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) emerged victorious, reverse-sweeping the series with dominant wins on Inferno (13-3) and Mirage (13-7) after losing Cache (4-13) thanks to a server-leading 1.60-rated showing by [Usukhbayar "910" Banzragch](https://www.hltv.org/player/21809/910).

It was a storybook ending to the event for the team's new addition, [nin9](https://www.hltv.org/player/15072/nin9), as he took over in the title decider after a relatively quiet tournament. The 28-year-old posted a 1.56 rating across a three-map series that saw [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) only tested on Mirage, needing all 24 rounds to secure the win after nearly letting a 12-4 lead slip while dominating on Ancient (13-6) and Dust2 (13-4).
