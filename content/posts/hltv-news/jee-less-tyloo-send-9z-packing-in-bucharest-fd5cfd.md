[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) have entered the 1-2 pool at PGL Masters Bucharest after a come-from-behind win over [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z), who have been sent packing in last place in shocking fashion.

Playing without AWPer [Dongkai "Jee" Ji](https://www.hltv.org/player/20702/jee), who has been ruled out for the rest of the event with a bout of chickenpox, the Chinese team gathered energy and found the best way to utilize head coach [WeiJie "zhokiNg" Zhong](https://www.hltv.org/coach/8601/zhoking) as the series went on after losing their pick of Inferno, where they nearly pulled off a comeback from 11-6 down.

[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) then effortlessly secured Nuke and Mirage, with rookie [Su "Zero" Jingshen](https://www.hltv.org/player/24857/zero) playing a key role on both maps.

The win is a significant boost for [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo), who earn 69 Valve points and see their chances of a Major invite climb from 52% to 91%.

[Ignacio "meyern" Meyer](https://www.hltv.org/player/14737/meyern) opened up Inferno with a Dual Berettas ace to fire [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) into the lead on [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo)'s choice of map. That was the start of a dominant half from the Argentinian AWPer, who went 17-5 (2.22 rating and +9.20% round swing) as [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) cruised to a 9-3 lead.

[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) faced a steep deficit but refused to give up. [Qianhao "Moseyuh" Chen](https://www.hltv.org/player/21621/moseyuh) led the comeback attempt, which fell short when the team couldn't hold down the B site in the final round of regulation.

[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) entered Nuke on a five-game losing streak on the map, so it certainly shocked many that they looked well-drilled on the CT side, limiting [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) to just three rounds in the first half.

The barrage continued in the second half as [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) widened their lead with a clean pistol round and the conversion. The two teams proceeded to trade rounds before [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) closed out the map on a force buy with kills from [Zero](https://www.hltv.org/player/24857/zero) and [Yi "JamYoung" Yang](https://www.hltv.org/player/19645/jamyoung).

[9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) started strongly on Mirage and built a 6-1 lead, but [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) won the final five rounds of the half, breaking through the defense with ease thanks to an aggressive, in-your-face approach that made up for their AWPer's absence.

[TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo) showed no sign of slowing down, stretching their winning run to 11-0 after a flawless start on the CT side. Facing series point, [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) finally halted the slide, but their lacklustre offense could produce only a single round.
