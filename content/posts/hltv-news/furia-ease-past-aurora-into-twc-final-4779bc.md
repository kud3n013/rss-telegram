Reigning champions [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) are through to the final of Thunderpick World Championship thanks to a 2-0 win over [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), the majority-Brazilian side earning their berth with wins on Dust2 (13-8) and Inferno (13-10).

These two teams met in the last tier one event, ESL Pro League, and it was [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) who walked away victorious. With a new approach to the veto and seemingly renewed form, [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) managed to turn things around in this rematch and continue their successful tournament run.

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) were deserved winners across the series but things were made more comfortable by their repeated success in low-buy rounds, and their individual firepower was a step above that of [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora).

[Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) has told HLTV during this event that one event victory can do a lot for a team's momentum, and considering how good [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) have looked thus far in Malta, perhaps they are on the brink of earning that all-important trophy as the Major fast approaches.

---

Strong starts to both halves were not enough for [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) on Dust2, as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) picked themselves up once their economy was in order and outplayed their opponents. [Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) and [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) were in immense form on the CT side, always finding themselves in the right place at the right time, before the duo continued their reign of terror as part of a well-called offensive half.

> [FURIA find crucial B hold in low-buy round](https://www.twitch.tv/thunderpicktv/clip/CrowdedTawdryJaguarFUNgineer-hrrpuYUp-8iF93RI)

A force-buy way kicked off Inferno and it was [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)'s T side that won out and took control of the half for a 4-1 lead, before the teams traded rounds back and forth with constant battles on the A bombsite. [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) took advantage of the conditioning by switching their focus to B, [YEKINDAR](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar)'s entry work bagging two victories in a row, before a last-round pivot back to A caught [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) flat-footed and sealed a dominant 8-4 half for [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia).

The second half began with yet another force-buy tussle that once again went the way of [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), granting them a 10-6 advantage, before a [Jimi "Jimpphat" Salo](https://www.hltv.org/player/18850/jimpphat) clutch helped [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) flipped the script and close the deficit. In the crucial round, however, [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) ran into a pistol stack on the B bombsite, collapsing their momentum and allowing [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) to sweep to the map win and a 2-0 series victory.
