PGL has revealed the opening matchups for Masters Bucharest 2026 ahead of the tournament's kick off on October 24. The 16 teams in attendance will play out a Swiss-style group stage, with eight teams progressing to a single-elimination playoff bracket.

Some of the headline matchups of the opening day will see [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) take on [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz), the latter playing their first tier one event with [Yesuntumur "nin9" Gantulga](https://www.hltv.org/player/15072/nin9) among their number, and [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) face a [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) squad seeking to secure their Singapore Major berth.

Elsewhere in the event [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) will be hoping to continue their push for a Singapore Major spot, as a playoff berth in Bucharest would seal the deal, and many eyes will be focused on [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) as they debut with their new AWPer [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus).
