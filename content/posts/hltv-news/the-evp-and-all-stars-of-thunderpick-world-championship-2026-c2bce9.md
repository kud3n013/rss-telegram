[Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) may be tired but he is in as good a form as he ever has been.

He's not had a LAN below a 1.15 rating this season, and now has earned his reward with an MVP and trophy lift at Thunderpick World Championship.

That means both of the Brazilian's MVPs come from TWC, adding to his one from last year. But this one was even stronger than last year's, with a whopping seven player-of-the-map performances.

The gap to his team was stark in almost every metric: +0.35 in rating, +0.24 against top 10 teams, +3.28% round swing. His average rank on his team's scoreboard was 1.75.

It's [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato)'s fifth EVP+ event of 2026, as he looks to improve on his best-ever ninth-place finish in the Top 20.

## Radar Chart Stat Glossary

- **Rating 3.0**: HLTV's composite rating of kills, damage, survival, KAST, multis and Swing
- **KPRW**: Kills per round win
- **Eco Swing**: Duels won above expected value, based on equipment of a player and their opponent
- **Impact Rounds**: Rounds with positive round swing
- **Round Swing**: The win probability sum of a player's actions
- **A Game**: A player's 75th percentile map
- **B Game**: A player's 50th percentile map
- **C Game**: A player's 25th percentile map
- **Vs Top 10**: Rating vs top 10 teams (HLTV Ranking)
- **Vs Top Half**: Rating vs teams who placed in the top half of the event
- **Rank in Team**: Avg. rank within your team by rating
- **Map Wins**: Rating in map wins in series wins

### Russia [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez)

If anyone was going to ruin [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato)'s party, it was [Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez).

After a day one loss to [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), [Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) stepped up a gear and posted a 1.44 rating in map wins to guide [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) through the lower bracket to the final.

He was player of the map six times, only one fewer than [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato), and finished the final with the highest rating (1.23) of anyone to cap a magnificent event.

[Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) was stellar in playoffs overall (1.29 rating) and in KPRW (1.08), making it a performance worthy of an MVP had [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) not been in such blistering form.

## Honorable mentions

There are no more EVPs, so we owe you all an explanation. In an eight-team event, we generally aim for four players above the EVP threshold, so one MVP and three EVPs.

But this is a guideline, and when no player crosses that threshold it is preferable to be strict rather than to elevate the third and fourth highest-ranked players to EVP status.

You've already seen this at BLAST Bounty, though the format there lends itself to a small number of EVPs as players play fewer games.

Here, it's more peculiar, but it is telling that the two awards we did hand out, to [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) and [Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez), were both very high-scoring ones with huge gaps to their teammates.

[Ilya "m0NESY" Osipov](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) was closest to a nod, but before the third-place decider — which we have treated with a low weight all year — he had three good maps and four middling ones. His rating in map wins was only 1.26, the same as [René "TeSeS" Madsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/12018/teses).

[Danil "molodoy" Golubenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/24144/molodoy) was consistent, but he lacked peaks, especially in playoffs. [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar), despite illness, had a few more peaks but not enough for an EVP.

[Pavel "S1ren" Ogloblin](https://www.hltv.org/player/18506/s1ren) and [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte) were similar stories, good events but not exceptionally valuable ones.

[Jimi "Jimpphat" Salo](https://www.hltv.org/player/18850/jimpphat) looks good by the end, but had a 1.05 rating before the third-place decider (1.07 in the four map wins). Even with lots of credit for his showing against [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons), he falls short of EVP status. [Ali "Wicadia" Haydar Yalçın](https://www.hltv.org/player/21243/wicadia) was better in the run to semi-finals but did not confirm it in playoffs.

[Luciano "luchov" Herrera](https://www.hltv.org/player/20394/luchov) and [Bruno "latto" Rebelatto](https://www.hltv.org/player/19045/latto) were great in groups, but neither was at the outstanding level required for a group stage nod.

## All-Star

There is room for [m0NESY](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy) and [S1ren](https://www.hltv.org/player/18506/s1ren) in our all-star lineup, however, as the best AWPer and Anchor of the event.

[S1ren](https://www.hltv.org/player/18506/s1ren) deserves credit for his 7-0 record in 1v1 clutches this event, while [m0NESY](https://www.hltv.org/player/19230/m0nesy)'s 1.18 KPRW is also excellent.

[Magnojez](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) and [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) are the easy picks, the two dominant players in Malta.

Finally, [Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) — who was fragging at a good level this event — completes the list as the in-game leader who steadied [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) when they were 2-1 down in the final and helped them emerge victorious.
