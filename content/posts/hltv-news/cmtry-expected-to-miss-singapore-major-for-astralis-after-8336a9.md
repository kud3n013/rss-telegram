[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) rifler [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) is ineligible to play for the team at the Singapore Major following [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut)'s elimination at PGL Masters Bucharest in the quarter-finals after a 2-0 loss to [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga).

That was only [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus)'s fourth official match for [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) following his arrival from [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) at the start of the month. The team has no more matches left to play before the November 2 invite cut-off for the Major, which means that [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) remain listed on their VRS roster.

[SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) will still be able to play for [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) as a substitute (though he won't be eligible for a sticker), while [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) is tied to the roster and cannot be registered by another team.

[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis)' only hope is for [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) to enter an open LAN, such as EPIC 49 in Kettering, so that [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) can play a fifth match and join the VRS roster, which would free up [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry). [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) have given no sign so far that they plan to.

[Jakob "jabbi" Nygaard](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi) returned to [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) on Tuesday for the rest of Stake Ranked Episode 5, a move that stops [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) from playing a fifth match for them before the cut-off.

When a player is on two VRS rosters, only the higher-ranked team gets a Major invite, which would have been [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut), who sit three places above [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) in the Europe VRS.
