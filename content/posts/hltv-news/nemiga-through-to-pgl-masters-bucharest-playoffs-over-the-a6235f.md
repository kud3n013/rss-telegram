[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) secured their spot in the PGL Masters Bucharest playoffs with a 2-0 win on Cache (13-5) and Dust2 (19-15) against [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) in the 2-2 Swiss group stage decider.

[Daniel "syph0" Ageyev](https://www.hltv.org/player/23319/syph0), the number two prospect in our ranking, who is on loan in [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) from [Spirit Academy](https://www.hltv.org/team/7900/spirit-academy), had a day against [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz), topping the scoreboard with a 1.90 rating, +8.09% swing, 111.3 ADR, and 51 kills across the two maps.

[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz)'s struggles on Cache, where they had played three times this month with no victories, showed as [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) went on a rampage on the T side of their pick. The Asian team couldn't muster consecutive rounds in a 9-3 half, which included a 1v3 by [syph0](https://www.hltv.org/player/23319/syph0) and a 1v2 by [Kirill "Xant3r" Kononov](https://www.hltv.org/player/20387/xant3r).

[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) linked their first two rounds after the switch, but wouldn't get another as [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) settled into the CT side and won four in a row to make it 13-5.

> [syph0 clutches 1v3 on Cache](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl/clip/ViscousHumbleAntKappaClaus-HERE68OepYL5P2Iv)

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) started Dust2 confidently, winning four of the first five rounds, but then [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) went on a tear, winning seven of their own before losing the final one to lead 7-5 at the half.

Having won the second pistol round, [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) brought it even, 7-7, before the series was tied up again two rounds later when [syph0](https://www.hltv.org/player/23319/syph0) defended a B afterplant situation with a triple.

[The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) hit map point, 12-9, and looked poised to send it to the Mirage decider with a 97% map win probability, but couldn't close it out as [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) won three in a row while up against the wall to force extra rounds.

It took double overtime, as the two teams remained at a stalemate after six rounds, 15-15, when [Beksultan "khaN" Ospan](https://www.hltv.org/player/23317/khan) activated with seven kills in the four rounds it took [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) to punch their ticket to the playoffs with a 19-15 victory.
