[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) kept their Thunderpick World Championship campaign alive at the expense of [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision), sending their domestic rivals packing from the tournament after dominating on Anubis (13-4) and Mirage (13-7) to complete a reverse sweep following a loss on Ancient (10-13).

It is an important win for [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom)'s PGL Singapore Major qualification hopes after their odds took a hit with a loss to [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in their opener. [Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4)'s side now sit at an 89.1% chance of securing a spot, according to [HLTV's simulation page](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302), and will have a chance to increase that further in the Group B decider match against the loser of [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) vs. [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy).

The match was inconsequential for [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision)'s own Major campaign, but the loss comes as a blow to the team's confidence after they showed an uptick in form by reaching the playoffs at ESL Pro League Season 24.

The series could have been a sweep for [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) had [Vladislav "xiELO" Lysov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22471/xielo) not come alive on Ancient, finding an early 1v3 clutch to help [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) salvage an otherwise torrid T half where individual errors and poor fights left them with only four rounds.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) then won the pistol and conversion to reach double digits, but failed to tally a single round after that as [xiELO](https://www.hltv.org/player/22471/xielo) put on a masterclass CT side (19-4 K-D, 136.4 ADR, +16.84% Round Swing, seven multi-kills, 2.82 rating) to power a lockout defense.

Nothing went [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision)'s way after that, though. [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) reigned supreme on Anubis with a phenomenal CT side, with errors again plaguing [Dzhami "Jame" Ali](https://www.hltv.org/player/13776/jame)'s side as communication lapsed in the mid-round.

That trend carried on to Mirage as [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) picked apart [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision)'s defaults round after round, with efforts to claw back in the late round falling by the wayside due to the early disadvantages. [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte)'s AWP found impact throughout a 9-3 CT side half, and [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) wasted no time in reaching match point before eventually putting to bed a comeback effort on a half-buy.
