[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) picked up their first victory at PGL Masters Bucharest with a convincing 2-0 win over [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) on Dust2 (13-5) and Nuke (13-3).

The result brings [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) back to the 1-1 pool after their opening defeat to [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid). [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z), meanwhile, have yet to win a map following losses to [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) and [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz), and must now win three consecutive series to reach the playoffs.

Dust2 remained competitive for much of the first half. [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) won the opening pistol but lost the following round, with the teams eventually reaching a 4-4. [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) then took three of the final four rounds to lead 7-5 at the break before shutting out their opponents in the second half.

[Luciano "luchov" Herrera](https://www.hltv.org/player/20394/luchov) topped the server with a 1.69 rating, but his efforts were not enough as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) won all six rounds after the break to close out Dust2.

[Clip link](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl_cs2/clip/EagerQuaintPheasantWoofer-Pj_trQmYk3ulONR1)

[9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) offered even less resistance on Nuke. [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) started with a 6-1 lead and finished the half 10-2, leaving [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z) with little room for a recovery. A second-half pistol win briefly kept the South Americans alive, but [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) answered with three straight rounds to close out the series.

[Ádám "torzsi" Torzsás](https://www.hltv.org/player/18072/torzsi) topped Nuke with 18 kills and a 2.09 rating, while [Lotan "Spinx" Giladi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18221/spinx) finished narrowly ahead across the series with a 1.53 rating to his teammate's 1.51. Both teams await their third-round opponents.
