Short: PGL disqualifies acidhouze from PGL Bucharest open qualifier

2h ago

[By king\_dempz](https://twitter.com/KingDempz)

[Short: PGL disqualifies acidhouze from PGL Bucharest open qualifier](https://www.hltv.org/news/45708/short-news-week-41#f1)

PGL has disqualified [acidhouze](https://www.hltv.org/team/14094/acidhouze) from the PGL Bucharest 2027 open qualifier due to competitive integrity concerns.

[Inner Circle Academy](https://www.hltv.org/team/13681/inner-circle-academy) played [acidhouze](https://www.hltv.org/team/14094/acidhouze) during the qualifier but forfeited the match partway through, the organization [stating on X](https://x.com/InCircleEsports/status/2109355385538674784) that they chose to forfeit "so the cheaters couldn't profit from betting."

[Inner Circle Academy](https://www.hltv.org/team/13681/inner-circle-academy) have been reinstated to the qualifier in the wake of [acidhouze](https://www.hltv.org/team/14094/acidhouze)'s disqualification.
