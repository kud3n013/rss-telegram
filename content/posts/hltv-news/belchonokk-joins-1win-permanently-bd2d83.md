[1win](https://www.hltv.org/team/10621/1win) has announced the signing of [Andrey "BELCHONOKK" Yasinskiy](https://www.hltv.org/player/19235/belchonokk) after coming to terms with [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) for the player's transfer as his loan term came to an end.

The Russian rifler rose to prominence at the beginning of the year when the young [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) roster won the BLAST Bounty 2026 Season 1 Finals, but he was the first player of that starting five to get benched in June after a period of regression.

[BELCHONOKK](https://www.hltv.org/player/19235/belchonokk) then joined [TDK](https://www.hltv.org/team/13644/tdk), who were grinding their way through the online circuit before finding a home with [1win](https://www.hltv.org/team/10621/1win) in August, although it was on a loan from [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision).

[BELCHONOKK](https://www.hltv.org/player/19235/belchonokk)'s best result with [1win](https://www.hltv.org/team/10621/1win) was his lastest, a quarter-final finish at ESL Pro League Season 24, where he posted a 1.12 rating. Now, as a full-fledged member of the team, his next event will be GLuck Moscow Cyber Games 2026.

[1win](https://www.hltv.org/team/10621/1win) have all but guaranteed a spot at the PGL Major in Singapore, according to our predictor, which would be [BELCHONOKK](https://www.hltv.org/player/19235/belchonokk)'s third after playing in Bucharest and Cologne with [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision).
