[Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) have claimed victory at Stake Pulse Beat III after beating [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) 2-1 in the grand final (13-2 Mirage, 17-19 Cache, 13-9 Ancient).

It is the third trophy in as many weeks for [Karim "Krimbo" Moussa](https://www.hltv.org/player/19899/krimbo)'s men, who soared up the VRS after securing consecutive titles at ROG JOURNEY Autumn and 1win Private Club Season 1.

A third win has all but secured the team an invite to PGL Major Singapore, with [HLTV's invite simulation](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302) putting them at a 98% chance of qualifying.

[Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) didn't drop a series en route to their title victory, although they were pushed to the limit in all but one of their five series. Wins over [Betclic](https://www.hltv.org/team/12916/betclic) and [Inner Circle](https://www.hltv.org/team/13117/inner-circle) in the group stage gave way to a hard-fought three-map series victory over [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) in the playoffs, where [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) had to fight back from a 0-8 deficit on map two to pull off a reverse-sweep.

They had to fight from behind again on the Mirage decider to eke out a 2-1 win over [HEROIC](https://www.hltv.org/team/7175/heroic) in the semi-finals, and could have swept [MIBR](https://www.hltv.org/team/9215/mibr) in the grand final after stealing the Brazilian team's Mirage pick in domineering fashion had [Carlos "venomzera" Eduardo](https://www.hltv.org/player/21288/venomzera) and [Felipe "insani" Yuji](https://www.hltv.org/player/21037/insani) not come alive to recover a 7-12 deficit on Cache and force a decider by winning in double overtime.

[Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) showed tenacity, though, and climbed back from a deficit of their own on Ancient, running away with the game after being down 2-7 by giving up just two rounds in the second half to secure the title win in Stockholm.
