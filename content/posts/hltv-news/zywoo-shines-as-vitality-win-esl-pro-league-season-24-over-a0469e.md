[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) took home the ESL Pro League Season 24 trophy after breezing past [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) in the best-of-five grand final (Cache 13-7, Inferno 13-8, Dust2 16-12).

The win is [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s second title in a row after they won StarSeries Fall earlier in September as they look to be getting back to the level they displayed at the start of the year.

"Thanks to my partner and my baby that it didn't happen too early, so that I could win another trophy," [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex) joked in a post-match interview.

---

The series began on Cache, where [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) got off to a hot 5-1 start thanks to some proactive plays on the CT side. While they shut down anything [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) had to offer in the opening few rounds, however, [apEX](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex) and company quickly woke up to win six consecutive rounds to win the half.

Once on the CT side, [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) didn't let off the gas to power his team to a 13-7 victory with 21 kills, 2.00 rating, and 107 ADR.

Inferno continued in the same vein, as [Shahar "flameZ" Shushan](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez) and [apEX](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex) locked down Banana to build a platform for a dominant 9-3 half. Their fortunes got even better as they won a second-round force-buy on their offense, and while [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) didn't down tools and made the scoreline respectable, [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) were unfazed on their way to a 13-8 win.

[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) were able to reset and start Dust2 on the right foot, but [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s unrelenting B hits eventually took their toll. Every individual in the [apEX](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex)-led squad had their moments, such as a snappy 3k by [William "mezii" Merriman](https://www.hltv.org/player/18462/mezii) that opened up the B site with seconds to go, and [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) were once again ahead at the halftime switch.

[Dorian "xertioN" Berman](https://www.hltv.org/player/20312/xertion) then took matters into his own hands with some crucial multi-kills that helped [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) secure three map points despite [Adrian "xelex" Vincze](https://www.hltv.org/player/24457/xelex) being almost non-existent on the map, but [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) cashed in all their experience to secure overtime.

[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz)'s mental seemed to have broken in added time, failing to win any of their three T rounds in the first half before petering out with a limp A defense to suffer a 0-3 defeat in the grand final.
