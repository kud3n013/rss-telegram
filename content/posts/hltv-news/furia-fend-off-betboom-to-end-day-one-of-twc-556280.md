The first day of the Thunderpick World Championship has wrapped up with [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) and [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) beating [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) and [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision), respectively, in Group B.

[Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) also beat [Ninjas in Pyjamas](https://www.hltv.org/team/4411/ninjas-in-pyjamas) in their Group A opener, leaving [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) as the only favorite to falter in Malta after losing to [9z](https://www.hltv.org/team/9996/9z).

Despite all four series ending 2-0, five maps were embattled affairs, including [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) needing overtime to close out Ancient after leading 8-4 and winning the pistol after the swap.

[Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) had to dig deep to beat [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) after squandering leads of their own, going up 8-2 and 12-5 on Dust2 in their return to the server with [Andrei "arT" Piovezan](https://www.hltv.org/player/12521/art) before falling flat on their T side.

It took until round 24 for [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) to get the job done after [PARIVISION](https://www.hltv.org/team/12467/parivision) fumbled a 3v2, and Mirage was a similar story as [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) needed overtime and a 1.57-rated map from [Eduardo "dumau" Wolkmer](https://www.hltv.org/player/15698/dumau) to secure victory after leading 12-8.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) missed out on a golden chance to increase their Major qualification odds to 93% after letting 8-4 leads slip on Ancient and Mirage against [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), but are still firmly in the running for an invite.

[Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4)'s men will lament just how many advantageous situations they dropped throughout the series, particularly on the T side of Mirage where they managed just one round as [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) was unrelenting in his aggression and [Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) was a menace, with the latter topping the charts for the series with a 1.72 rating.
