[MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) booked a place in the PGL Masters Bucharest semi-finals on Thursday with a comprehensive 2-0 win over a lacklustre [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora), who took an early lead on Mirage but won only five of the remaining 31 rounds in the series.

It was the first bout between the two teams since PGL Astana, where [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) fielded [Justinas "jL" Lekavicius](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) as a stand-in and [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) still had a full Turkish roster. In a post-match interview, [Adrian "xelex" Vincze](https://www.hltv.org/player/24457/xelex) said that his team prepared for the series "as usual."

"It worked out," he told [James "BanKs" Banks](https://www.hltv.org/player/18473/banks). "It was very good by sycrone and all the other guys. The prep worked well, we had good ideas. We called it at the right moment. It worked out."

In a post on X after the series, [Özgür "woxic" Eker](https://www.hltv.org/player/8574/woxic) said, "Today it just wasn't our day."

"Unfortunately, we couldn’t put up a fight against MOUZ, either as a team or individually, and as a result, we’re eliminated from the tournament," the Turkish AWPer wrote.

[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) jumped out to a 3-0 lead on the CT side of Mirage, but the game shifted once [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) could afford full buys. [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) took control from there and went into half-time up by two, led by [Lotan "Spinx" Giladi](https://www.hltv.org/player/18221/spinx), who posted a 1.74 rating and a +4.13% round swing.

[Oldřich "PR" Nový](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr) picked up a triple kill in the following pistol to push [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) further into the lead. One T round was all that [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) could muster before [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) finished the game off, taking the series to Cache.

The barrage continued on the following map as [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora)'s toothless offense struggled to find gaps in [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz)'s setup, and none of their players looked capable of making a difference. After a 10-2 landslide, [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) quickly picked up three rounds on the offense to close out the game and seal passage to the next round.
