[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) are through to the playoffs of the Thunderpick World Championship, earning their berth via a 2-1 win over [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) that featured wins on Inferno (13-5) and Mirage (13-8) after a loss on Cache (10-13).

This grudge match for the title of best South American team had already been played twice this season, [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) coming out on top at both Esports World Cup and FISSURE Playground 3.

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) opted to change up their veto in the search for a win against [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy), leaving Inferno open whilst going for a Cache pick themselves. We also saw a positional switch up on Inferno, with [Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) moving from his usual B support role on CT side into the rotator slot.

> [dumau bags an ace with a dropped AWP to clinch Cache](https://www.twitch.tv/thunderpicktv/clip/UgliestBillowingLemurNotLikeThis-1-LjRb49ZIQz_qzZ)

The game was scrappy early on Cache, the teams fighting through a multitude of force-buy rounds before [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) stabilized to secure a 7-5 lead at the half. [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) picked up a pistol win on their T half and ran with it to a 10-5 score, and although [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) got their feet under them, the deficit was too much to overturn; an [Eduardo "dumau" Wolkmer](https://www.hltv.org/player/15698/dumau) ace on a bad buy sealed the deal and gave [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) the lead in the series.

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) found much more success on the opening half of Inferno thanks to a force-buy win in the second round. Their CT side came up with opening kill after opening kill whilst [FalleN](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) made excellent rotations, and although [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) managed to get their foot in the door mid-way through the half via [Bruno "latto" Rebelatto](https://www.hltv.org/player/19045/latto) and [Santino "try" Rigal](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try) clutches, [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) did enough for an 8-4 lead at the half. Another force-buy win set [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) up for success on their offense, and [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) had little to offer as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) romped to victory.

> [FalleN 1v2 helps clinch the series](https://www.twitch.tv/thunderpicktv/clip/VibrantEntertainingCurryHeyGirl-afjbfZaMct9uWosD)

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)'s dominance continued into the first half of Mirage, a slow-burn offense reminiscent of [Dzhami "Jame" Ali](https://www.hltv.org/player/13776/jame) suffocating and picking apart [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy)'s CT side. [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) picked up some momentum early on their own T-side effort, but a late surge by [Danil "molodoy" Golubenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/24144/molodoy) combined with a vital [FalleN](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) 1v2 righted the ship and gave [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) the series win.

With their win [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) progress to the playoffs, whilst [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) will have to fight for their playoff berth in a final group stage game against [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom).
