[Imperial](https://www.hltv.org/team/9455/imperial) have won Circuit X Curitiba Season 4 to strengthen their VRS position significantly ahead of the Major invite deadline.

Three spots are still to play for in the Americas, and [Imperial](https://www.hltv.org/team/9455/imperial) sat at a 54% chance of being in that trio one week ago. After this win, they're now at [87%](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302#team-9455).

In their run to the trophy, [Imperial](https://www.hltv.org/team/9455/imperial) beat [BESTIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/12144/bestia) and [paiN](https://www.hltv.org/team/4773/pain) twice, boosting their own odds and harming their rivals'.

The final stayed in their control throughout after [Vinicius "VINI" Figueiredo](https://www.hltv.org/player/12822/vini) dropped a 2.15 rating on map one before a team performance got them over the line on Cache.

[Imperial](https://www.hltv.org/team/9455/imperial) are up to 40th in the VRS now, and inside the crucial top nine in their region.

[BESTIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/12144/bestia)'s third-place finish drops them to a 4% chance, while finalists [paiN](https://www.hltv.org/team/4773/pain) still have a thin pathway to the Major with a 23% chance and Gamers Club Masters VII around the corner.
