PGL Masters Bucharest is headed into the penultimate day of its group stage, which will see three teams join [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) and [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) in the playoffs and three others sent packing from Romania.

[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) and [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) will be the first teams to fight for a playoff berth, followed by the day's headline match between [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) and [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz). It will be the eighth meeting between the teams this year after recent clashes at ESL Pro League and the finals of BLAST Bounty Season 2 Finals and BLAST Open Porto, with [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) holding a 3-1 record over [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) since the summer break.

[Justinas "jL" Lekavicius](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) will face off against [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) for the first time in his career and since departing the organization in July in the final 2-1 match of the day. Regardless of the result, the Lithuanian rifler will play five matches as a stand-in for [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) before the November 2 VRS update, making it likely he will receive stickers at the Singapore Major instead of [Dan "apEX" Madesclaire](https://www.hltv.org/player/7322/apex).

The elimination pool will see three Brazilian teams fighting for their lives, starting with [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) looking to carry forward their momentum from their victory over [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2) against [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga). 1win Private Club Season 2 champions [The MongolZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/6248/the-mongolz) will then hope to avoid an early elimination in Bucharest against [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy), while [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) look to rebound from a loss to [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) against a hampered [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo).
