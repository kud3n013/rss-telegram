[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) booked a spot in the semi-final stage of the Thunderpick World Championship with a 2-1 victory over [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy), exacting revenge on their Brazilian rival for the recent losses at the Esports World Cup and FISSURE Playground 3.

One of the biggest talking points surrounding [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) in recent weeks has been their grueling schedule. Thunderpick marks their seventh stop of the season, an apparent push to maximize their chances of winning a trophy before [Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) retires at the end of the year.

"I'd say the main change is in our day-to-day planning, because since we've had so many tournaments, there's no time to practice," he told HLTV after the match. "So we've been practicing every day before the games, and it's been good that these games are taking place later in the day, because we manage to play two, three, sometimes even four maps before coming to the tournament, which is the only way to practice, it's on the day of the matches.

"Even today, before the match, there were two guys lying down sleeping, tired from practice. But that's life. We have to find a way to make up for that lack of practice time by practicing on game days."

Thunderpick is without a doubt [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia)'s best shot at lifting a trophy before the final leg of the season. Defending their 2025 title, [FalleN](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) argued, would give the team substantial momentum heading into the remaining events.

"As I even told the guys on the team last year, before we won [those titles], there's a real boost from winning a tournament," he said. "You become more confident, happier in your day-to-day life, and more satisfied. And it's no coincidence that we won almost four in a row last year after getting that boost, right?

"So, it would be great if we could win a tournament to really feel that again."

---

**FalleN, a 2-1 victory and you're going to the semi-finals here in Malta. You lost your map pick but then had a great showing on the other two maps. Give me your thoughts on the series.**

I think we played well. We had chances in the first map too. I especially really liked how we played Mirage. We managed to make it 9-3 as T, a side where it's hard to get many rounds nowadays, right? And I was happy with the team's resilience, with the performance we put in.

**You were coming off two consecutive losses against Legacy this year. How important was it to break this cycle? Even if it wasn't the best version of Legacy, given what happened with arT.**

It's important, Legacy has been playing well. They've managed to have good games against good teams, they won two tournaments this year, so it's a team we've been following, watching. We had a third-place decider against them, which in my opinion doesn't count for much. Nobody really wants to play those games much. And then we had a good game in which they beat us, and now we beat them in a good game. So, yeah, we're going to try to even out that rivalry at 2-2.

Even though the team has had decent results all year [...] since our expectations are set on winning tournaments, there's always that feeling that something's missing, right?

[Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen)

**You told Dust2 Brasil recently that you felt that team had gotten a bit stagnant, that maybe something needed to change. Have you managed to make those changes — maybe what happened on Inferno, switching positions, is a bit of an example of what you're trying to do to break this cycle of less positive results?**

I believe some position adjustments tend to happen from time to time, when we realize something could be different. So that thing on Inferno, even though it was just for that game — [laughs] otherwise, they'll go long [every time]. The game is very B-based nowadays, and having KSCERATO on B has huge potential for us, so we made that swap believing strongly in that. But I think these in-game swaps tend to happen every now and then, from time to time we make some changes.

But I'd say the main change is in our day-to-day planning, because since we've had so many tournaments, there's no time to practice. So we've been practicing every day before the games, and it's been good that these games are taking place later in the day, because we manage to play two, three, sometimes even four maps before coming to the tournament, which is the only way to practice, it's on the day of the matches. Even today, before the match, there were two guys lying down sleeping, tired from practice. But that's life. We have to find a way to make up for that lack of practice time by practicing on game days.

**Now your semi-final opponent: 9z or Aurora? Any preference?**

9z or Aurora? No preference at all. I believe they're both good teams. I think Aurora is a bit stronger than 9z. I think they're an opponent that, for us— at least in theory — poses a greater challenge, but 9z played an excellent game in their first match against Aurora, and if they can play at that level against us, we'll certainly have a tough time, right? So they're both good opponents, and whoever we end up facing, we'll see how it goes.

**There's been a lot of talk about FURIA playing tournament to tournament without getting any rest. How important is it for you to go far in this tournament, to reach the semi-finals, a final, or even win, to feel like all this effort is worth it? Because everything gets harder when results are not there, right?**

Yes, exactly. Thanks to last year's good results, we've raised the bar for what we can achieve as a team, right? So, even though the team has had decent results all year — we made it to the Major finals, we've had several playoff runs — since our expectations are set on winning tournaments, there's always that feeling that something's missing, right?

So, certainly, winning a title, making it to the finals, and being able to have that feeling that it's all worth it and the effort is paying off — that would definitely be a huge boost. So, as I even told the guys on the team last year, before we won [those titles], there's a real boost from winning a tournament. You become more confident, happier in your day-to-day life, and more satisfied. And it's no coincidence that we won almost four in a row last year after getting that boost, right? So, it would be great if we could win a tournament to really feel that again.
