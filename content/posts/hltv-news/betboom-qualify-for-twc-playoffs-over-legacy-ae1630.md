[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) became the final team to make the Thunderpick World Championship top four with a resilient 2-1 victory over [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) (Nuke 16-13, Dust2 11-13, Mirage 13-11).

The Russian squad will now face [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) in Saturday's semi-final, a team they beat at the IEM Cologne Major.

The win is also crucial for [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom)'s Major prospects, as the victory over [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) secured them 62 VRS points and clinched Major qualification as per [HLTV's invite simulator](https://www.hltv.org/valve-ranking/teams/event/8302).

The match began on Nuke, where [Bruno "latto" Rebelatto](https://www.hltv.org/player/19045/latto) and [Santino "try" Rigal](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try) combined for 28 kills to help [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) secure an 8-4 CT half. They extended that lead with a second-half pistol, but [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) completed a comeback from 4-10 down to win the map in overtime.

Dust2 followed a similar storyline, with [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) once again squandering four map points before closing out the map in the final round of regulation.

That meant a decider on Mirage, where [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) took matters into his own hands. A stylish AWP 4k on the B hold helped [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) stay in control early before a crucial 1v1 clutch secured double digits for the team, with the Russian rifler finishing the map on 25 kills, 104.5 ADR and a 1.58 rating.
