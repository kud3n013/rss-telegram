[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) have eliminated [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) from PGL Masters Bucharest after a one-sided victory (Nuke 13-3, Ancient 13-6) in the 1-2 pool.

The majority-Russian squad lock in their place in Wednesday's deciding 2-2 pool, where they will require a win to secure their playoff spot.

For [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele), this is a disappointing end to the tournament, but the Brazilian squad can find solace in their win against [G2](https://www.hltv.org/team/5995/g2), which saw them all but confirm their Singapore Major spot.

The series began on Nuke, where [Aleksandr "KaiR0N-" Anashkin](https://www.hltv.org/player/19920/kair0n) started like a man possessed and had a 16-0 K-D after eight rounds. While he cooled off somewhat and finished on a 1.88 rating, his teammates did more than enough to cruise to a 13-3 victory.

Ancient was slightly closer thanks to [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) winning two pistol rounds, but [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) barely broke a sweat on the way to a 13-6 victory to stay alive in Bucharest.
