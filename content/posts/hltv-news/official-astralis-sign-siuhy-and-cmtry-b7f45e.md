[Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) have announced the signing of [Kamil "siuhy" Szkaradek](https://www.hltv.org/player/16820/siuhy) and [Nikita "cmtry" Samolotov](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry), confirming previous reports by HLTV and [Sebastien "KRL" Perez](https://www.hltv.org/player/8101/krl) that indicated the pair were targeted as replacements for [Rasmus "HooXi" Nielsen](https://www.hltv.org/player/10096/hooxi) and [Jakob "jabbi" Nygaard](https://www.hltv.org/player/17956/jabbi).

The move will see [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) give up AWPing and become a rifler, leaving [Love "phzy" Smidebrant](https://www.hltv.org/player/16726/phzy) on the Big Green. The Ukrainian joins [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) after he was moved to [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut)'s bench at the start of October to make room for [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus), and will hope that a pivot away from the sniper can help turn around a [rough run of form](https://www.hltv.org/stats/players/events/22674/cmtry?startDate=2026-01-01&endDate=2026-12-31).

[siuhy](https://www.hltv.org/player/16820/siuhy) entered free agency on October 2 after a little over a year with [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid), during which the team struggled to curb a downward spiral amidst early tournament exits, a lack of cohesion, and disappointing T side showings.

He, too, will hope to get back on track in [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis), which will not feature a Danish majority for the first time. The team clinched an invite to PGL Major Singapore, according to HLTV's simulation page, after winning Stake Ranked Episode 4, which will help alleviate some pressure as it gets acquainted with its new players.

[siuhy](https://www.hltv.org/player/16820/siuhy) and [cmtry](https://www.hltv.org/player/22674/cmtry) will debut for [Astralis](https://www.hltv.org/team/6665/astralis) at Stake Pulse Beat III, where the team has been placed in Group C and will face [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi) in its opener.
