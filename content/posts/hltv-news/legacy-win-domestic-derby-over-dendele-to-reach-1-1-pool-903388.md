[Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) are through to the 1-1 pool at PGL Masters Bucharest after battling past their countrymen from [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) in a two-map series (13-9 Cache, 19-17 Mirage).

[Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) had stumbled in their opener in Bucharest, ending up on the losing side of the longest Ancient map at a Big Event (23-25) against [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) and faltering on Dust2 (8-13) to get cast down to the 0-1 pool.

Their form remained shaky in a domestic matchup against [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele), but winning all four pistols in the series and an excellent showing out of [Santino "try" Rigal](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try) ensured they would avoid an early trip to the elimination pool.

[Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) won both pistols on Cache, and made up for losing to a force-buy in the first half with a six-round run spurred on by an on-fire [try](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try). The Argentine sniper tallied eight multi-kills and a 2.28 CT rating en route to a 20-5 K-D by the break, which, combined with a 14-6 half from [Eduardo "dumau" Wolkmer](https://www.hltv.org/player/15698/dumau), left [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) with just three to their name.

[Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) wasted little time in moving up to map point at 12-4 before [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) won a few clutches to close the gap, but [Vinicius "n1ssim" Pereira](https://www.hltv.org/player/19686/n1ssim) eventually got his team over the line with a 1v3 to seal victory on his opponent's pick.

[Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) added another pistol win to their tally as [n1ssim](https://www.hltv.org/player/19686/n1ssim) and [try](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try)'s solid showing continued, but this time their early lead dwindled as a few B hits were shut down by [Danilo "doc" Barros](https://www.hltv.org/player/22911/doc) and [Daniel "rdnzao" Monteiro](https://www.hltv.org/player/21921/rdnzao). [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) found success disrupting [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy)'s defaults and went ahead 7-5 at the half, but another lost pistol meant they would have to get the job done the hard way.

Solid calling, combined with multis from [rdnzao](https://www.hltv.org/player/21921/rdnzao), saw [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) make good on that effort as they climbed up to map point, 12-9, but they couldn't get the job done in regulation as [Andrei "arT" Piovezan](https://www.hltv.org/player/12521/art) and [try](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try) found impact to force overtime.

There, [try](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try) continued to fire off with several multi-kills, but [rdnzao](https://www.hltv.org/player/21921/rdnzao) kept [DENDELE](https://www.hltv.org/team/13926/dendele) afloat with a pair of quad-kills as the teams battled into double overtime. [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) eventually converted the map after a 2v4 by [try](https://www.hltv.org/player/19869/try) and [arT](https://www.hltv.org/player/12521/art) gave them a lead heading into their CT side half, which was enough to clinch a 19-17 win.
