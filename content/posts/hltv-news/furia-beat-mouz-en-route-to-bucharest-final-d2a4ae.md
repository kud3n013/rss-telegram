[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) defeated [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) in three maps in the PGL Masters Bucharest semi-finals on Friday to advance to their second consecutive final, after they claimed the Thunderpick World Championship title in Malta less than two weeks ago.

"I'm very happy," [Danil "molodoy" Golubenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/24144/molodoy), who posted a series-high 1.24 rating, said in a post-match interview with [James "BanKs" Banks](https://www.hltv.org/player/18473/banks). "It's always hard to play against MOUZ. They have good structure and know how to play the game."

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) will contest Saturday's final with [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga), who upset [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) earlier on Friday to reach their first-ever Big Event final.

When asked about the match-up, [molodoy](https://www.hltv.org/player/24144/molodoy) said: "I will play against my friends, syph0 and khaN. I think they want to beat me. It's hard to play against them because they will be confident playing against me. We will see. Good luck to them."

[FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) jumped out to an early lead on Dust2, but it proved short-lived as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) took control once they had money in the bank, prompting [Mareks "YEKINDAR" Gaļinskis](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) to question his teammates' lack of communication.

The Latvian's comments sparked an immediate reaction as [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) woke up and took the game to their opponents, led by [molodoy](https://www.hltv.org/player/24144/molodoy) and [Yuri "yuurih" Santos](https://www.hltv.org/player/12553/yuurih).

The Kazakh AWPer followed it up with three USP headshots, setting his team up for a flawless CT pistol. That marked the start of a dominant run from the Brazilian side, who closed out the map with an impressive 7-0 run.

The onslaught continued on Mirage, where [Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) fired his team into the lead with a crisp 4K in the pistol round. The Thunderpick World Championship MVP powered his team to an 8-4 lead with an 18-6 score, with [Oldřich "PR" Nový](https://www.hltv.org/player/22279/pr) the only player on [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) who looked capable of making a difference as he racked up 14 frags, one more than his teammates combined.

The tables turned in the second half as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) took control with a tight defense and built a commanding 7-0 lead. [YEKINDAR](https://www.hltv.org/player/13915/yekindar) and [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) still tried to breathe some life into [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) when they combined for all five kills to win their team's first round of the half, but their efforts would be in vain as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) quickly put the game to bed.

A chaotic opening on Cache gave way to a dominant first half from [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), who picked up an 8-4 lead by keeping [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) from stringing wins together. Nerves crept in after the break, with both teams dropping anti-ecos, but [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) pulled through by winning back-to-back rounds.
