[GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) will be without [Timur "FL4MUS" Marev](https://www.hltv.org/player/20994/fl4mus) for their RLG Tesla Cup opening match due unexpected travel and visa issues, the organization announced on Tuesday morning.

The news comes after a tumultuous time for the team, with the Serbian event now following a string of tournaments that [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) won't be able to play with their full roster.

Previously, [Sebastian "Tauson" Tauson Lindelof](https://www.hltv.org/player/20301/tauson) missed the IEM Beijing qualifier due to health issues, [Janusz "Snax" Pogorzelski](https://www.hltv.org/player/2553/snax) missed FPG 3 due to travel issues, and [Fredrik "REZ" Sterner](https://www.hltv.org/player/9278/rez) was absent from Logitech G Play Connect due to food poisoning.

[Denis "Grashog" Hristov](https://www.hltv.org/player/18465/grashog) will step in for [FL4MUS](https://www.hltv.org/player/20994/fl4mus) for their opening match against [Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) in what will be his third match for the team this season.

[GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) will play their first match at RLG Tesla Cup with:
