VRS has been a hot-button topic ever since its introduction, and the race to the Major always gives rise to new conversations as teams fight for every edge they can.

Sometimes, that goes too far, like with [Sashi](https://www.hltv.org/team/11712/sashi)'s recent attempt to pivot to an Asian core that almost had the knock-on effect of making [BC.Game](https://www.hltv.org/team/12878/bcgame) miss out after Valve updated its rulebook to close the loophole.

But is the loophole truly closed, or are there still ways to exploit it? [Danish "Nohte" Allana](https://www.hltv.org/player/20824/nohte) and [Harry "NER0" Richards](https://www.hltv.org/player/25798/ner0) dove into that topic and more on the latest Come Here episode with VRS expert Finn ["Mischief"](https://x.com/MischiefCS2) Farrer, who has proposed numerous fixes and adjustments on Valve's VRS Github page.

The panel also discussed other issues with the VRS, including the current regional divide and over-importance of LAN wins, problems with open LANs using VRS seeding to determine invites, and answered questions for HLTV Brain from the [Come Here Discord](https://discord.com/invite/xkbnGGas8Q).

You can watch the episode on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5KHfcv5QCqsBI3KT8UXgiV) or [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rod4Ky1mRjg).
