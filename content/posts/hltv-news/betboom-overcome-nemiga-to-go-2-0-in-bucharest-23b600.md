[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) moved within one win of the PGL Masters Bucharest playoffs after beating [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) 2-0 with wins on Anubis (13-3) and Mirage (13-11), following up their opening victory over [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit).

[Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga), who had swept [Legacy](https://www.hltv.org/team/12468/legacy) in their opener, drop to the 1-1 pool. They remain in contention for a playoff berth, which would secure their place at the Singapore Major.

The series began with [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) taking the first six rounds on Anubis. [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) briefly found their footing with three consecutive rounds, but that was all they could manage. [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) restored control to lead 9-3 at the break and swept the second half, with [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) topping the scoreboard with a 1.87 rating.

Mirage proved considerably tougher. [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga) recovered from a 0-2 start to lead 7-5 at halftime, while [Nikita "robo" Dushin](https://www.hltv.org/player/22864/robo) contributed a 1v2 clutch helping them along the way, before extending their advantage to 9-5 after the switch.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) responded with three straight rounds to bring themselves back into contention. The teams traded blows until Nemiga led 11-10, but [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte)'s 28-kill showing helped his team take the final three rounds to complete the sweep.
