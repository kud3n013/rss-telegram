[HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu) have claimed the RLG Tesla Cup trophy after a 2-0 win over [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion) (Dust2 13-6, Nuke 13-9).

The win serves as a massive boon for [HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu)'s Major chances, as they now stand at 73.3% as per HLTV's invite predictor. The Russian squad is now set to travel to Moscow for GLuck Moscow Cyber Games 2026, which will be their final tournament before the invite cutoff.

[HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu)'s Tesla Cup campaign was flawless, as they qualified for the grand final with three 2-0 wins over [Luminosity](https://www.hltv.org/team/6290/luminosity), [B8](https://www.hltv.org/team/11241/b8) and [GamerLegion](https://www.hltv.org/team/9928/gamerlegion).

The latter was their opponent in the grand final as well, with [HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu) once again barely breaking a sweat in a one-sided 2-0 victory.

[Dmitry "mizu" Kondratev](https://www.hltv.org/player/21385/mizu) dominated on the Dust2 opener and racked up 24 kills, 140 ADR and a 2.40 rating for his troubles, before [David "n0rb3r7" Danielyan](https://www.hltv.org/player/16612/n0rb3r7) took over on Nuke to finish the map with a 1.69 rating.

Outside of [HOTU](https://www.hltv.org/team/11581/hotu), who massively increased their chances of making Singapore, the event also had a big impact on several other squads' chances of qualifying.

[FaZe](https://www.hltv.org/team/6667/faze) clinched qualification with a third-place finish, while [B8](https://www.hltv.org/team/11241/b8) missed a golden opportunity in the game against [Russel "Twistzz" Van Dulken](https://www.hltv.org/player/10394/twistzz)'s troops and now sit at 12.0%.

[Envy](https://www.hltv.org/team/5991/envy) and [Luminosity](https://www.hltv.org/team/6290/luminosity) also came into the event with a slim chance of making it, but both squads are out of the race after respective early exits.
