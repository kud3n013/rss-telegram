[FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) have enjoyed a winning start to life with [Álvaro "SunPayus" García](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) amongst their number, overcoming a shaky start to sweep their opening series at PGL Masters Bucharest against [TYLOO](https://www.hltv.org/team/4863/tyloo). Not only did the team grab the win, but [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus) also impressed individually, posting a scoreboard-topping 1.47 rating.

"He is really good at the AWP. Like, when we put him in some angle, we know he will get the kills," [András "coolio" Fercsák](https://www.hltv.org/coach/14202/coolio) said to HLTV when giving his assessment of his new sniper. "He has really good experience and he is able to control the mid round, and we were really lacking a guy like that."

With an opening win in Bucharest under his belt, [coolio](https://www.hltv.org/coach/14202/coolio) spoke with HLTV to give his insight into the addition of [SunPayus](https://www.hltv.org/player/19164/sunpayus), what he sees his new sniper bringing to the team, and how far [FUT](https://www.hltv.org/team/13286/fut) can go considering the change.

**So first, a win on your debut with SunPayus. What was your assessment of the series?**

Happy that we could bounce back on Nuke because it was a shaky start after we won the pistol. TYLOO has a bit of a crazy style, not like EU teams, and we were prepared but still, the people are not used to playing [against] this style. So I think that's why they could start good on Nuke T-side. But yeah, after we won the pistol we kind of felt that we are in control again, and we could close the game.

**In general, you've only had a little bit of time with SunPayus, that was your debut game. Where do you feel the team is at now in terms of level with SunPayus?**

To be honest we had enough time, we even could do a bootcamp as we had more than one month off. So since we ended BLAST Porto we were practicing with him, even though it wasn't official. We did tryouts, and we were playing with him. So I would say we have a good map pool, but we didn't test it in officials, so you never know how it is. But I feel like we had enough practice, and we were working so hard while we didn't play, so I think we are okay.

He's really easy to work with, funny guy, bringing the moods up

[András "coolio" Fercsák](https://www.hltv.org/coach/14202/coolio) on SunPayus

**Was it easy to slot SunPayus in?**

Yeah, I think it was. That's why we also did a trial with him, we wanted to understand if the personality fit or how he is. People are saying that he is old and not fit for this team, but to be honest, he is same age as us, kind of, how he acts and how he is as a guy. He's really easy to work with, funny guy, bringing the moods up, and yeah, it was easy. He also didn't want to change much things, so he just wanted to come and learn our stuff instead of doing his things, and bring the full experience. So yeah, it was easy.

**Looking forward, what are you hoping to get out of SunPayus that you maybe weren't getting out of cmtry?**

The difference is that he has really good experience and he is able to control the mid round, and we were really lacking a guy like that. That's why, first of all, we took xfl0ud to the team, but it wasn't like how we expected, so we were still missing a guy who is in the pack when Krabeni is lurking. So now we have a guy who is able to control us a little bit. It's not like he's a caller, but he's so good at understanding if he should smoke, if he should speed up, and that's what we were missing. And he is really good at the AWP. Like, when we put him in some angle, we know he will get the kills.

Sadly, in the end with cmtry, it was a bit different because he was also losing his confidence, and he was missing shots which he should have gotten, and that's why our CT side was not the best. Now with SunPayus, he's getting these kills, and if he gets an entry 5v4 it's already easier to win. To be honest, I was also shocked that they benched him in G2 because I feel like he's still so good a player.

Sadly, in the end with cmtry, it was a bit different because he was also losing his confidence

[András "coolio" Fercsák](https://www.hltv.org/coach/14202/coolio)

**How much do you think this raises the ceiling for FUT, having SunPayus in for cmtry?**

I don't want to say any crazy things, because it can jinx, and I don't want to praise us like crazy, but I think we will be a little bit more... How can I explain it? It's like, because of the experience he has, we will be more controlled. We were losing many games because people got crazy in their head, and we were over fighting, over peeking, and now it's disappeared in a way.

We still have the style of FUT, we don't want to change fully, but yeah, if we can show what we did in practice, I have high hopes. But as I said, it's first tournament, so we can easily lose. You could see TYLOO was also winning 7-1, so it's not like we are the best team in the world. But I would say we can be, for sure, a top-eight team. That's what I expect.

**Like you say, you've had a good amount of time to practice, are you looking to just hit the ground running, or do you still give yourself a little bit of time to get up to speed with SunPayus in official matches?**

We didn't put any expectation for this tournament. For me this game was the most important, I would say, because it's good to start with a win, and obviously the teams are here really strong. TYLOO I don't want to say [are] easy, but we have to take this win. And then, if you're going to lose to MOUZ or some teams that, we cannot be sad, you know?

We need to still do the things what we were practicing and see how it goes in official and then understand if it's the right way, if it's not. so, yeah, there is no pressure. We are trying to talk about this with no pressure, we just play and let's see how it goes. After we have Hong Kong and Major, so for Hong Kong it's going to be a bit different.
