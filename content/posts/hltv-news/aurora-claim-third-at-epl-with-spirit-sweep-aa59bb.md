[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) have taken third place at ESL Pro League 24 with a comfortable 2-0 beating of [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), securing their victory with wins on Mirage (13-9) and Anubis (13-6).

The match certainly meant more to [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) than [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), with the newly-minted international lineup hoping to legitimize their recent improvements and get closer to a Stage 3 berth for Singapore Major.

Those facts bore out on the server, with [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) seeming sharper in every department as [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit) fell to a lacklustre defeat, frustration evident on their player cams throughout much of the series.

> [Wicadia Dualies ace kicks off third-place decider](https://www.twitch.tv/eslcs/clip/InterestingSpookyKeyboardCoolStoryBob-bj4TgyENfJdo3wrz)

[Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) should have secured Mirage more quickly than they did, as the European combine raced out to a 6-1 lead off the back of a pistol win and a couple of very convincing gun rounds. [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), however, were able to break [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora)'s momentum with some low-buy wins and secure five rounds by the half.

Another strong start to the second half kept [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) in the driver's seat, and although [Andrey "tN1R" Tatarinovich](https://www.hltv.org/player/19808/tn1r) and [Dmitry "sh1ro" Sokolov](https://www.hltv.org/player/16920/sh1ro) offered a bit of resistance, [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) duly marched to a victory on their opponent's map pick.

> [Aurora outplay Spirit in the mid round despite insane sh1ro MP9 spray transfer](https://www.twitch.tv/eslcs/clip/UgliestSpoopyBunnyFreakinStinkin-ax4-lIHVxXcfYkI0)

Anubis was even more one-sided, the frustration apparent on [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit)'s player cams as they simply couldn't play themselves into the game. [Aurora](https://www.hltv.org/team/11861/aurora) were better in the mid rounds, got more firepower out of their individuals, and eventually sealed a comfortable 13-6 win to close the series.
