[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) advanced to the playoffs of PGL Masters Bucharest with a 3-1 record after a resounding victory over [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere), robbing [Aleksi "Aleksib" Virolainen](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib)'s men of their Inferno pick (13-2) before putting on a flawless defense on Dust2 (13-9) to complete a comeback from 3-9 down.

It was the first meeting for [Justinas "jL" Lekavicius](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) against [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) in his career and since he departed the organization in July, and he was all smiles after helping [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) secure the victory.

[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) will hope to convert upon their third chance to make the playoffs after faltering against [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) on Monday, but will need to show far better form than they did today as their calling and individual decision-making left much to be desired.

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) utterly dominated [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere)'s Inferno pick, a team-wide performance leaving [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere)'s meek offense destitute at every turn. Early multis from [Mathieu "ZywOo" Herbaut](https://www.hltv.org/player/11893/zywoo) gave way to a stellar defensive half by [William "mezii" Merriman](https://www.hltv.org/player/18462/mezii), who bagged a kill in 11 straight rounds while [Shahar "flameZ" Shushan](https://www.hltv.org/player/16693/flamez) and [jL](https://www.hltv.org/player/19206/jl) locked down Banana.

[Mihai "iM" Ivan](https://www.hltv.org/player/14759/im) had a map to forget, dying in every round of the first half, failing to tally a kill, and ending the effort with 5.1 ADR. He avoided finishing the map on a goose egg with two MP9 frags in the second half, and NAVI could do nothing but laugh as they managed to avoid a 0-13 loss.

The Romanian rifler had a return to form on Dust2, where he combined with [Drin "makazze" Shaqiri](https://www.hltv.org/player/22673/makazze) and [Ihor "w0nderful" Zhdanov](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) in a rock-solid defense. Two of [Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality)'s three rounds in the first half came by way of clutches as [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) ran away with a dominant lead at the break, but the tables turned after the swap.

[Vitality](https://www.hltv.org/team/9565/vitality) put up a brick wall on their own CT side, aggression up Catwalk and excellent positioning on B leaving [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) no avenue for entry. [Aleksib](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib)'s men got the bomb down on just two occasions, once in the pistol and again in the final round, but even the latter plant was soured as [mezii](https://www.hltv.org/player/18462/mezii) clutched a 1v2 to seal a flawless 10-round run to victory.
