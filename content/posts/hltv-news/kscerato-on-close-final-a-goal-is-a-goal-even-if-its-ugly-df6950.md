After an 11-month drought stretching back to November of last year, [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) have added another trophy to their cabinet, securing the Thunderpick World Championship title with a hard-fought victory over [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom).

The victory not only represents a breakthrough success for a [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia) side struggling amidst a packed schedule, but it also serves as another chapter in [Gabriel "FalleN" Toledo](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen)'s legacy, with the legendary Brazilian set to retire at the end of the year.

"We add another trophy to FalleN's huge HLTV [profile], right?" tournament MVP [Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) told HLTV when reflecting on his team's achievement. "I'm really happy for him. Happy that I helped him win this trophy in his retirement year. He’s a guy who deserves more than just one trophy; it's a boost to win the next ones, too, because he deserves it all."

Not only did [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato)'s MVP-worthy performance power his team to the trophy, he also strengthened his own case as one of the best riflers in the world. " I think I'm playing better than last year," he told HLTV. "I think this year I've been landing more shots, making a lot of impact and getting a lot of multi-kills."

Fresh from lifting the trophy, [KSCERATO](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) also spoke to HLTV about what the victory means for his team, how they got it done in a close series, and the impact of a strong performance by [FalleN](https://www.hltv.org/player/2023/fallen) himself.

---

**Congratulations, KSCERATO. These last few months haven't been easy, right? Results haven't been good; there has been a lot of criticism… How are you feeling?**

It’s been a really tough year for us. We lost the Krakow final; to be honest, we threw the Krakow final, and we lost the Major final. Even though we were coming off a few mixed results before the Major, we still managed to make it to the final. So, it was impressive, and we began the second season on the wrong foot, losing to DENDELE, etc.

I believe we’re still finding our footing; we’ve been playing a lot of tournaments, so it’s been a little tough for us to perform at our absolute best, but we’re trying our hardest.

So I think… It’s really fun that we won this championship. It gives us some confidence and a bit of breathing room, you know?

If it isn't a struggle, it isn't FURIA, right?

[Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) on tough grand final vs. BETBOOM

**This was a very close series with all five maps. Does that make it a bit more special?**

If it isn't a struggle, it isn't FURIA, right? There's no way around it. We could have made it a little easier, but we made some situations harder on ourselves; we lost several rounds, especially on Ancient, where we had a few rounds where we could have easily closed out the game, but we ended up messing up. But that's how it goes, right? A goal is a goal, even if it's ugly.

**You finally managed to win a trophy this year — something you’d been striving for, especially since FalleN announced that he is retiring at the end of the year. How important is this achievement for you, to add another trophy to FalleN’s legacy?**

We add another trophy to FalleN's huge HLTV [profile], right? I'm really happy for him. Happy that I helped him win this trophy in his retirement year. He’s a guy who deserves more than just one trophy; it's a boost to win the next ones, too, because he deserves it all.

**He played an important role today with a 1.08 rating and some crucial rounds, like the clutch now on Cache. Today, 'The Professor' really gave a masterclass, didn't he?**

I even tell him that I don't know why he is retiring in his prime, but it's alright. [laughs].

He plays really well. We joke around a lot, but he’s an excellent player—he communicates really well, helps a lot with macro. He is really good. He's been playing really well. I don't agree with his retiring, but I do think he deserves to retire and get some rest.

I don't agree with his retiring, but I do think he deserves to retire and get some rest

[Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) on FalleN

**Like you said, on Ancient there were a few rounds you gave away; you had a 7-1 lead and then you had two team kills and BETBOOM turned it around. On Mirage, they almost overturned an 11-2 deficit. Did you sense that the team was nervous and feeling the pressure a little?**

 I could tell the team was a little nervous, but at the same time, BETBOOM were also playing really well. I think they have a really strong core; they can pull off multikills and they can get kills through smokes, which happened in several rounds.

I think it was a great game, worthy of a final. I think you need rounds like that to really get your heart racing a little. So, I’m happy that we pulled it off. Even with those tough rounds we lost, we managed to bounce back and win the tournament.

**I saw YEKINDAR coughing a lot and wearing a cooling pad on his forehead. How is he doing? How did his illness affect his performance?**

Even though he was sick, he played really well, especially on Ancient, where he made some calls towards the end of the game that were well-executed, and we managed to win some rounds, especially in overtime. Even though he was sick, with a fever and coughing a lot… I even told him, 'Man, can you stop coughing just a little bit?'. Just to tease him a bit. He said, 'I can't.' But he played really well and communicated really well, too. I’m happy to have a guy like him on the team who, even when he’s sick, can still make an impact.

I’m happy to have a guy like him on the team who, even when he’s sick, can still make an impact

[Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato) on YEKINDAR

**You're now back-to-back Thunderpick champions. How would you compare the two trophies? Which would you say was more special?**

The first Thunderpick was special because we won the final after being 0-2 down. But I think this one holds a special place in our hearts because we were coming off a really tough year. I think this tournament is important for us as a team, so I think this one is a little bit more important.

**This year, you have one MVP and four EVPs to your name. That's one more EVP than last year. Do you think this can be your best year ever in the Top 20 ranking?**

I think I was ranked ninth because of Ronaldo. I think HLTV is a fan of Ronaldo [laughs].  I think I'm playing better than last year. I think winning those tournaments definitely helped me get into the top nine. But I think this year I've been landing more shots, making a lot of impact and getting a lot of multi-kills. And with the addition of Ancient, I've been getting a lot of kills on Ancient. Overall, I think I've been better than last year. But you need to prove it on the server and win tournaments, right?

Anyone who thinks this tournament wasn't worth anything could have come here and won it, too

[Kaike "KSCERATO" Cerato](https://www.hltv.org/player/15631/kscerato)

**yuurih told me that it would be extra special to beat Falcons in the final. There might be an asterisk attached to your title, because you didn't beat the supposedly best team here, but I guess you don't feel it takes anything away from what you achieved here, right?**

Like I said, even if it's ugly, a goal is a goal. That's it. Anyone who thinks this tournament wasn't worth anything could have come here and won it, too.

**And now, Bucharest. What are your goals?**

Bucharest starts in five or six days. So I think we’ll rest a little bit, and I hope we can perform well in this tournament. Regardless of the result, I think we have to take it one step at a time. Our first game is against DENDELE. Let's go for it.
