[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) have advanced to the playoffs of PGL Masters Bucharest after a 2-0 victory over [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) (13-10 Dust2, 13-9 Nuke).

[Kirill "Boombl4" Mikhailov](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4)'s men have carried on their fine form from Thunderpick World Championship, where they beat [Falcons](https://www.hltv.org/team/11283/falcons) and finished as the runner-up to [FURIA](https://www.hltv.org/team/8297/furia), by taking down [Spirit](https://www.hltv.org/team/7020/spirit), [Nemiga](https://www.hltv.org/team/7969/nemiga), and now [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) en route to a playoff berth.

[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) went racing out of the gates on Dust2, making up for a lost pistol with some quick tempo on the attack. [Drin "makazze" Shaqiri](https://www.hltv.org/player/22673/makazze) and [Ihor "w0nderful" Zhdanov](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) found success with entries and late-round recoveries, respectively, which put their side up 6-3 before [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) closed the gap to two rounds at the break off the back of a 1v2 by [Aleksandr "zorte" Zagodyrenko](https://www.hltv.org/player/15662/zorte).

[w0nderful](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) continued to shine for [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) in the second half, but his efforts — including a quad-kill and multis in all of NAVI's wins — was only enough for three CT rounds. Great calling and conditioning by [Boombl4](https://www.hltv.org/player/11840/boombl4), paired with offensive fragging from [Pavel "S1ren" Ogloblin](https://www.hltv.org/player/18506/s1ren), let [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) take control in the second half, and [S1ren](https://www.hltv.org/player/18506/s1ren) ensured a win wouldn't slip away with a 1v3 to reach map point that [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) immediately converted upon.

[Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) put up a masterclass CT side on Nuke after starting down 1-4, converting an eco round by pushing into Lobby and never letting control slip from there. [w0nderful](https://www.hltv.org/player/20127/w0nderful) and [Aleksi "Aleksib" Virolainen](https://www.hltv.org/player/9816/aleksib) led a flawless defensive spree to end the half up 8-4, but the wheels fell off the bus on the attack.

[BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom)'s defense was impregnable, with [Kirill "Magnojez" Rodnov](https://www.hltv.org/player/21667/magnojez) constantly disrupting NAVI's defaults and dominating them in openers. That, combined with excellence in the late-round from [S1ren](https://www.hltv.org/player/18506/s1ren), left [Natus Vincere](https://www.hltv.org/team/4608/natus-vincere) with just one round to their name as [BETBOOM](https://www.hltv.org/team/12394/betboom) romped to a 13-9 victory.
