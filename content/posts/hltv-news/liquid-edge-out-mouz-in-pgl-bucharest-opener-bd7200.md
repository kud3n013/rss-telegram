[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) have opened their PGL Masters Bucharest campaign with a 2-1 victory over [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz). After losing Ancient, they responded on Inferno with 16-12 and completed the comeback on Mirage with 13-11. The victory sends [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) into the 1-0 pool, while MOUZ join the 0-1.

[Keith "NAF" Markovic](https://www.hltv.org/player/8520/naf) provided the series' standout highlight with a 1v5 clutch to force overtime on Ancient. Although it ultimately proved insufficient to secure the overtime, [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) seized their chances on the next two maps.

[Clip link](https://www.twitch.tv/pgl_cs2/clip/BitterNurturingMoonUncleNox-JYGKy56Z93F_bofu)

[Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) started brightly on [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz)'s pick, winning the opening 4 rounds and taking a 7-5 lead. [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) turned the game around in the second half, however, stringing together 5 rounds to reach map point at 12-9. [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) saved all three, culminating in [NAF](https://www.hltv.org/player/8520/naf)'s remarkable ace clutch, but [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) regained control in overtime to close out their pick.

Inferno brought another reversal. [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid)'s 5-0 start disappeared as [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) won the next 8 rounds and forced overtime, but [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) swept the extra rounds to level the series. [Johnny "JT" Theodosiou](https://www.hltv.org/player/11630/jt) finished this map with 26 kills, including four AK-47 headshots in round 27 to put his team on map point.

The decider remained finely balanced through a 6-6 first half before [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) moved ahead 10-7. [Liquid](https://www.hltv.org/team/5973/liquid) responded with five consecutive rounds to reach series point, and although [MOUZ](https://www.hltv.org/team/4494/mouz) briefly delayed them, they finished the job in regulation. [Georgi "Jorko" Mitev](https://www.hltv.org/player/24203/jorko) led the way with 21 kills and 91.5 ADR as his team completed the reverse sweep.
