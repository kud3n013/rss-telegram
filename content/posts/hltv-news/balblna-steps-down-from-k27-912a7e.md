[K27](https://www.hltv.org/team/12895/k27) have announced that their head coach, [Gregory "balblna" Oleinick](https://www.hltv.org/player/8294/balblna), has [decided to step down from the team](https://t.me/K27_esports/3390).

The decision brings an end to the 30-year-old's 15-month-long tenure with the organization, during which he helped the team secure several smaller titles such as MPKBK CIS LAN seasons 3 and 8 and make their first tier-one appearances at PGL Astana and Esports World Cup.

"Thank you for everything, Griga! Thank you for believing in these young guys a year ago and taking the helm of the team," the organization's CEO, Ivan Kondratov, said in a statement.

"We started at the very bottom of the rankings, and in just one year, we've reached such great heights! I am confident that the success achieved with K27 will propel Grigory toward new milestones with a new team — one he will undoubtedly find in no time!"

The organization further announced that [Dmitry "Twizell" Rygin](https://www.hltv.org/player/23478/twizell), who has served as [K27](https://www.hltv.org/team/12895/k27)'s analyst for the better part of two years, will take over as interim coach in their upcoming tournaments.

The team is currently fighting an uphill battle for a chance to qualify for PGL Singapore with [WINLINE MPKBK CIS LAN Season 9](https://www.hltv.org/events/9426/winline-mpkbk-cis-lan-season-9) and the [RLG Tesla Cup](https://www.hltv.org/events/9434/rlg-tesla-cup-2026) ahead of them before the early November invite deadline hits.
