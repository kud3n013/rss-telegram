He then attempted to connect the rather commonsense criticisms of Omar with the Left’s bête noir Donald Trump, saying, “One of the most dangerous things Trump has done is to divide Americans by using false allegations of antisemitism, mostly regarding the U.S.-Israel relationship.”

To bring Israel into the debate is, of course, a favorite judo throw employed by progressives.
