**President Donald Trump** is assembling quite the roster of sports legends for his midterm campaign rallies.

**Roger Clemens**. **Mike Tyson**. **Floyd Mayweather** **Jr**.

Three all-time greats have joined the president onstage in the past eight days, with the undefeated boxing legend becoming the latest Wednesday night in **Arizona**.

And that’s not even the entire roster.

The 50-0 Mayweather joined Trump at his rally in **Bullhead City**, Ariz. The former boxing champion praised Trump and reflected on representing the **United States** at the **1996 Olympics**.

“We couldn’t have chose a better president twice than Donald Trump,” Mayweather told the crowd.

The appearance was the latest in a growing trend of Trump bringing some of the biggest personalities in sports to his political events.

And he certainly isn’t slowing down.

## **Trump’s star-studded lineup**

Last Wednesday, seven-time **Cy Young** Award winner **Roger Clemens** joined Trump at his rally in **San Antonio**. But Trump had another surprise for the Rocket.

The president announced he would award Clemens the **Presidential Medal of Freedom**, the nation’s highest civilian honor, while also renewing his support for the pitcher’s Baseball Hall of Fame candidacy.
