**Oregon State** was one of two programs left behind when the **Pac-12** collapsed.

Three years later, the **Beavers** might be playing their way into the **College Football Playoff**.

Oregon State is 4-2, has won four straight games, and just scored 122 points over its past two outings. Behind first-year head coach **JaMarcus Shephard**, the Beavers have built one of the highest-scoring offenses in college football around a freshman receiver leading the country and a former **FCS** quarterback doing the same.

If you haven’t been paying attention to what’s happening in **Corvallis**, now might be the time.

### **From Pac-12 limbo to one of the nation’s best offenses**

After the 2023 season, 10 Pac-12 programs departed for other conferences, leaving only Oregon State and **Washington State** behind.

The Beavers went 5-7 in 2024 before bottoming out at 2-10 last season.

Then came Shephard.

Oregon State hired Shephard after he spent two seasons at **Alabama**, most recently as assistant head coach, co-offensive coordinator and wide receivers coach. Before following **Kalen DeBoer** to Tuscaloosa, Shephard spent two seasons on his staff at **Washington**.

His background developing receivers and high-powered offenses has translated immediately.

After opening the season with losses to **Houston** and **Texas Tech**, Oregon State has won four straight. The Beavers beat **Montana** 52-17, **UTEP** 33-7, **Colorado State** 56-26 and **San Diego State** 66-14.

That’s four consecutive wins by at least 26 points. Oregon State is now averaging 41.8 points per game, the 11th-best mark in the country.

And two players are driving it.

### **College football’s best duo may reside in the Group of Six**

Freshman receiver **Jesse Legree** and quarterback **Braden Atkinson** have become one of the most productive combinations in the sport.

Legree currently leads the country in both receiving yards and receiving touchdowns. Yes, even more than **Heisman**-favorite **Jeremiah Smith**.

An FCS transfer and a freshman receiver are now powering one of the most explosive offenses in college football. And they may have something bigger to play for.

### **The playoff path is real**

This is where Oregon State gets really interesting.

Under the new **College Football Playoff** format for 2026, the highest-ranked team from the Group of Six conferences receives an automatic bid into the 12-team field.

Right now, **Boise State** is the only Group of Six team ranked in the **AP Top 25**, sitting at No. 23. Oregon State received just one AP Poll vote this week.

That can change quickly.

The Beavers and Broncos are both undefeated in Pac-12 play and will meet in **Boise** on Nov. 14 in what could become one of the most important Group of Six games of the season.

Oregon State still has **Washington State**, **Fresno State**, **Texas State**, Boise State and **Utah State** remaining before its flex matchup to close the regular season.

The Beavers have already shown they can hang around with **Power Four** competition, losing to Houston by 13 and Texas Tech by 11 before rattling off four dominant victories. Keep winning, and the playoff conversation will take care of itself.

Oregon State went from being one of the programs left behind by conference realignment to having a legitimate path into the 12-team playoff. With Atkinson and Legree putting up video-game numbers, that possibility doesn’t look quite as crazy anymore.
