Dust off the denim, throw on a graphic t-shirt, and break out your brightest colors. Perhaps even ask Dad for a vintage teal Starter jacket while you’re at it if you don’t mind sweating in the name of throwback fashion.

That’s the message from **Coastal Carolina** football as it dropped its fluorescent, 1990s-themed jerseys ahead of Friday night’s nationally-televised home matchup against **Appalachian State**at **Brooks Stadium**.

The night time affair is also part of the football team’s annual earmarked contest dubbed ‘**Wait Til You See This**,’ which in 2025 featured the Chanticleers sporting [glow-in-the-dark](https://www.si.com/college-football/costal-carolina-glow-in-the-dark-uniforms-mascot-fights) uniforms around Halloween.

“If you look at [the conversation] online right now…[some people hate the jerseys](https://x.com/CSOonX/status/2109978822284280286), [some](https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2109822989441048893) [people love it](https://twitter.com/BarstoolBigCat/status/2109822989441048893), but [they’re all talking](https://x.com/IowanStallion/status/2109809329117315428) about our university, our football team, and our uniforms,” said **[Chance Miller](https://x.com/cmiller05), Coastal Carolina Athletic Director**, during a phone interview with **Sport & State**. “You don’t have to be just a football fan to be engaged with it.”

Miller, a former practicing attorney in New York, explained that the creative initiative stemmed from conversations led by **Chief Creative Officer [Jake Johnson](https://x.com/StateFarm_Jake6)** and **[Patrick Osborne](https://x.com/PatrickONealOK), Executive Associate Athletic Director for External Communications,** about how the program could engage the campus community in a fun and unique way, similar to last year’s themed game.

Given the ’90s comeback in pop culture, the group landed on a decade-inspired concept, with creative and colorful influence from classic hit TV sitcoms like **Saved By The Bell, Friends,** and **The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air**, among others.

This year’s theme, which centers around the throwback threads, will also include in-stadium videos boards with a VHS-style look and feel, in addition to ’90s music played throughout the stadium. Ultimately, it will provide broader brand exposure for not only the football team but also the university.

Maybe, just maybe, the deeper sense of nostalgia, for at least some of the **Chanticleer** fans who remember the good ol’ decade, will translate into more of a home-field advantage for the 2-4 program as it squares off against its biggest rival.

“There’s so many people that are relocating from other places in the Northeast and the Midwest that might not have had that traditional college football fandom,” Miller said. “And we’re trying to get them to come to the games, buy tickets, and really adopt us. This is one of those ways that we can do it.”

The senior administrator and his staff already already have their sights set on 2027 as the Chanticleers once again try to make a creative splash — even it may result in the Internet comparing the future idea to [a popular fast food chain](https://x.com/GlassOfBennett/status/2109810982734495928) or [an iconic disposable cup design](https://x.com/FBallAnalysisYT/status/2109836796360004005).

“We’ve thrown a couple of ideas around, one of them is pretty unique, but we’re still trying to figure out how to pull it off,” Miller said.

In other words, stay tuned.

### Deadhead influence

**Cal football** joined Coastal Carolina with some nostalgia-themed uniform modifications of their own for the upcoming weekend.

This time, the **Golden Bears** dialed it all the way back to the ’60 and ’70s with a **Grateful Dead-inspired helmet**, which will be worn at home against **Wake Forest** on Saturday night for the first and only time ever.
