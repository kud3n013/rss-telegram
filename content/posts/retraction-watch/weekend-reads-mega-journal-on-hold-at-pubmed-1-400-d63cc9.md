If your week flew by — we know ours did — catch up here with what you might have missed.

The week at Retraction Watch featured:

- Another Sydney investigation of astrophysicist [leads to retractions](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/10/26/joachim-schmidt-university-sydney-investigation-space-scientist-retraction/)
- Game theory society [yanks support for journal](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/10/27/game-theory-society-yanks-support-for-journal-after-elsevier-fails-to-renew-eic-contract/) after Elsevier fails to renew EIC contract
- Federal U.S. judge [tosses $1 billion defamation lawsuit](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/10/28/global-plasma-solutions-gps-elsevier-lawsuit-defamation-ruling/) against Elsevier
- Springer Nature journal [could lose registry standing](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/10/29/innovative-higher-education-springer-nature-norway-registry-duplication/) for clearing author of duplication
- Scientific Reports on hold in PubMed [while NLM reevaluates journal](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/10/30/scientific-reports-pubmed-on-hold-national-library-medicine-evaluation/)

In case you missed [the news](https://retractionwatch.com/2025/12/26/retraction-watch-hijacked-journal-checker-now-has-400-entries/), the [Hijacked Journal Checker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ak985WGOgGbJRJbZFanoktAN_UFeExpE/edit?gid=5255084#gid=5255084) now has more than 450 entries. The [Retraction Watch Database](https://gitlab.com/crossref/retraction-watch-data) has over 68,000 retractions. Our [list of COVID-19 retractions](https://retractionwatch.com/retracted-coronavirus-covid-19-papers/) is approaching 800, and our [mass resignations list](https://retractionwatch.com/the-retraction-watch-mass-resignations-list/) has more than 50 entries. We keep tabs on all this and more. If you value this work, please consider showing your support with a tax-deductible donation. Every dollar counts.
