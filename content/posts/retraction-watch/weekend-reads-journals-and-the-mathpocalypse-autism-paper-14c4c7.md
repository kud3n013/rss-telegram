If your week flew by — we know ours did — catch up here with what you might have missed.

The week at Retraction Watch featured:

- Japanese institute finds materials researcher with 41 retractions guilty of ‘[extensive’ manipulation](https://retractionwatch.com/?p=136270)
- Journal founded by plastic surgeon touts studies of products he’s tied to – [including one that has been retracted](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/10/21/journal-founded-by-plastic-surgeon-touts-studies-of-products-hes-tied-to-including-one-that-has-been-retracted/)
- Thousands of research studies violate journal policies on collaborations [between editors and authors](https://retractionwatch.com/?p=136295)
- Journal updates retraction notice as Princeton student [author stays in the spotlight](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/10/23/correction-retraction-rishika-porandla-princeton-macromolecular-materials-engineering-wiley/)

In case you missed [the news](https://retractionwatch.com/2025/12/26/retraction-watch-hijacked-journal-checker-now-has-400-entries/), the [Hijacked Journal Checker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ak985WGOgGbJRJbZFanoktAN_UFeExpE/edit?gid=5255084#gid=5255084) now has more than 450 entries. The [Retraction Watch Database](https://gitlab.com/crossref/retraction-watch-data) has over 67,000 retractions. Our [list of COVID-19 retractions](https://retractionwatch.com/retracted-coronavirus-covid-19-papers/) is approaching 800, and our [mass resignations list](https://retractionwatch.com/the-retraction-watch-mass-resignations-list/) has more than 50 entries. - “Highly cited autism paper earns expression of concern [over data duplications](https://www.thetransmitter.org/spectrum/highly-cited-autism-paper-earns-expression-of-concern-over-data-duplications/).”
- Cell Press investigating editor-in-chief accused of [soliciting bribes from authors](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3371384/cell-publisher-investigates-editor-accused-soliciting-bribes-china).
- “University probe leads to retraction of [four neural tube studies](https://www.thetransmitter.org/academia/university-probe-leads-to-retraction-of-four-neural-tube-studies/).”

- Bulgarian Academic Ethics Committee [report](https://www.mon.bg/nfs/2026/10/doklad_kae_signal94-1314_07102026.pdf) found “over 95% plagiarism” [in professor’s paper](https://www.dnevnik.bg/obrazovanie/2026/10/13/4964421_komisiia_otkri_nad_95_plagiatstvo_pri_profesor_v_dve/).
- Former editor-in-chief says journals “should cap each author’s submissions – [but with a twist](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/opinion/journals-should-cap-each-authors-submissions-twist).”
- Journal [issues correction](https://academic.oup.com/europace/article/28/10/euag287/8931508) for article on heart device with incorrect clinical trial registration number [following our coverage](https://retractionwatch.com/2026/09/30/journal-investigating-paper-on-heart-device-with-registration-number-for-wrong-trial/).

- “Will a M-index to measure academic mentorship [create more problems than it solves](https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/impactofsocialsciences/2026/10/22/will-a-m-index-to-measure-academic-mentorship-create-more-problems-than-it-solves/)?”
- “Post-publication review as a [digital colloquium](https://reproducibilitea.org/jc/2026/10/13/post-publication-review-as-a-digital-colloquium).”
